# Investigation: Quranic Cognition / Research / Inquiry

> Multi-phase, multi-agent investigation. Started from the question "how does the Quran represent research?" Discovered the Quran has no single word for "research" — instead a 14-root cognitive constellation. Mapped the constellation, then traced four cross-cutting structural patterns.

## Topic

How does the Quran represent **inquiry, knowledge-seeking, cognition, research**? The starting question was about "research" specifically; the answer turned out to be that the Quran refuses the modern Western category and instead deploys a constellation of 14 distinct cognitive roots, each naming a different cognitive operation.

## How to read these files

Three layers, in order of granularity:

1. **Start here:** [`concept-research-deep.md`](concept-research-deep.md) — the integrated synthesis. ~5500 words. Tells the full story.
2. **For depth on any one root:** the 15 `concept-<root>.md` files — each is a ~700-1000 word agent deep-read. Read these to verify or extend any synthesis claim.
3. **For the structural data underlying the synthesis:** the 5 `research-<topic>.md` files — cross-cutting investigations and raw aggregate data.

- [`research-cross-cutting-data.md`](research-cross-cutting-data.md) — raw aggregate data: inter-root co-occurrence matrix (567 root pairs), imperative-vs-indicative analysis, Meccan/Medinan distribution per root, surah cognitive density rankings.

- [`research-institutional-gap.md`](research-institutional-gap.md) — **Medieval Islam built scholar-classes on Quranic vocabulary the Quran does not authorize.** Audit of ~25 institutional terms (mufti, qadi, faqih, mujtahid, mufassir, ijtihad, ijmāʿ, qiyās, madrasa, etc.) — none appear in the Quran in their medieval-technical sense.
- [`research-god-human-mirror.md`](research-god-human-mirror.md) — **Same root used for divine + human cognition with morphological asymmetry.** Confirmed in 8 of 10 tested roots. Counter-evidence (xlq, rzq) reveals the boundary: cognition can be participated; existence cannot.
- [`research-negative-space.md`](research-negative-space.md) — **What the Quran does NOT have.** No positive vocabulary for doubt-method, hypothesis-as-tool, individual originality, formal logic, abstract definition, experimentation. AND it equally rejects ancestral imitation. The Quranic epistemic ideal is a third thing.

## Headline findings (one-line each)

1. The modern Arabic word for "research" (بحث) appears exactly once — a crow scratching earth (5:31).
2. The Quran has no single word for "research" — instead 14 distinct cognitive roots.
3. Real cognition happens in the **heart** (qalb), not the head — embodied, conditional, morally indexed.
4. **8 of 10 tested roots** show a God-human cognitive mirror — humans do scaled-down reflexive forms of what God does causatively.
5. Medieval Islamic scholar-classes (ʿulamāʾ, fuqahāʾ, mufti, etc.) inhabit Quranic vocabulary that did not originally describe them.
6. The Quran's epistemology is **neither** skeptical-individualist **nor** tradition-conformist — a third thing: attentive communal reception of signs.

## Method snapshot

This was the first investigation to use the **parallel-agents methodology**: 13 agents simultaneously deep-read individual roots, then 4 more agents pursued cross-cutting patterns identified by the synthesis of those reports. Documented in methodology log Entry 3, where it produced findings (heart-locus, institutional gap, God-human mirror, negative space) that single-pass analysis could not have surfaced — they only appeared as cross-confirmations across independent investigations.
