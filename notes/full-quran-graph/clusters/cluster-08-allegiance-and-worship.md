# Cluster 8 — Allegiance & the Object of Worship

> **Size:** 64 roots | **Source:** Parallel-agent analysis, 2026-04-26
> **Top members:** اله · قوم · هدي · عبد · ولي · دعو · ضلل · شرك · سلم · جنب · نصر
> **Full data:** `data/full-quran-graph/clusters.json` key "8"

## Cluster name
**Allegiance & the Object of Worship**

## Semantic territory

This cluster is the Quran's vocabulary of **theological allegiance** — the relational grammar of who you call upon, obey, ally with, lean on, and ultimately worship, versus who you take "besides Allah" (دون). Its gravitational center is اله (2,851), and almost every other root in the cluster is grammatically and rhetorically downstream of that center: عبد (worship), دعو (invocation/calling), طوع (obedience), وكل (reliance), ولي (patron/ally), نصر (helper), كفي (sufficiency), عصم (refuge), لجا (sanctuary), ندو (calling out), هدي vs ضلل (guidance vs misguidance), شرك vs حنف (associating partners vs pure monotheism), and ملل/وثن (creed/idol). The cluster captures the *relational verbs of devotion* — every relationship word that can take Allah (or a false rival) as its object. The "false rival" pole is sharply marked by دون ("besides"), شرك, زعم (false claim), حلف (oath-swearing), زور (falsehood), ميل (inclining away), ركن (leaning toward wrongdoers), and نكث (oath-breaking).

## Sub-themes

1. **The divine name and its rivals:** اله, ربب-adjacent usage, دون ("besides"), وثن (idols), ملل (creed/community), شرك (association), حنف (pure-monotheist), ندو/جنب (calling on / shunning).
2. **Worship and obedience:** عبد, طوع, سلم (submission/Muslim), نوب (turning back), هدي, صرط (the path), جبي (being chosen), ميل/ركن (deviation).
3. **Reliance, refuge, rescue:** وكل (entrusting), كفي (sufficient), عصم (preserving), لجا (refuge), نصر (help), نقذ (rescue), فاي (band/troop), صرخ (cry for help), جوب (response to دعو).
4. **Calling and answering:** دعو, ندو, جوب, صرخ, سمر (night-talk), صدع (proclaim) — the speech-acts of invocation and proclamation.
5. **Guidance vs ruin:** هدي, صرط, طرق, جبي vs ضلل, بور (perish), تبب (loss), ثبر (destruction), شقو (wretchedness), قرح (wounding), عنت (hardship), طرد (driving away), لحد (deviating from God's names).
6. **False speech and broken bonds:** زعم, زور, حلف, نكث, سفه (foolishness), استحوذ (overpowering by Satan), همن (guardian-over).

## Top 7 structurally important roots

1. **اله** — the theological gravity well; co-occurs with nearly every other member.
2. **عبد** — the canonical verb of devotion; defines the relationship اله sits on the receiving end of.
3. **دعو** — invocation; the operational verb that splits true vs false objects of worship (cf. دعو + دون).
4. **ولي** — patronage/allegiance; structures the human-divine and human-human loyalty axis.
5. **هدي** — guidance, paired antithetically with ضلل to form the binary outcome of allegiance.
6. **شرك** — the negative pole that gives the cluster its polemical edge; defines what monotheism *isn't*.
7. **دون** — the syntactic operator ("besides Allah") that organizes the entire false-worship subnetwork.

## Surprises

- **حرث (cultivation)** sits in this cluster — likely via Q 2:223 and Q 42:20 (harvest of this world vs the next), where tilth is metaphorized as the chosen object of striving. A devotional metaphor, not an agricultural one.
- **سمر (Sāmirī / night-talkers)** appears here — pulled in by the Sāmirī's idol-making episode and Q 23:67's "talking nonsense at night" against revelation; both are *false-allegiance* events.
- **قنطار (heaped wealth)** is in a theological-allegiance cluster, presumably because its Quranic occurrences (Q 3:14, 3:75, 4:20) discuss wealth as a rival object of trust/devotion.
- **شفا (brink/edge)** clusters here via Q 3:103 ("on the edge of a pit of fire — He saved you"), a rescue/refuge frame.
- **قوم** is high-ranked (660) but functions doubly — both "people/nation" (the addressees of prophetic call) and "to stand/establish" (as in اقام الصلاة); its centrality reflects how peoples are defined by *whom they worship*.
- **ليس** (the negative copula) being a top-frequency cluster member is striking — almost certainly because the cluster is saturated with negations like "there is no god but…" and "you have besides Him no…".
- **أَمَد (Amd, term/duration)** is unexpected; likely linked to Q 57:16's "the term grew long" rebuke of hardened hearts.

## Suggested research directions

1. **Map the دون-syntax network:** extract every "X دون Y" construction; quantify which roots fill the X slot (دعو, عبد, ولي, اتخذ) and which fill Y. This reveals the Quran's grammar of false worship.
2. **Antonym-pair analysis (هدي vs ضلل, حنف vs شرك, نوب vs نكث):** test whether these pairs co-occur within the *same ayah* more than chance, indicating deliberate rhetorical pairing vs distributional opposition.
3. **The "rescue lexicon" (نصر, نقذ, كفي, عصم, لجا, جوب, صرخ):** cluster these by surah-period (Meccan vs Medinan) to see whether deliverance vocabulary shifts from existential/eschatological in Mecca to martial in Medina.
4. **Verbal vs nominal اله:** separate occurrences referring to ٱللَّه (proper name) from إِلَٰه (common noun, often in the polemic "no ilah but Allah"); map which cluster-members co-occur with which form.

## One-sentence summary

Cluster 8 is the Quran's full relational vocabulary of *devotional allegiance* — every verb, noun, and operator the text uses to ask "whom do you call, obey, trust, ally with, and worship — and whom have you taken besides Him?"
