# Cluster 17 — Bodily Purity, Prayer, and Orientation of the Person

> **Size:** 33 roots | **Source:** Parallel-agent analysis, 2026-04-26
> **Top members:** يدي · وجد · صلو · احد · وجه · موه · زكو · طيب
> **Full data:** `data/full-quran-graph/clusters.json` key "17"

## Cluster name
**Bodily Purity, Prayer, and Orientation of the Person**

## Semantic territory

This cluster occupies the space where ritual law meets the body. Its gravitational center is the wudu/tayammum complex (Q 4:43 and Q 5:6): six rare roots co-occur there almost uniquely — لمس (touching women), غائط (relieving oneself), غسل (washing), مسح (wiping), يمم (intending → tayammum), and صعيد (clean earth from صعد). Around this ritual core orbits صلو (prayer, the act being prepared for), جنب (state of major impurity / "being beside"), زكو and طيب (purification and the wholesome), and the bodily/postural roots يدي (hand), وجه (face), قدم (foot), جنب (side/flank), all of which are precisely the body parts named in the wudu prescription. Three contextual exemptions for tayammum — مرض (illness), سفر (travel), and حرج (constraint/discomfort) — are all here too. A second band covers the existential posture of the person before God: وجد (finding/being-found), أحد (one/any), موه (water as ritual medium), and physical/moral states like كسل (laziness in prayer), مهن (lowly), نضر (radiant faces), كبب (cast down on faces).

## Sub-themes

1. **The wudu/tayammum apparatus** — لمس, غسل, مسح, يمم, غائط, صعيد, موه, جنب: an unusually tight ritual-law sub-network that cluster algorithms surface because these roots barely appear outside two or three legal verses.
2. **Purification taxonomy** — زكو (zakah, growth-purity), طيب (the pure-good), خبث (the foul), رجس (defilement), غسلين (the pus of Hell as anti-purity, gsl). The cluster encodes both the means and the polarity of clean/unclean.
3. **Body parts as ritual loci** — يدي, وجه, قدم, جنب: not just anatomy but the parts that are washed, prostrated, turned, lain on. وجه doubles as "face of God / direction one turns" (qiblah orientation).
4. **States and exemptions of the worshipper** — مرض, سفر, حرج, كسل, جنب (junub): the law's enumeration of who is excused, delayed, or in a transitional state.
5. **Eschatological inversion of the body** — نضر (faces radiant in 75:22, 83:24), كبب (cast on faces in Hell, 27:90), مهن (lowly torment), نكل (exemplary punishment), غسلين, شهب (flame). The same body that is washed and turned in prayer is the body raised, brightened, or hurled in the Hereafter.

## Top 7 structurally important roots

1. **صلو (Slw)** — prayer; the ritual whose preconditions organize the entire cluster.
2. **يدي (ydy)** — hand; high-frequency anchor that bridges ritual washing, agency ("what your hands have sent forth", قدم+يدي), and divine power.
3. **وجه (wjh)** — face; ritual washing + qiblah direction + "face of God" + radiant/blackened faces of resurrection.
4. **يمم (ymm)** — tayammum; low frequency but topologically irreplaceable — without it the dry-ablution sub-network collapses.
5. **جنب (jnb)** — junub/side/flank; the cluster's pivot between body, state of impurity, and "lying on one's side" (worship and rest).
6. **طيب (Tyb)** — wholesome/pure; the positive pole of the purity axis, linking food law, speech, and ritual cleanness.
7. **زكو (zkw)** — purification/zakah; the moral-economic counterpart to physical washing, and the bridge from this cluster to charity-and-growth vocabulary elsewhere.

## Surprises

- **يمم (tayammum) survives as its own root** even though it has only 11 occurrences — the algorithm correctly identifies that 3 of the 11 are uniquely co-located with the entire wudu vocabulary, giving it enormous local weight.
- **وجد (107 occurrences) sits in this cluster rather than in a cognition or existence cluster.** Its inclusion suggests that "finding" is statistically tethered to states of the embodied subject ("if you find no water…", "they found their fathers…", "you will not find…") rather than to abstract being.
- **شهب (flame/meteor) and عصر (squeezing/whirlwind)** look like outliers but join via shared verses about elemental purification and judgment (rain that purifies, fire that punishes).
- **رفق (rfq) — gentleness/elbow** shows up because of "marāfiq" (elbows) in 5:6, not its other meanings; a single anatomical word pulls a whole semantic root into a ritual cluster.
- **ميز (myz) — separation** likely enters through the eschatological "be set apart, O criminals" (36:59), echoing the cluster's purity polarity at Judgment.

## Suggested research directions

1. **Map the cluster's dependency on Q 4:43 and Q 5:6.** Quantify how many edges in this Louvain community would vanish if those two ayahs were removed; this would show whether it is a genuine semantic field or an artifact of two legal verses with extreme vocabulary density.
2. **Body-as-ritual-instrument lexicon.** Cross-reference يدي / وجه / قدم / جنب co-occurrences to test whether the Quran consistently treats these four parts as a closed set for ritual action, prayer posture, and resurrection imagery.
3. **The purity polarity (طيب/زكو ↔ خبث/رجس/غسلين).** Build a signed-edge subgraph and check whether the same agents/objects move across the polarity (e.g., is wealth marked طيب in some ayahs and خبث in others?).
4. **State-exemption logic.** Trace مرض + سفر + حرج across the corpus to see whether these three "exempting conditions" cluster only here or also govern fasting, jihad, and travel-prayer rules — testing if Quranic law has a unified "diminished-capacity" template.

## One-sentence summary

Cluster #17 is the Quran's *wudu cluster* — the body, its washings and wipings, the states that excuse them, the prayer they prepare for, and the moral polarity of purity that radiates from those two ritual verses outward into eschatology and ethics.
