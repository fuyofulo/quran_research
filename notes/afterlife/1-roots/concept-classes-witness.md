# Eschatological Classes + Witness Vocabulary
## $hd (شهد) · ymn (يمن) · sbq (سبق) · qrb (قرب)

---

## 1. Headline Finding

Four roots triangulate a single eschatological architecture: the cosmos divides into three ranked classes at death (56:7-14), and the ranking criterion is witnessed twice over — once by prophets testifying for or against their nations, once by the person's own body testifying against them. The witness-system ($hd, 160 occ) and the class-system (ymn, sbq, qrb) are structurally linked: the muqarrabūn who sit at the apex of the class hierarchy are themselves the *witnesses* of the abrār's record (83:21). Witness and proximity are the same function at the eschatological summit.

| ma$ohuwd | مَشْهُود | 3 | N | the witnessed / the event-witnessed |
| $aha`da`t | شَهَٰدَٰت | 3 | N | multiple testimonies |
| {sota$ohidu | ٱسْتَشْهِدُ | 2 | V | to seek a witness |
| m~a$ohad | مَّشْهَد | 1 | N | scene of witnessing |

The dominant lemma is *shahīd* (56×) — the person who witnesses. The root distributes across three distinct semantic layers.

**Layer 1: Self-witness — the body testifying.** The most eschatologically distinctive use. At [24:24], tongues, hands, and feet testify against the person. At [36:65], mouths are sealed and hands speak, feet testify. The body becomes a testimony-machine against its own possessor. The logic escalates in 41:19-23 (detailed in §3).

**Layer 2: Prophets as witnesses.** [4:41] — each prophet testifies for or against his *umma*. [16:89] — Muhammad is brought as witness *over* these, a witness-of-witnesses, alongside the Quran as *tibyānan li-kulli shayʾ* (clarification of everything).

**Layer 3: God as master-witness.** *Wa-kafā bi-llāhi shahīdan* (God suffices as witness) recurs at [4:79] and [4:166]. Forensic formula: closes a dispute by invoking the supreme observer. [4:166] adds the angels also testify, but God's witness is declared *sufficient* (*kafā*). The hierarchy: God > angels > prophets > body-parts — four tiers, descending rank.

**The architecture:** $hd builds a witness-infrastructure around the human person at all four levels simultaneously. Nothing is unobserved. The eschatological courtroom was always already operating.

## 3. The Skin/Limbs Testimony: 41:19-23

This is the Quran's most distinctive move in the witness-domain.

> **[41:20-22]**
> حَتَّىٰٓ إِذَا مَا جَآءُوهَا شَهِدَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ سَمۡعُهُمۡ وَأَبۡصَٰرُهُمۡ وَجُلُودُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَعۡمَلُونَ • وَقَالُواْ لِجُلُودِهِمۡ لِمَ شَهِدتُّمۡ عَلَيۡنَاۖ قَالُوٓاْ أَنطَقَنَا ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنطَقَ كُلَّ شَيۡءࣲ • وَمَا كُنتُمۡ تَسۡتَتِرُونَ أَن يَشۡهَدَ عَلَيۡكُمۡ سَمۡعُكُمۡ وَلَآ أَبۡصَٰرُكُمۡ وَلَا جُلُودُكُمۡ وَلَٰكِن ظَنَنتُمۡ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَعۡلَمُ كَثِيرًا مِّمَّا تَعۡمَلُونَ
>
> *"Until, when they reach it, their hearing, their eyes, and their skins testify against them of what they used to do. And they say to their skins: 'Why did you testify against us?' They say: 'We were made to speak by Allah, Who makes everything speak.' ... You did not hide yourselves from your ears, eyes, and skin — rather, you assumed that Allah did not know much of what you used to do."* (Saheeh + Khattab composite)

Four moves in this passage:

1. **The skins (*julūd*) are foregrounded.** The word for skin/hide (*jild*, pl. *julūd*) appears three times in 41:20-22 — more than hearing and sight. The skin is the outermost surface, the most intimate-to-deed layer. It witnessed every transgression by contact.

2. **The skins' defense-claim.** *Anṭaqanā llāhu alladhī anṭaqa kulla shayʾ* — "the One who makes everything speak made us speak." The skin invokes universal divine speech-power: its testimony is not voluntary; it is compelled by the same force that makes all creation articulate. This is not betrayal — it is *nature fulfilling its design*.

3. **The wrong assumption exposed.** The text diagnoses the root theological error: *ẓanantum anna llāha lā yaʿlamu kathīran mimmā taʿmalūn* — "you assumed God doesn't know much of what you do." The disbelievers' practical atheism was not philosophical but operational: they acted *as if* unobserved. The witness-system refutes this assumption with their own bodies.

4. **[41:23] closes the loop:** the wrong assumption (*ẓannukum*) destroyed them, made them losers (*khāsirīn*). The eschatological disaster is traced to an epistemological failure — a false belief about divine observation. The body-witness is the corrective demonstration.

The structural signature: the Quran does not describe recording-angels writing a ledger (though that exists elsewhere). Here the body *itself* is the ledger. The evidence is organic, not administrative. The person cannot disown their body's record: the skins are not external accusers but the person's own flesh.

## 4. al-Shuhadāʾ — Testifier vs Martyr

**$ahiyd* (56×) carries both senses simultaneously in much of its distribution.

**As testifiers:** The shuhadāʾ at [4:69] are listed as one of four favored eschatological classes (nabiyyīn → ṣiddīqīn → shuhadāʾ → ṣāliḥīn). In this frame, the shuhadāʾ are those who *bear witness* — they verify truth with their existence, as prophets verify with revelation and ṣiddīqūn verify with total affirmation.

**As martyrs:** [3:169] — those killed in God's path are not dead but alive with their Lord. [57:19] — those who believe and die in God's path are the *ṣiddīqūn wa-l-shuhadāʾ*. [47:4] names their status. The martyr-sense is etymologically *derivative*: the martyr is one who witnesses unto death, whose life-expenditure is the testimony itself.

**The integration:** In [4:69], the shuhadāʾ sit between ṣiddīqūn and ṣāliḥūn — those whose verification is embodied, enacted, ultimate. They are ṣiddīqūn who went all the way. The martyr-sense is the intensification of the testifier-sense: the skin testifies in 41:20 because it bore witness; the martyr testifies because they bore witness with their whole self.

## 5. ymn — aṣḥāb al-yamīn (Right-Hand Companions)

**Lemma profile:** *yamīn* (يَمِين) dominates with 65 of the root's 71 occurrences, all as nouns. The root also produces *maymana* (يَمَنة, blessedness/right-side, 2 occ — 56:8, 90:18).

**The ymn polysemy:** yamīn = (a) right hand/side, (b) oath/pledge, (c) blessing/felicity. All three senses overlap in the eschatological frame: receiving one's book in the right hand [69:19-21], being sorted to the right side, the felicity implied by position.

**Eschatological uses:** [56:8] — rhetorical exclamation (what are the right-hand companions?). [56:27, 38] — reward: lote trees, banana trees, shade, water, fruit, raised beds — sensory paradise. [56:39-40] — multitudes from both earlier and later generations (symmetric, unlike the foremost). [56:90-91] — at death: *salāmun laka min aṣḥābi al-yamīn* (peace to you from the right-hand companions). [74:39] — excluded from the damned. [90:17-18] — defined by faith + mutual exhortation to patience + mutual exhortation to mercy: orientation-traits, no capability-traits.

**The right-hand class is the saved majority.** Reward is sensory-concrete; distinguished from sābiqūn who receive proximity-reward (relational). The right-hand are saved; the foremost are *near*.

## 6. The Four-Class Cosmic Typology (56:7-14)

> **[56:7-14]**
> وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجًا ثَلَٰثَةً • فَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ • وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ • وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ • أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ • فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ • ثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ • وَقَلِيلٌ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ
>
> *"You will be three groups: the right-hand companions — what are the right-hand companions? / the left-hand companions — what are the left-hand companions? / and the foremost, the foremost — those are the brought-near, in the Gardens of Bliss; a multitude from the earlier ones, and a few from the later ones."*

**Five decisive structural moves:**

1. **Three, not two.** *Azwājan thalāthatan* — the Quran's explicit cosmic anthropology breaks the believer/disbeliever binary into three. The third class (foremost) is carved *out of* the believers, not added as a fourth category.

2. **Intensification by doubling.** *al-sābiqūna al-sābiqūna* — no description, only repetition. The foremost are defined entirely by being foremost; the doubling is their only predicate. Identity = the act.

3. **Grammatical equation.** *ulāʾika al-muqarrabūn* — "those are the brought-near" — the foremost ARE the brought-near. Not "they will receive proximity" but "they ARE the proximity-class." The eschatological status is identical with what they were in life.

4. **The time-inversion.** *Thullatun min al-awwalīn / wa-qalīlun min al-ākhirīn* — many from the early (pre-Islamic) generations, few from the later. Anti-progressivist: spiritual elites are *rarer* as history advances, not more common.

5. **Demographic contrast with right-hand.** [56:39-40] — for the right-hand, *thullatun min al-awwalīn / wa-thullatun min al-ākhirīn* — multitudes from BOTH generations. The mass-saved are temporally symmetric; the elite are historically skewed backward.

The cross-cutting finding (from success investigation): the cosmic tripartition ranks by *proximity to God*, not by deeds-volume, knowledge-depth, or social status. The taxonomic principle is relational distance from the divine center.

## 7. sbq — The Foremost: Active Orientation

**Lemma profile (37 total across 7 lemmas):**

| Lemma | Arabic | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| sabaqa | سَبَقَ | 20 | verb, "to precede/outrun" |
| sābiq | سَابِق | 7 | active participle, "foremost" |
| istabaqā | ٱسْتَبَقَ | 5 | competitive verb, "to race each other" |
| masbūqīn | مَسْبُوقِين | 2 | passive "outrun/defeated" |
| sābiqū | سَابِقُو | 1 | verb-participle |
| sābiqāt | سَّٰبِقَٰت | 1 | "the racing-ahead ones" (79:4) |
| sabq | سَبْق | 1 | noun "outrunning" |

The dominant verb (*sabaqa*, 20×) means to outrun, precede, get ahead of. The dominant use is temporal or competitive. The eschatological *sābiqūn* are the people who *raced to good works*.

**Key eschatological loci:**

- **[56:10-11]** — foremost = brought-near (§6)
- **[23:61]** — *ulāʾika yusāriʿūna fī al-khayrāti wa-hum lahā sābiqūn* — "those hasten in goods, and they are the foremost in them." *Yusāriʿūna* is present-tense continuous — ongoing posture, not past achievement.
- **[35:32]** — *wa-minhum sābiqun bi-l-khayrāti bi-idhni llāh* — "some are foremost in goods, by God's permission." The qualifier *bi-idhni llāh* makes their forwardness divinely-enabled, not self-generated — matches the anti-capability thesis.
- **[9:100]** — first forerunners of Emigrants and Helpers. Historical instantiation.
- **[57:21]** — *sābiqū ilā maghfiratin* — imperative: race toward forgiveness.

**The active-orientation signature:** The sābiqūn are not described as having *achieved* a status. They are perpetually racing. The active participle *sābiqūn* = the-ones-currently-outrunning. Their class membership is their momentum, not their arrival.

## 8. qrb — Muqarrabūn: Humans Joining Angelic Ranks

**Lemma profile (96 total across 11 lemmas):**

| Lemma | Arabic | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| qarīb | قَرِيب | 26 | adj/n "near" (spatial/temporal) |
| aqrab | أَقْرَب | 19 | comparative "nearer" |
| qurbā | قُرْبَىٰ | 16 | "kinship/nearness" |
| yaqrabu | يَقْرَبُ | 11 | verb "to approach/come near" |
| muqarrabūn | مُقَرَّبُون | 8 | "the brought-near" (pass. participle) |
| qarrabā | قَرَّبَ | 5 | verb "to bring near / to offer" |
| iqtaraba | ٱقْتَرَبَ | 5 | verb "to draw near" (of Hour, etc.) |
| qurbān | قُرْبَان | 3 | "offering/sacrifice" |
| ... | | | |

The key eschatological lemma is *muqarrabūn* (8×) — the brought-near, passive Form-II participle. They are *made-near* by God, not self-approaching.

**Human muqarrabūn:**
- [56:11] — the foremost ARE the brought-near (cosmic top class)
- [56:88-89] — at the moment of death: *fa-ammā in kāna mina al-muqarrabīn / fa-rawḥun wa-rayḥānun wa-jannatu naʿīm* — "if he is of the brought-near — comfort (*rawḥ*), fragrance (*rayḥān*), and gardens of bliss"
- [83:21] — *yashhaduhu al-muqarrabūn* — the brought-near *witness* the book of the abrār (kitāb in ʿilliyyīn)
- [83:28] — *ʿaynan yashrabu bihā al-muqarrabūn* — a spring (Tasnīm) from which the brought-near drink directly; the abrār drink from its mixture (83:27)

**The 83:21-28 Tasnīm gradient:** The abrār drink from a sealed cup (83:25) mixed with Tasnīm (83:27). The muqarrabūn drink Tasnīm *directly* (83:28). The spring belongs to the muqarrabūn; the abrār receive the mixture. Internal hierarchy: muqarrabūn > abrār, not equivalent.

**Angelic muqarrabūn:** [4:172] — *wa-lā al-malāʾikatu al-muqarrabūn* — the brought-near angels, the same term covering the seraphic rank. [3:45] — Jesus described as *mina al-muqarrabīn*, of the brought-near.

**The theological claim:** The *muqarrabūn* label bridges human and angelic ontology. Human brought-near *join* the relational category that the highest angels already occupy. Elite human salvation is not a better garden but *crossing into the proximity-class* shared with celestial beings.

## 9. The Complete Eschatological Class Typology (Integrated)

**The three-class cosmic tripartition (56:7-14) cross-mapped with 4:69 and the nested-disposition hierarchy:**

| Class | Root/Formula | Reward | Demographics | Defining Posture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **muqarrabūn / sābiqūn** | sbq + qrb | rawḥ + rayḥān + gardens; drink Tasnīm directly | Many ancients, few latecomers [56:13-14] | Racing toward good by God's permission |
| **abrār** | brr | kitāb in ʿilliyyīn; drink Tasnīm-mixture [83:25-28]; kāfūr fountain [76:5] | (overlaps with muqarrabūn?) | Pure-sincerity giving for God's face |
| **aṣḥāb al-yamīn** | ymn | Sensory paradise: gardens, shade, food, water, virgins [56:27-40] | Multitudes from both generations [56:39-40] | Faith + mutual exhortation [90:17-18] |
| **aṣḥāb al-shimāl / mashʾama** | — | Fire, scalding water, black smoke [56:41-56]; saqar [74:26-29] | — | Refused to feed, pray, believe [74:43-46] |

**The 4:69 four-favored overlay:** These describe *modes of enacted saved-ness*, not cosmic-rank positions. Nabiyyīn = divinely-appointed office; ṣiddīqūn = total verification (maps to muqarrabūn range); shuhadāʾ = testimony-to-the-death (overlaps with sābiqūn: they gave everything racing forward); ṣāliḥūn = broad righteous (maps to aṣḥāb al-yamīn).

**The witness-class double-link:** The shuhadāʾ (4:69) appear between ṣiddīqūn and ṣāliḥūn. The muqarrabūn (83:21) *witness* the abrār's record in ʿilliyyīn. Witness is the function at both the entry to the favored classes (shuhadāʾ) and the apex (muqarrabūn as witnesses). The entire hierarchy is witnessed through.

## 10. Negative Space

The Quran's class hierarchy has no **intermediate zone between saved and damned** — there is no "mostly-good" category, no purgatorial middle class. The three types at 56:7 are three, not four. The witness-system (bodies testifying against themselves) is framed entirely for the **damned** — the right-hand companions receive not body-testimony but angelic greeting (*salāmun laka*, 56:91). What is conspicuously absent: any verse where the right-hand companions' skins testify *for* them.

## 11. Verses for Direct Study

1. **[41:19-23]** — The skin-testimony sequence: the body speaks against itself, the wrong assumption exposed, khāsirīn as the conclusion.
2. **[56:7-14 + 56:39-40]** — The full cosmic tripartition with demographic inverts for foremost vs right-hand.
3. **[83:21-28]** — The Tasnīm gradient: muqarrabūn drink directly, abrār drink the mixture. The hierarchy within the elite.
4. **[4:69]** — The four favored classes with the *maʿa* (companionship-with) framing and the qualifying "bounty from God."
5. **[4:172]** — Muqarrabūn applied to angels: the same proximity-class already includes celestial ranks.

## 12. Rabbit Holes

- **The Tasnīm problem.** If muqarrabūn drink Tasnīm directly and abrār get the mixture, are they two ranks or two roles? 83:21 puts the muqarrabūn as *witnesses* of the abrār's record — they are not *above* the abrār as judges but *alongside* them as attestors. The Tasnīm-gradient may describe drinking access, not ontological rank.

- **Body-witness vs recording-angels.** Two witness-architectures coexist: the body testifying (41:20, 36:65, 24:24) and the Kirāmān Kātibīn recording (82:11, 50:18). No verse explicitly reconciles them — same evidence, different evidence, or cumulative layers?

- **Why are the foremost *mostly from ancient generations*?** The anti-progressivist demographic [56:13-14] — *qalīlun min al-ākhirīn* — is unexplained theologically. One reading: the earliest nations faced God's signs *directly* (Noah's flood, Abraham's fire, Moses' sea) and responding rightly to direct signs is harder-harder-harder to replicate. Another: the Quran's anti-institutional grammar (no accumulated spiritual technique) makes historical proximity to prophets the variable.

- **The right-hand companions defined by descent (90:11-18).** In surah 90, the aṣḥāb al-maymana are defined not by what they possess but by *iqtiḥām al-ʿaqaba* — storming the steep ascent downward into solidarity (freeing slaves, feeding hungry orphans, mutual exhortation). The sensory-paradise reward is given to those who performed the most *descending* acts. The inverse: those who rise (in the world) fall; those who descend (into others' suffering) are raised.

- **$hd and $hd: the shahīd / martyr etymology vs shahīd / witness.** Classical Arabic lexicography treats them as one root with semantic expansion: the martyr (*shahīd*) is a witness because the blood of the martyred testifies. Linguistically coherent; but the Quran never explicitly makes this derivation. Whether the martyr-sense was read back into 4:69 from post-Quranic tradition, or whether 4:69 itself intends the dual sense, remains underdetermined from the text alone.
