# Success in the Quran — Grand Synthesis

> Phase 4 integrated synthesis. Reads across 1 structural, 4 root, 2 narrative, and 2 cross-cutting Phase-1/2 files. Connects to the prior Time, Teleportation, and Cognition investigations.

---

## 1. Thesis

**The Quran does not define success as achievement. It defines success as ESCAPE FROM A DEFAULT-LOSS, granted by divine guidance, actualized through human cooperation, and visible in orientation rather than capability.**

Four structural facts hold up the claim:

- The Quran's most concentrated success-statement, Surah 103, opens by placing **all of humanity** *fī khusr* (in loss) and constructs success only as the *illā*-exception.
- The "qad aflaḥa" (he has certainly succeeded) formula is **always** preceded by, or paired with, conditions that name **orientation** (purification, prayer, faith, mutual exhortation) — never with capability terms (intelligence, courage, leadership, wealth, knowledge-as-possession).
- Across every believer-list (2:1-5, 23:1-11, 25:63-77, 70:22-35, 76:5-12, 90:11-18, 103:1-3) the trait-bank totals roughly **40 distinct orientations** and **zero capabilities**. The negative space is exhaustive and consistent.
- Both directions of the success-architecture are simultaneously asserted: divine grace is upstream (24:21 — *had it not been for Allah's grace, not one of you would have ever been purified*) AND human cooperation is required (13:11 — *Allah does not change a people until they change what is in themselves*). The Quran refuses both pure determinism and pure self-causation.

Success is therefore a **rescue-event**, not a wage. The successful are not climbers — they are those who turned, were turned-toward, and were drawn out.

## 3. The Five Master Patterns

### a. The default-loss frame: success is escape, not achievement

**Cross-confirmed by:** flH/fwz/xsr root agent + believer-lists narrative + short-formulas narrative + research-success-as-escape (Phase 2).

The Quran's signature success-grammar is the *illā*-exception: humanity is in failure-state X, **except** those who Y. Five structural instances:

| Verse | Default | Exception | Carved-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 103:2-3 | *fī khusr* — in loss | *illā* | believed + did righteous + mutually exhorted truth + patience |
| 70:19-22 | *halūʿ* — restless-by-creation | *illā* | the praying ones (then 10 traits) |
| 95:4-5 | *radadnāhu asfala sāfilīn* — cast lowest | *illā* | believers + righteous deeds |
| 12:53 | *ammāra bi-l-sūʾ* — soul commands evil | *illā mā raḥima rabbī* | those upon whom God shows mercy |
| 76:3 | *hadaynāhu al-sabīla* — shown the way | binary | grateful vs ungrateful |

This is not a conditional ("do X to get Y") — it is a *rescue from default*. The *muflihūn* are not those who built success; they are exceptions to the constitutive condition of *al-insān*. Note: the verb *waqā* (protect, shield) recurs as the condition for becoming muflih — *whoever is protected from stinginess of soul* [59:9, 64:16]. **Success is a passively-received protection, not an active conquest.**

The flH agent's decisive observation: in [7:8-9] and [23:102-103], the muflihūn vs khāsirūn binary is determined by *scale-weight* — not by what you produced but by what was found in you. Surah 103, the highest-density success-vocabulary surah in the corpus (30%), names *time itself* as the oath-witness, making loss the universal temporal condition.

### b. The orientation-not-capability theology

**Cross-confirmed by:** believer-lists narrative (negative-space catalog) + class-hierarchy Phase 2 (40-trait inventory) + cognition-investigation cross-link.

Across every believer-list, every *qad aflaḥa* formula, and every cosmic-class taxonomy, **no constitutive trait names a capability**. The trait-bank decomposes into three orientations:

- **Vertical (toward God):** prayer, dhikr, tazakkā, khushūʿ, yaqīn, tawba, mushfiqūn, dawn-istighfār — orientation-acts directed toward the divine.
- **Horizontal (toward others):** zakāh, ḥaqq maʿlūm, sincere giving, freeing slaves, feeding orphans, honest testimony, peaceful response to provocation, mutual exhortation, chastity, family-prayer — orientation-acts directed toward neighbor.
- **Internal (toward self):** ṣabr, balanced spending (*qawāman*), avoidance of *laghw*, restraint of anger, forgiveness of others, acknowledgment of one's faults, active responsiveness to signs.

What is **conspicuously absent**: *ʿaql* (intellect-as-possession), *ʿilm* (knowledge-as-trait), *shajāʿa* (courage), *quwwa* (strength), *imāma* (leadership), *ḥusn ḥadīth* (eloquence), *ḥasab/nasab* (lineage), *ghinā* (wealth), *shuhra* (fame), *naṣr ʿaskarī* (military victory) as constitutive trait. The cognition-investigation finding crystallizes this: knowledge is *instrumental* (the channel through which orientation forms), never *constitutive* (the content of saved-ness). Success agent-nouns describe **who is currently exhibiting an orientation**, not who holds an institutional rank.

### c. The nested-and-favored class hierarchy

**Cross-confirmed by:** wqy/brr/SlH root agent (nested dispositions) + short-formulas narrative (4:69) + class-hierarchy Phase 2 (56:7-14 + integration).

Three architectures describe the same population:

1. **Phase-1 nesting:** ṣāliḥūn (broadest, faith+deeds) ⊃ muttaqūn (God-shielded disposition) ⊃ abrār (silent-sincerity elite). The decisive textual move is at [2:177]: the verse defines *birr* by long enumerated list (belief in 5 objects, giving wealth to 6 categories, prayer + zakāh, covenant-keeping, patience under three forms of difficulty), then collapses: *those are the truthful, those are the muttaqūn*. **Birr is the content of taqwā.** And [2:1-5] equates *muttaqūn* with *muflihūn*. The Quran's first act (its second surah's opening five ayahs) is to fuse disposition with success.

2. **4:69 favored types:** *nabiyyīn / ṣiddīqīn / shuhadāʾ / ṣāliḥīn* — the obedient become *maʿa* (with) them. *Maʿa* is decisive: company, not rank-merger. The closing *wa-ḥasuna ulāʾika rafīqā* (excellent are those as companions) confirms; [4:70] adds *that is bounty from God* (faḍl) — even companionship is grace, not earned promotion. The *ṣiddīqūn* are the disputed middle: the Quran names Mary [66:12], Idrīs [19:56], Yūsuf [12:46] as ṣiddīq — verification by total inner sincerity, between revelation-bearing (prophets) and testimony (martyrs).

3. **56:7-14 cosmic tripartition:** *al-sābiqūn al-sābiqūn* (foremost-foremost) = *al-muqarrabūn* (brought-near) ⟶ *aṣḥāb al-maymana* (right-hand) ⟶ *aṣḥāb al-mashʾama* (left-hand). The Quran's only explicit *three-fold* anthropology. Two structural surprises: (i) the foremost are doubled and unmodified — the defining identity is *being-foremost*, no further description; (ii) the demographic inverts time — *thullatun min al-awwalīn / wa qalīlun min al-ākhirīn* (many of the early, few of the latter). **Anti-progressivist:** the Quran refuses a model where humanity gets spiritually better over time.

The integrating principle: **all three rank by orientation.** Office (4:69) is conferred; depth (Phase 1) is moral-spiritual; proximity (56) is relational. None introduces achievement or possession as criterion. The 4:69 four-classes is the **tax mode of saved-ness** (how it is enacted); the Phase-1 nesting is the **psychological depth** (how it is felt internally); the 56 tripartition is the **eschatological location** (how close you sit to God). Same humans, three slices.

### d. The three-vocabulary partition: naṣr / fawz / falāḥ

**Cross-confirmed by:** hdy/nSr root agent + flH/fwz/xsr root agent + structural co-occurrence (zero overlap in same ayah).

| Root | Domain | Tense / form | Top co-roots | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **naṣr** | Conflict / collective / historical | Verb-heavy; *yanṣur* (he gives victory) | wly (allegiance), kfr (disbelief), Zlm (oppression) | [110:1-3] *idhā jāʾa naṣru Allāh* |
| **fawz** | Eschatological event | Noun-heavy (*fawz* 19/29); verb only perfect (3 occ — *fāza*) | EZm (great), jnn (gardens), nhr (rivers) | [3:185] *whoever is pulled away from the Fire and admitted to Paradise has triumphed* |
| **falāḥ** | Dispositional / ongoing class | Imperfect verb + agentive noun *muflihūn* | Amn (faith), Slw (prayer), Alh, rbb | [2:5] *ulāʾika hum al-muflihūn* |

The negative-space test holds cleanly: naṣr almost never appears with paradise vocabulary (jnn, nhr); fawz almost never appears with battle vocabulary (qtl); falāḥ almost never appears in mission-completion contexts. **Each register owns its domain.** The three vocabularies handle three temporal-spatial scales of "winning": adversarial-historical (naṣr), eschatological-completed (fwz), dispositional-ongoing (flH). Conflating them flattens the architecture.

A consequence: when modern translations render fawz, naṣr, and falāḥ all as "success," they erase a deliberate semantic differentiation. The structural agent's translator-divergence finding — fwz/wqy verses have outlier rates above 9% for Pickthall, Khattab, and Arberry — confirms the resistance.

### e. Success-is-given-but-to-reachers: divine agency + human cooperation

**Cross-confirmed by:** hdy/nSr root agent (yashāʾu Allāh structure) + zkw/Slw/Sbr root agent (87:14 vs 24:21 paradox) + research-success-as-escape Phase 2 (full integration).

The Quran asserts both poles maximally:

**Divine-agency pole:**
- [28:56] *innaka lā tahdī man aḥbabta wa-lākinna Allāha yahdī man yashāʾu* — even prophethood does not transfer the guiding function.
- [24:21] *wa-lawlā faḍlu Allāhi ʿalaykum wa-raḥmatuhū mā zakā minkum min aḥadin abadan wa-lākinna Allāha yuzakkī man yashāʾu* — without grace, **not one of you ever** would have been purified.
- [7:43] *mā kunnā li-nahtadiya lawlā an hadānā Allāh* — paradise-dwellers' retrospective testimony foreclosing self-credit at the moment of greatest possible self-credit.
- [8:17] *wa-mā ramayta idh ramayta wa-lākinna Allāha ramā* — even physical motor-acts get reattributed.

**Human-cooperation pole:**
- [13:11] *Allāh lā yughayyiru mā bi-qawmin ḥattā yughayyirū mā bi-anfusihim* — divine change is contingent on prior human change.
- [47:7] *in tanṣurū Allāha yanṣurkum* — human action triggers divine action.
- [2:45 / 2:153] *istaʿīnū bi-l-ṣabri wa-l-ṣalāh* — help comes only to those who seek through patience and prayer.
- [87:14] *qad aflaḥa man tazakkā* — reflexive Form V places agency with the human.

The paradox dissolves in [91:7-10]: God *fashioned* the soul (91:7), God *inspired* both depravity AND taqwā (91:8 — capacity divinely deposited), the human *purifies* it (91:9 — *zakkāhā*) or *buries* it (91:10 — *dassāhā*). **Three layers — divine endowment, human actualization, divine grace.** Remove any and the theology collapses. The Quran's signature theological grammar is *cooperative*, not *unilateral* in either direction. This is also what differentiates Quranic soteriology from Augustinian *sola gratia* (rescue is unilateral) and from Aristotelian *aretē* (excellence is self-produced); see comparative-religion contrast in research-success-as-escape §7.

The cleanest single-verse fusion is **[2:5]**: *ulāʾika ʿalā hudan min rabbihim wa-ulāʾika hum al-muflihūn*. Two clauses, both anchored by *ulāʾika*, placing the same group at both poles. Guidance is *from their Lord* (*min* = source). Success is who they *are* (predicate nominative). **Guidance is the cause; success is the effect; both predicated of the same humans, but only one carries the divine-source clause.**

## 4. The Path-Architecture: What the Quran Says Is the Way to Falāḥ

The "habits and routines" of the saved, drawn from the believer-lists and *qad aflaḥa* formulas:

**Prayer (Slw) — the structural FRAME.** Surah 23 brackets the entire believer-list with prayer: 23:2 opens with *khushūʿ* (interior humility), 23:9 closes with *ḥifāẓ* (exterior maintenance). Between them, every other trait — turning from laghw, zakāh, chastity, covenant-keeping — is what authentic prayer produces in the character. Surah 70's *illā al-muṣallīn* (except the praying ones) makes prayer the structural exit from the *halūʿ* default. Prayer is the ground-state of the muflih.

**Purification (zkw) — the SOUL-WORK.** Two Meccan equations: [87:14] *qad aflaḥa man tazakkā* and [91:9] *qad aflaḥa man zakkāhā*. The reflexive (*tazakkā*, Form V) places agency with the human; the transitive with object-suffix (*zakkāhā*) treats the *nafs* as object. Purification is preparation: [87:15] continues *fa-ṣallā* — "then he prayed." Tazakkā flows into prayer. The institutional outer expression is *zakāh* (alms): [9:103] explicitly fuses them — the Prophet's act of taking alms is *tuṭahhiruhum wa tuzakkīhim* (purifies them and increases them).

**Patience (Sbr) — the FUEL.** Patience is overwhelmingly verbal (62 of 103 occurrences) — an *act* performed repeatedly, not a trait you possess. Three sub-senses: patience under affliction, patience from sin, patience in obedience. The *istaʿīnū bi-l-ṣabri wa-l-ṣalāh* formula [2:45, 2:153] makes ṣabr instrumental — patience is the inner stance that keeps prayer meaningful. [25:75] explicitly attributes *al-ghurfa* (the elevated chamber) to ṣabr: "for what they patiently endured." [103:3] makes patience *communal*: *tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr* — mutual exhortation, Form VI reciprocal.

**Taqwā (wqy) — the DISPOSITION.** *Taqwā* is not fear-trembling (*khawf*); it is active shielding-by-obedience. The Form VIII *ittaqā* (166 occ) is reflexive: "make yourself shielded." The Quran's first believer-profile [2:1-5] equates *muttaqūn* with *muflihūn* — disposition IS outcome. Taqwā is the *precondition* for benefiting from revelation: *hudā li-l-muttaqīn* (2:2) — guidance is *for* those who already shield-themselves-by-obedience. The most-honored are *atqākum* [49:13] — most God-shielded.

**Belief + good deeds (Amn + ʿamal ṣāliḥ) — the IRREDUCIBLE PAIR.** *Alladhīna āmanū wa-ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt* is the single most-repeated descriptor in the Quran (~52 occurrences in this exact form, dozens more in variants). Why always paired? A deed without faith has no directionality (cannot be *ṣāliḥ*); faith without deeds has no expression. The pairing is constitutive — orientation + evidence-it-is-real.

**Guidance (hdy) — the EPISTEMIC PRECONDITION.** Surah 1's *ihdina al-ṣirāṭ al-mustaqīm* is the Quran's foundational prayer; the entire Fatiha's grammar is built around one verb of guidance. [2:5] makes the chain explicit: *the guided* (those upon guidance from their Lord) **are** *the successful*. Form VIII *ihtadā* describes a state of being-guided, never a self-initiated act — the agent is the one guided, but the underlying cause is divine.

**Mutual exhortation (tawāṣaw) — the COMMUNAL CONTAINER.** [103:3] and [90:17] both use the Form VI reciprocal: *tawāṣaw bi-l-ḥaqq*, *tawāṣaw bi-l-ṣabr*, *tawāṣaw bi-l-marḥama*. This action requires two or more parties — it cannot be done alone. **Isolation fails by default** in Quranic anthropology. The fourfold *illā*-exception of 103 places two relational acts (mutual exhortation to truth, mutual exhortation to patience) alongside the two individual acts (faith, deeds). Community is not added on top — it is built into the minimum.

These seven elements are the integrated system. The structural finding from the co-occurrence analysis confirms: zkw and Slw share **28 ayahs (lift 5.09)** — the strongest pairing in the success-vocabulary corpus. The vertical (prayer) and horizontal (zakāh) axes appear together at [2:43, 2:83, 2:110, 9:5, 9:11, 22:41, 98:5] — wherever the Quran defines the dīn al-qayyimah (the upright religion), this couplet recurs.

What is **not** in the path: Fasting (*sawm*), Pilgrimage (*Ḥajj*), and study (ʿilm-as-trait) appear **in zero of the compressed success-formulas**. This is the believer-lists narrative's negative-space finding: the Quran's compressed success-definitions exclude even major obligatory rituals. Falāḥ is not constituted by ritual completeness — it is constituted by orientation-practice (prayer, purification, patience, mutual exhortation).

## 5. The Connection to Prior Investigations

The success synthesis surfaces a meta-pattern that two prior investigations (cognition, time) and one parallel investigation (teleportation) independently surfaced.

| Investigation | What the Quran prefers | What the Quran refuses |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** (concept-time-deep) | Endpoints / orientation (*qabl*, *baʿd*, threshold) | Process / interior (*during*, middle, gradient) |
| **Teleportation** (concept-teleportation-deep) | Result-statements (throne at A → B) | Mechanism-description (how it moved) |
| **Cognition** (concept-research-deep) | Cognitive *verbs* (*ʿalima*, *dhakara*, *tafakkara*) | Cognitive *institutions* (*ʿulamāʾ*, *fuqahāʾ*, *mufti* — absent) |
| **Success** (this synthesis) | Orientation (relational quality, divine source) | Capability / institution / mechanism / process |

Three convergent claims emerge:

**(a) The Quran is a theology of decision, not of experience.** It addresses a creature at thresholds, asking which way that creature has turned. It does not narrate an interior unfolding (time-finding: no *during*). It does not credit accumulated capability (success-finding: no faculty/feat traits). It does not inventory possessed institutions (cognition-finding: no nominalized roles). It asks one question, in one tense, at one point: *toward what are you oriented?*

**(b) The Quran's anthropology is coherent: humans don't act, humans receive or refuse.** Cognition: knowledge-as-reception, kernel-people are honored not for production but for orientation to what was disclosed. Teleportation: extraordinary deeds are explicitly reattributed (*you did not throw when you threw, but Allah threw* — 8:17). Success: even the most extreme self-purification verse [87:14] is countered by [24:21] — *had it not been for grace, not one of you would have ever been purified*. The four investigations triangulate a single anthropology: **humans are recipients with cooperation-duty, not autonomous causes.**

**(c) Mechanism-silence is the doctrine.** Teleportation: no vocabulary for *how* the throne moved — naming a mechanism would name a created agent doing God's work. Success: no description of *how* the *halūʿ* becomes *muṣallī* — bracketed by *yashāʾu Allāh* on the divine side and *istaʿīnū* on the human side. Time: no *during* vocabulary — only *qabl* and *baʿd*, never the threshold's interior. The withholding is not a gap; it is **apophatic discipline**. To describe the mechanism would be to domesticate divine agency. The Quran reports juxtaposition (was-A, then-B), not process.

A further parallel: the cognition-finding's **knowledge-over-strength inversion** at 27:38-40 (Solomon's throne — Book-knowledge outpaces strength by orders of magnitude) maps onto the success-architecture's **guidance-over-effort inversion**. The substantive variable is epistemic-receptive, not energetic-productive. The *muflihūn* are not those who tried hardest; they are those who received guidance (2:5 — *ʿalā hudan min rabbihim*). Quranic cosmology and soteriology share the same operating logic.

## 6. Negative Space — What Success Is NOT

The architecture is defined as much by absences. The following are conspicuously *refused* across the entire success-corpus:

- **No "self-made" success** — no formula in which the human alone is the cause; *yashāʾu* + *lawlā* clauses foreclose autonomous self-causation [28:56, 24:21, 7:43, 8:17].
- **No celebration of human capability per se** — no praise of intelligence, courage, leadership, eloquence, strength, or wealth as constitutive of saved-ness.
- **No comparative ranking by worldly metrics** — the most-honored is *atqā* (49:13), not *aʿlam* (most knowledgeable), *aghnā* (richest), or *aqwā* (strongest).
- **No leadership / political / military success vocabulary** as saved-trait — *qāʾid, raʾīs, malik, sulṭān* not among saved-classes; *naṣr* is divine-given, never earned by strategy.
- **No "luck" or "chance"** — success is intentional divine act, never random.
- **No knowledge-as-success-trait** — *ʿilm* appears only as instrumental (the channel of orientation), never as constitutive (see cognition synthesis).
- **No nominalized "great person" / "elite" institution** — *ʿabqarī* (55:76) means a fine carpet, not a genius. No institutional saved-class (no *ʿulamāʾ*, no aristocracy, no priesthood).
- **No celebration of the foremost generations as a category to be IMITATED** — they are *praised as past* (56:13 *thullatun min al-awwalīn*), not held up as INSTITUTION. The anti-progressivism cuts both ways: humanity does not get better, but neither does the past become a guild to claim membership in. You can only be a sābiq by *being foremost yourself*, not by belonging to those who were.
- **No "earning" language for paradise** — always *inheritance* (23:11 — *yarithūna al-firdaws*), *bounty* (4:70 — *dhālika al-faḍl*), *companionship* (4:69 — *maʿa*), *being honored* (70:35 — *mukramūn*). Paradise is not a wage.
- **No middle state** between *muflihūn* and *khāsirūn*; no "moderately successful," no "advancing in success," no graduated success vocabulary. Falāḥ-language is binary in modality, though the cosmic 56-tripartition adds *foremost-vs-right-hand* gradation among the saved.
- **No "self-actualization" / "potential-fulfillment" terms.** Modern frameworks center on potential as the substrate of success. The Quran's substrate is *fitrah* (divinely-deposited capacity for taqwā, 91:8, 30:30) — but fitrah is divine endowment, recoverable through purification, never earned and never owned. Failure mode: *dassāhā* (burying what was placed there). Success mode: uncovering, not building.
- **No "successful in this world"** — *dunyā*-success is the **opposite** pole [4:77, 29:64, 87:16-17]. The believers' worldly status (often poor, persecuted, displaced) is not adjusted upward by their saved-ness.

These refusals are *cohesive*: every absence forecloses a move that would convert success into (a) a meritocratic transaction, (b) an autonomous accomplishment, (c) a graduated process, or (d) a comparative ranking. What remains is the binary, the rescue, and cooperative grace. Same discipline as cognition's negative-space (no doubt-method, no hypothesis, no *ijtihād*-as-cognition) and time's (no *during*, no middle, no abstract *zamān*). One pattern: **the Quran refuses the worldly category-set in order to install its own.**

## 7. Open Questions and Rabbit Holes

Phase 3 was skipped per the cost-optimization plan. The richest unresolved threads:

1. **The fitrah architecture.** [30:30] presents creation-on-fitrah as divine endowment. How does this square with *halūʿ* (70:19), *ammāra-bi-l-sūʾ* (12:53), *ʿajūl* (21:37), *radadnāhu asfala sāfilīn* (95:5)? Are the soul's two poles (*fujūr* + *taqwā*, 91:8) the same fitrah, or is fitrah specifically the *taqwā* side that depravity tries to bury? This connects to the default-loss frame and may resolve the apparent tension between created-good and created-failing humanity.

2. **The three nafs states (12:53 ammāra / 75:2 lawwāma / 89:27 muṭmaʾinna).** The Quran names three psychic states — soul commanding-to-evil, soul self-reproaching, soul at-peace — but does not narrate the transitions. This is proto-psychology that the Sufi tradition later expands into a developmental arc. Question: is the Sufi developmental reading load-bearing extrapolation, or vocabulary-gap retrofit (mirroring the time-investigation's finding that Sufi *waqt*-mysticism fills a Quranic vocabulary gap)?

3. **The Sufi tazkiya tradition's reading of zkw + flH.** A targeted analysis of how al-Ghazālī, Ibn ʿArabī, and the *Iḥyāʾ* tradition map *tazakkā* onto the *muflih* identity would test whether classical Sufism preserves or modifies the divine-grace-required-but-human-cooperation-also-required structure surfaced here.

4. **The 4:69 four-classes hierarchy in detail.** What is the *prose* of *ṣiddīqiyya*? The Quran names Mary [66:12], Idrīs [19:56], and Yūsuf [12:46] as ṣiddīq — three different kinds of total verification. A focused investigation of the *Sdq* root in its ṣiddīqūn-marking lemmas would clarify the disputed middle of the four-classes hierarchy.

5. **The Meccan vs Medinan distribution of negative anthropology.** *Halūʿ*, *kanūd*, *yaʾūs kafūr*, *ʿajūl*, *fujūr*-of-soul cluster early/middle Meccan; *ammāra bi-l-sūʾ*, *ẓalūm jahūl* cluster later. Is the default-loss frame foundational (in the Quran's earliest theology) or a specific Meccan rhetorical posture? The structural agent's finding that flH and Slw are 0% Meccan in noun form, while brr and fwz are 70%+ Meccan, suggests a phase-shift in success-vocabulary itself.

6. **The *yashāʾu* operator across the corpus.** *Yahdī man yashāʾu*, *yuzakkī man yashāʾu*, *yanṣuru man yashāʾu*, *yaghfiru li-man yashāʾu*. Map all *yashāʾu* + divine-rescue-verb occurrences and test against human-cooperation conditionals (13:11, 47:7) for relative weight. This would harden the cooperative-grace claim or identify pressure points where determinism dominates.

7. **The mukhliṣ / mukhlaṣ active-vs-passive distinction (xlS, 31 occ).** Active (those-who-make-themselves-sincere) vs passive (those-whose-sincerity-is-divine-conferred). At [37:40, 37:74, 37:128] the *mukhlaṣūn* are the exempt class spared from the eschatological gathering. May map onto the sābiqūn / muqarrabūn cosmic top.
