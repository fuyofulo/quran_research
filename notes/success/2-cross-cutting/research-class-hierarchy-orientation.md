# Class Hierarchy of the Saved + Orientation-Not-Capability

> Phase 2 cross-cutting synthesis. Reads across the six Phase 1 files (wqy/brr/SlH, flH/fwz/xsr, hdy/nSr, believer-lists, short-formulas, structurals) and integrates with the cognition + time deep syntheses.

---

## 1. Headline Finding

The Quran names roughly twenty distinct classes of "the saved" — but no class is defined by **capability**. Across every list, every formula, every cosmic ranking, success-traits are exclusively traits of **orientation**: vertical (toward God), horizontal (toward others), internal (toward self). Intelligence, knowledge, courage, leadership, eloquence, wealth, lineage — none appears as a constitutive trait. The classes are not synonyms but a triangulated architecture: Phase 1 surfaced **nested dispositions** (ṣāliḥūn ⊃ muttaqūn ⊃ abrār), 4:69 surfaces **four favored types of saved-ness** (nabiyyīn / ṣiddīqīn / shuhadāʾ / ṣāliḥīn), and 56:7-12 surfaces a **cosmic tripartition** (sābiqūn / right / left). All three converge on the same anthropology: success is a *posture*, not a *possession*.

## 3. The 56:7-14 Cosmic Tripartition

> وَكُنتُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجࣰا ثَلَٰثَةࣰ • فَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ • وَأَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ مَآ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ • وَٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ ٱلسَّٰبِقُونَ • أُوْلَٰٓئِكَ ٱلۡمُقَرَّبُونَ • فِي جَنَّٰتِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ • ثُلَّةࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأَوَّلِينَ • وَقَلِيلࣱ مِّنَ ٱلۡأٓخِرِينَ
>
> *"And you will become three kinds: the right-hand companions — what are the right-hand companions? / and the left-hand companions — what are the left-hand companions? / and the foremost, the foremost — those are the brought-near, in the Gardens of Bliss; a company from the early ones, and a few from the later ones."* [56:7-14, Saheeh-composite]

Five decisive moves:

1. **Three, not two.** *Azwājan thalāthatan* — the Quran's only explicit cosmic anthropology of *three*. The corpus's dominant believer/disbeliever binary is here re-cut.
2. **The right-hand are saved but ordinary.** Reward (56:27-40) is sensory: lote-trees, banana-trees, shade, water, fruit, raised beds.
3. **The foremost are doubled and unmodified.** *al-sābiqūn al-sābiqūn* — intensification by doubling, no further description. Defining identity is being-foremost.
4. **The foremost ARE the muqarrabūn.** *ulāʾika al-muqarrabūn* — grammatical equation: foremost = brought-near. Their reward is **relational** (proximity), not external. The right-hand inherit gardens; the foremost inherit *nearness*.
5. **The demographic inverts time.** *Thullatun min al-awwalīn / wa qalīlun min al-ākhirīn* — many of the early, few of the latter. Anti-progressive: the Quran refuses a model where humanity gets spiritually better over time.

The 56 architecture ranks by **proximity to God**, not by deeds-volume or knowledge-depth. The taxonomic principle is *relational distance from the divine center*.

## 4. Integrating the Three Hierarchies

The Phase 1 nested-classes finding, 4:69's four favored, and 56:7-12's tripartition describe *the same population from three different angles*. Each angle is structurally orthogonal to the other two.

| Angle | What it ranks | Where the cut is | Top class |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 nesting** | Inner dispositions | By depth of orientation | abrār (purest sincerity) |
| **4:69 favored** | Modes of saved-ness | By how one's saved-ness is *enacted* | nabiyyūn (revelation-bearing) |
| **56:7-12 cosmic** | Eschatological proximity | By distance from God | sābiqūn = muqarrabūn |

The three are **not competing maps**. They are slices through one population:

- A **prophet** (4:69 top) is necessarily muttaqī (Phase 1 core), abrār-grade in disposition, and likely al-sābiqūn (56 top) — but is named by office (4:69), by disposition-depth (Phase 1), or by proximity (56) depending on the idiom.
- The four 4:69 classes form a *graded company* — *maʿa* (with), not equal-with. The obedient become "with" the prophets. They join the company; they do not dissolve into it.

The integrating principle: **all three hierarchies rank by orientation, not capability.** Office is conferred; disposition's depth is moral-spiritual; proximity is relational. None introduces achievement or possession as criterion.

## 5. The Orientation-Not-Capability Thesis

Phase 1's believer-lists agent surfaced a striking pattern: across 23:1-11, 70:22-35, 25:63-77, 2:177, 90:11-18, plus the compressed formulas at 2:1-5, 87:14-15, 91:9-10, 103:1-3, **every named trait** is a trait of orientation, not capability. Below is the consolidated trait-bank, categorized.

### Vertical orientation (toward God)
- Faith / belief in unseen, the Day, scriptures, prophets, angels [2:3-4, 2:177, 25:65, 70:26, 23:7-8, 90:17, 103:3]
- Establishing prayer (Slw) — both performed (qiyām) and guarded (ḥifāẓ) [2:3, 23:2-9, 70:22-23/34, 25:64, 87:15, 24:56]
- Constancy in prayer (dāʾimūn) [70:23]
- Night-prayer (sujūd + qiyām) [25:64, 51:17-18]
- Dhikr — mention/remembrance of God's name [87:15]
- Tazakkā / zakkāhā — soul-purification (zky) [87:14, 91:9]
- Khushūʿ — interior humility in prayer [23:2]
- Yaqīn / certainty about the Hereafter [2:4]
- Prayer for deliverance from Hellfire [25:65-66]
- Tawba — repentance when one falls [25:70-71]
- Mushfiqūn — fearful expectation of the Lord's punishment [70:27-28]
- Pleading at dawn for forgiveness [51:18]

### Horizontal orientation (toward others)
- Zakāh / spending from provision [2:3, 23:4, 24:56]
- Ḥaqq maʿlūm — known right of the asker and deprived [70:24-25]
- Giving wealth despite loving it (relatives, orphans, poor, wayfarer, beggars, freeing slaves) [2:177]
- Pure-sincerity giving li-wajh allāh — neither reward nor thanks sought [76:8-9]
- Feeding on a day of hunger: orphan-of-kinship, dust-covered destitute [90:14-16]
- Freeing the neck (manumission) [90:13]
- Faithfulness to trusts and covenants [23:8, 70:32, 2:177]
- Honest testimony / qāʾimūn bi-shahādātihim [70:33]
- Avoiding false witness [25:72]
- Truthful speech (ṣidq) [2:177, 33:35]
- Walking on earth lightly (hawnan) [25:63]
- Responding to ignorant provocation with peace (salāman) [25:63]
- Mutual exhortation to truth and patience (tawāṣaw) [103:3]
- Mutual exhortation to mercy (tawāṣaw bi-l-marḥama) [90:17]
- Chastity / fidelity to spouse and avoidance of zinā [23:5-7, 70:29-31, 25:68]
- Avoiding murder of the unjust kind [25:68]
- Praying for righteous family / models for the righteous [25:74]
- Enjoining good, forbidding wrong [3:104, 3:110, 9:71]

### Internal orientation (toward self)
- Patience — ṣabr — under hardship, adversity, and battle [2:177, 25:75, 103:3, 90:17]
- Avoidance of laghw (vain speech / passing it with dignity) [23:3, 25:72]
- Balanced spending — qawāman — neither extravagant nor stingy [25:67]
- Self-purification (tazakkā) [87:14]
- Restraint of anger / kāẓimīn al-ghayẓ [3:134]
- Forgiving people (ʿāfīna ʿan al-nās) [3:134]
- Acknowledging one's faults and seeking forgiveness when one errs [3:135]
- Active responsiveness to God's signs (not deaf, not blind) [25:73]
- Striving against the steep ascent (iqtiḥām al-ʿaqaba) [90:11]
- Humility, sincerity (ikhlāṣ) [98:5]

### Capability traits — ABSENT from every list
- **No ʿaql / ʿilm / fikr / dabbara / fiqh** as a possessed faculty (yaʿqilūn is *demanded*, never *possessed*; knowledge is instrumental — see §6)
- **No shajāʿa** (courage), **no quwwa** (strength) as virtue — quwwa appears for camels, walls, jinn, never as saved-trait
- **No imāma / riʾāsa** — *imām* appears as exemplar/record, not success-criterion
- **No ḥusn ḥadīth** (eloquence), **no karam ḥasab / nasab** (lineage)
- **No ghinā** (wealth) — only the *orientation* of wealth counts
- **No shuhra** (fame), **no naṣr ʿaskarī** (military success) as saved-trait — naṣr is divine-given
- **No naturalist intellectual feats** — no list rewards understanding nature, classifying species, proof

The pattern is exhaustive. The anthropology is **relational, not attributive**. The successful are defined by *quality of relationships*, not faculties or feats.

## 6. Why No ʿIlm / ʿAql in Believer-Lists?

This absence is striking precisely because the cognition investigation found a **14-root cognitive constellation** in the Quran (Elm 854×, ḏikr 292×, Ḥkm 210×, bSr 148×, etc.). The Quran is densely cognitive. Yet none of the believer-lists names knowledge, reason, comprehension, or wisdom as a success-trait.

The resolution is the **instrumentality vs constitutionality distinction**:

- **Knowledge is instrumental.** It is the *medium* by which guidance is received. *Hudā* is delivered through the cognitive faculties; the heart that is open *understands* what God reveals. Without cognition, no orientation can form. Cognition is the *channel* of orientation, not its content.
- **Orientation is constitutive.** What gets you into the saved class is not having processed information correctly but having *oriented* — toward God in prayer, toward others in giving, toward yourself in restraint. The orientation, once formed, *is* the salvation.

The cognition investigation's finding crystallizes this: the cognitive ideal is *ulu l-albāb* (people of the inner kernel) — an *honorific* given to those who have *received and remembered*, not to those who have produced new knowledge. The kernel-people are honored not because they discovered something but because they oriented to what was disclosed. **The Quran rewards reception, not production.** Cognition is the receiver; the orientation is the antenna's direction.

Three confirming details:

1. **17:36** — accountability is for hearing, sight, and *fuʾād* (innermost witnessing core). Not for intellect's outputs. Accountability is for *what entered*, not what was *generated*.
2. **Form-VIII *ihtadā*** — to-be-guided is reflexive-passive; the agent is the one guided, source is divine. Cognition's role is *be open*, not *find*.
3. **22:46** — "It is not the eyes that are blinded, but blinded are the hearts within the breasts." Cognitive failure is medical-spiritual, not logical. Saved-ness is open-heart, not high-IQ.

Knowledge without orientation is useless. Orientation is what's measured. 4:69 names *ṣāliḥūn* — believers without specialized knowledge or office — as the broadest favored class. There is no *al-ʿālimūn* in any believer-list.

## 7. The Anti-Elitism Cut

The Quran's class hierarchy is **inverted relative to social hierarchies**.

- **Foremost are demographically backward** [56:13-14]: many of the early, few of the latter. Anti-progressivist.
- **Favored exclude social rank.** 4:69 includes martyrs (who lost worldly status by dying) and the unspecified ṣāliḥūn. Not kings, scholars, or chiefs.
- **Most-honored = most God-shielded.** [49:13] *atqākum* — not *aʿlam* (knowledgeable), *aghnā* (richest), or *aqwā* (strongest).
- **Abrār chief example: silent feeding** [76:8-9] — for God's face, neither reward nor thanks. Elite trait is anonymity, opposite of celebrity.
- **90:11-18 ʿaqaba** redefines "going up" as freeing slaves, feeding hungry orphans, exhorting to patience and mercy. Right-hand companions *descended* into solidarity with the lowly.
- **Naming refuses worldly categories.** All saved-classes are passive-or-relational. No *aʾimma* (leaders), no *aghniyāʾ* (wealthy), no *ʿulamāʾ* in any believer-list.

The saved are **spiritually ranked, not socially ranked**. The two rankings are at points *inverted*: early generations win demographically; silent givers win in elite-class; dying-witnesses outrank kings.

## 8. The Recurrent Meta-Pattern: Connection to Cognition + Time

This success-investigation surfaces a pattern that two prior investigations also found, in different vocabularies. The convergence is not coincidence; it is the Quran's deep grammar.

### Cognition: Attributional, not institutional

The cognition synthesis found the **institutional vocabulary gap**: cognitive *verbs* dense (ʿalima, dhakara, tafakkara, dabbara, faqiha, taʿaqqala), but the *nominalized institutional roles* (ʿulamāʾ, fuqahāʾ, mufti, mujtahid, mufassir) absent.

Identical pattern here. Success-*traits* (taqwā, iḥsān, ṣabr, ṣidq, ikhlāṣ) and *agent-noun classes* (muttaqūn, muḥsinūn, ṣābirūn, ṣādiqūn, mukhliṣūn) describe *who is currently exhibiting the trait*. But there are **no nominalized success-institutions**: no class of "honored ones" defined by initiation, no rank conferred by election, no certificate of saved-ness. Classes are defined by *what one orients toward*, not by *membership in a structure*. Cognitive verbs and success agent-nouns both refuse to crystallize into institutions.

### Time: Orientation, not process

The time synthesis found the Quran is **orientation-obsessed, not process-obsessed**: prefers *qabl/baʿd* over *during/middle*; verbs of salvation are punctual decision-acts (*aslama, tāba, āmana, kafara*), not gradient process-verbs. *"You either have taqwā or you don't."*

Identical pattern here. Success-grammar has no *gradient*. Classes are categorical: you are or aren't muttaqī, muflih, muhtad. No "intermediate ṣāliḥ" or "honors-track abrār." The nested disposition-hierarchy is not a pipeline (you don't graduate from muttaqī to abrār by adding deeds); abrār are *those whose orientation is already pure*. Orientation is binary in modality though graded in depth.

The combined claim across investigations:

| Investigation | What the Quran prefers | What the Quran refuses |
|---|---|---|
| **Cognition** | Verbs (acts of cognition) | Nouns (institutional roles) |
| **Time** | Endpoints (when, threshold) | Process (during, middle) |
| **Success** | Orientation (relational quality) | Capability (faculty, achievement) |

Three angles, one underlying commitment: **the Quran is a theology of *decision* not *experience*.** It addresses a creature at thresholds, asking which way that creature has turned. It does not narrate an interior unfolding. It does not credit accumulated capability. It does not inventory possessed institutions. It asks one question, in one tense, at one point: *toward what are you oriented?*

## 9. Negative Space — The Refused Vocabulary

The following success-categories — standard in human cultures — are conspicuously *absent* from saved-class vocabulary:

- **No "leader"** (qāʾid, raʾīs). *Imām* means exemplar/record (36:12) or supplicated-for state (25:74), never office of salvation.
- **No "champion / hero"** — baṭal absent; ghālib only of God or of disbelievers being-overcome.
- **No "winner-of-contest."** The one *sabaqa* (sābiqūn) is a race *toward goodness*, defined by anonymity.
- **No "great man" / "genius"** — ʿabqarī (55:76) means a fine carpet.
- **No "wealthy"** as success — wealth is tested, not rewarded.
- **No "powerful"** as success — *quwwa* belongs to God and to physical structures.
- **No "famous"**, no "respected by people" — God's respect alone is named.
- **No "knowledgeable"** as saved class (see §6).
- **No "successful in this world"** — dunyā-success is the *opposite* pole (4:77, 29:64, 87:16-17).
- **No "victorious in battle"** as saved-trait — naṣr is divine-given.
- **No "ascetic"** — qawām principle (25:67) refuses both extravagance and stinginess.
- **No political office** — *malik / amīr / sulṭān* not among saved-classes. *Sulṭān* in the Quran means *evidentiary authority/proof*.

This is **refusal-by-design**, not absence-by-omission. The Quran has the words (or could easily coin them); they are left *outside* saved-class vocabulary. Same discipline as cognition's negative-space (no doubt-method, no hypothesis, no ijtihād-as-cognition) and time's (no during, no middle, no abstract zamān). One pattern: **the Quran refuses the worldly category-set in order to install its own.**

## 10. Verses for Direct Study

1. **[56:7-14]** — The cosmic tripartition: three kinds, the foremost-foremost = brought-near, the time-inversion of "many of the early, few of the latter."
2. **[4:69-70]** — The four favored classes; the *maʿa* (companionship-with) framing; the closing "that is bounty from God" qualifier.
3. **[90:11-18]** — *Iqtiḥām al-ʿaqaba* (storming the steep ascent): the ascent is *down* — freeing the neck, feeding the hungry orphan, mutual exhortation. Aṣḥāb al-maymana defined by descent into solidarity.
4. **[83:18-28]** — al-abrār in ʿIlliyyīn, witnessed by the muqarrabūn, drinking the sealed wine of Tasnīm. The elite-class portrait *and* the cross-link between abrār (Phase 1 elite) and muqarrabūn (56 cosmic top).
5. **[3:110]** — *kuntum khayra ummatin* — the best community defined entirely by *amr bi-l-maʿrūf, nahy ʿan al-munkar, īmān bi-llāh*. Three orientation-traits; no capability-trait. The collective parallel to the individual saved-classes.
6. **[49:13]** — *inna akramakum ʿinda llāhi atqākum*: the most-honored is the most God-shielded. The explicit refusal of every other ranking.
7. **[2:5 + 2:177]** — The two foundational equations: muhtadūn = muflihūn (2:5); abrār = ṣādiqūn = muttaqūn (2:177 collapse). The vertices that hold the whole architecture together.

## 11. Open Questions / Rabbit Holes

- **The muqarrabūn as a class with internal substructure.** They appear at 56:11, 83:21, 83:28, 4:172. At 83:21 they are *witnesses* of the abrār's record; at 83:28 they *drink directly* from Tasnīm while the abrār get its *mixture*. Do the muqarrabūn outrank the abrār, or are they identical? The doubled witnessing-and-drinking suggests two *roles* within proximity, not a rank gap.
- **The atqā superlative [49:13, 92:17].** If taqwā is shielding, is *atqā* simply more-shielded, or is it a qualitative threshold? [92:17] — *wa-sayujannabuhā al-atqā* — the most-God-shielded will be kept far from the Fire. A separate vocabulary of *most*-pious that may map onto the sābiqūn.
- **The ṣiddīqūn between prophet and martyr.** 4:69's middle two — ṣiddīqūn and shuhadāʾ — together name the verifying-and-witnessing function. Ṣiddīq is structurally between prophethood (revelation-bearing) and martyrdom (testimony-by-death). What is the *prose* of ṣiddīqiyya? The Quran names Mary (66:12) and Idrīs (19:56) and Yūsuf (12:46) as ṣiddīq — three different kinds of total verification. Worth a focused investigation.
- **Why are aṣḥāb al-yamīn given a sensory-paradise while sābiqūn get only proximity?** The 56:27-40 right-hand portrait is dense with sensory detail (lote-trees, banana-trees, shade, water, fruit, raised beds, virgins). The sābiqūn portrait (56:15-26) is cosmic-mystical — thrones, eternal youths, cups from a flowing spring, no idle talk. The reward-stratification mirrors the orientation-stratification: those who oriented sensorily-and-rightly get sensory paradise; those who oriented relationally get relation.
- **Khayr ummah vs aṣḥāb al-yamīn.** Is the "best community" of 3:110 a *collective* version of the aṣḥāb al-yamīn (the broad saved community) or of the sābiqūn (the elite vanguard)? The trio of traits (enjoin good, forbid wrong, believe in God) maps neither cleanly. It may be a third category: collective-historical saved-ness, distinct from individual-eschatological saved-ness.
- **Does ʿilm appear *anywhere* as a constitutive saved-trait?** A negative finding stands or falls on its exhaustiveness. A targeted scan of every "muflih / muhtad / ṣāliḥ / muttaqī / abrār" verse for any cognitive-root co-occurrence at the trait-defining position would harden (or break) §6.
- **The mukhliṣ/mukhlaṣ distinction.** The active participle (those-who-make-themselves-sincere) vs the passive participle (those-who-have-been-purified). 31 occurrences of the root. Is the mukhlaṣūn (passive) the *highest* class — those whose sincerity is divine-conferred, not self-achieved? At [37:40, 37:74, 37:128] the *mukhlaṣūn* are the exempt class spared from the eschatological gathering. May map onto sābiqūn.
