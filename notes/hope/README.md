# Hope and despair: investigation index

**The answer in one sentence.** In the Quran, hope is a turning toward God that may name what it wants, said as "perhaps my Lord will…"; grief, fear, "when?" and asking for years are never blamed, while despair is faulted only as a closed sentence about God ("He will never", "He does not know", "He has humiliated me"), and help is called near and ease said to come with hardship, but never with a date or a promise of the specific thing.

## Corrections log

Later files override earlier ones. Each line gives what was claimed, what holds, and where it was fixed.

**Phase 1 fixing Phase 0**
1. *Lā yarjūna liqāʾanā* describes deniers **4×** (10:7, 10:11, 10:15, 25:21), not 7× (hope words).
2. **57:23 is grief** (*taʾsaw*), not despair; Saheeh's "despair" misleads. The same fix was made in the regret study (despair words).
3. **12:110's object of despair is unstated** in the Arabic; "of their people" is interpretation (despair words).
4. **17:83's opposite state is turning away**, not exultation; only 11:11 names a cure among the four reflex passages (despair words).
5. **`hmm` is the verb *hamma*, "intend"**, not worry, except at 3:154 (despair words).
6. **`kshf`: 16 harm-removals; `njw`: 66 rescues** (ease and relief).
7. **65:7's *ʿusrin* is indefinite**; only 94:5-6 has definite *al-ʿusr* (ease and relief).

**Orchestrator addenda**
8. **Abraham did pray for a son** (37:100, answered 37:101). Waiters who pray: 9 of 11, not 8.
9. **12:34 was answered exactly as asked**; prison was Joseph's own stated preference.
10. **Job's return-with-more is shown** (21:84, 38:43), but the "better than it" sentences are never shown arriving.

**The spine's 8 corrections**
11. **7:129 and 12:83 are shown fulfilled** (7:136-137; 12:99-100).
12. **Worldly hope voiced without God is not faulted** (12:21, 28:9, 20:10, 28:29).
13. **The believers' frightened sentences are answered**, not rebuked (26:61, 2:249).
14. **The *an lan* construction about God's side occurs 7×**: 2 despair, 4 presumption, 1 Jonah.
15. ***Ḥattā idhā* forks by refused humbling, not depth**; 6:44 strikes at the top.
16. **The 18 grief items are 14 imperatives + 3 purpose clauses + 1 statement.**
17. **Jacob's *jamīʿan* means three sons** (the eldest stayed, 12:80): "both returned" becomes "all".
18. "No hope word is paired with a promise of the specific wish" holds for *promise* only; narrated fulfilment exists.

**The wish file's 6 corrections**
19. **Naming the thing is the more frequent pattern** of asking, not "ask for the good, not the form".
20. **25:74 presupposes spouses**, asking comfort *in* them; it is no model for a spouse not yet given.
21. **"Perhaps it is bad for you" is never applied to a withheld thing**; applying it is analogy.
22. **17:11 is silent on delay**; "slow prayers are haste" is interpretation.
23. **2:61 rebukes a named worldly ask (and grants it); 2:200 faults asking for this world alone.**
24. **Refusals also occur at 9:114 and 7:143, and 2:124 is narrowed**; Noah's is not the only one.
25. **Zechariah: no length of asking is given.**

**Resolved in the synthesis**
26. "No duration in years for any pain" → **no exact length is given**; Joseph's "several years" in prison (12:42) and Moses's "years" in Madyan (20:40) are vague spans told afterwards.
27. "Despair is faulted only when its object is God (10 of 16)" → **6 of the 10 have God's side as object; 4 are the object-less reflex.**
28. "Abraham's tidings came unasked" → holds **only for 11:69-73 and 51:24-30**.
