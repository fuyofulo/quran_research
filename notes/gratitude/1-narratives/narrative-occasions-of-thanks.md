# Occasions of thanks: when and for what the Quran asks someone to thank

## Headline finding

The Quran's occasions for thanks are mostly ordinary and repeated. Of the 69 *shkr* ayahs, 43 name a gift: 22 are food, sea, night and day, or the senses; 3 the eased law; 4 rescue; 3 a second chance; 11 a rare gift (a skill, a child, wisdom). Thanks is never asked for the gift alone. Beside it the text sets another response: *seek His bounty* (5 of 6 creation verses), *magnify God* (2:185), *fear God* (3:123), *mention God's name* (22:36). The eased law is thanked at 2:185 and 5:6. Forgiveness pairs with thanks by deed only at 2:52. In the four senses verses the order never changes.

---

## 1. The fourteen *laʿallakum tashkurūn*, in context (data-grounded)

Companion = the other purpose or command in the same ayah. Phase-0's five categories stand, with two corrections below.

| Ref | What was given or done | Companion in the same ayah |
|---|---|---|
| 2:52 | pardon after the calf (*ʿafawnā*) | none (2:53 next: *tahtadūn*) |
| 2:56 | revival after the thunderbolt (*baʿathnākum*) | none |
| 2:185 | the fast; illness/journey made up later; *ease, not hardship* | *complete the count*; *magnify God for guiding you* |
| 3:123 | Badr "while you were few" | *fear God* (imperative) |
| 5:6 | ablution; dry ablution | *purify you*; *complete His blessing* |
| 5:89 | oath expiation (feed ten, clothe, free, else fast three days) | *guard your oaths*; *thus God makes clear His verses* |
| 8:26 | few, oppressed, sheltered, supported, provided | *remember* (imperative) |
| 16:14 | the sea: fresh meat, ornaments, ships | *that you may seek of His bounty* |
| 16:78 | out of the womb knowing nothing; hearing, sight, hearts | none |
| 22:36 | camels subjected, as God's markers | *mention God's name*; *eat*; *feed the needy and the beggar* |
| 28:73 | night to rest, day to seek bounty | *rest*; *seek His bounty* |
| 30:46 | winds with good news, rain, ships | *taste His mercy*; *ships sail*; *seek His bounty* |
| 35:12 | two seas, meat, ornaments, ships | *seek His bounty* |
| 45:12 | the sea subjected for ships | *seek His bounty* |

**Counts.**
- *Seek His bounty* (*li-tabtaghū min faḍlih*) sits beside thanks in **5 of 6** creation ayahs: 16:14, 28:73, 30:46, 35:12, 45:12. The sixth, 22:36, gives instead *mention, eat, feed*.

**Corrections to Phase 0.**
1. **5:89 is not "ease".** It speaks of clarity, not ease: *thus God makes clear to you His verses*. It does have a graded ladder (the fast of three days only for whoever cannot find the means). So "law eased" is 2 firm cases (2:185, 5:6) and 1 "law made clear" (5:89).
2. **2:56 is a second life, not a pardon.** The verb is *baʿathnā*, "raised". Only 2:52 has a forgiveness verb (*ʿafā*). So forgiveness-to-thanks in the purpose clauses is 1, with 1 more second chance.
- Tally after correction: creation subjected 6, law 2 + clarity 1, forgiveness 1 + second life 1, rescue 2, senses 1.

**Companion purposes elsewhere.** No ayah holds two *laʿalla* verbs. The nearest neighbours:
- *tahtadūn* at 2:53 (beside 2:52 and 2:56) and 16:15 (beside 16:14);
- *tattaqūn* at 2:183 (two ayahs before 2:185): the fast opens in *taqwā* and closes in thanks;
- *tufliḥūn* at 5:90 (right after 5:89), 2:189 (after 2:185);
- *tuslimūn* at 16:81, three ayahs after 16:78.
- Sūrat al-Naḥl's list of signs closes each unit with a different verb: *think* (16:11), *reason* (16:12), *remember* (16:13), **thank** (16:14), *be guided* (16:15), *remember* (16:17), *listen* (16:65), *reason* (16:67), *think* (16:69), **thank** (16:78), *submit* (16:81). **[interpretive]** The same gifts can call for understanding, remembering, or thanking. Thanks is the response named for the gifts a person *uses*: sea, food, senses.

## 2. Ease in the law (data-grounded, then [interpretive])

> **2:185** …يُرِيدُ ٱللَّهُ بِكُمُ ٱلۡيُسۡرَ وَلَا يُرِيدُ بِكُمُ ٱلۡعُسۡرَ وَلِتُكۡمِلُواْ ٱلۡعِدَّةَ وَلِتُكَبِّرُواْ ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَىٰكُمۡ وَلَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ
> Saheeh: *Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship and [wants] for you to complete the period and to glorify Allah for that [to] which He has guided you; and perhaps you will be grateful.*
> Khattab: *…so that you may complete the prescribed period and proclaim the greatness of Allah for guiding you, and perhaps you will be grateful.*

- **The grammar.** 2:185 gives two *li-* purposes (*complete*, *magnify*) and then a separate *wa-laʿalla*, "perhaps". Thanks is the hope after one deed (completing the days, including the made-up ones) and one word (*takbīr*, from *kabbara*).
- **5:6 has the same shape**: *to purify you* and *to complete His blessing on you*, then *perhaps you will be grateful*. 16:81 has the same clause (*yutimmu niʿmatahu ʿalaykum*) but ends *tuslimūn*, "submit".
- **The same concession, two endings.** Dry ablution appears at 4:43, which ends *ʿafuww ghafūr* (Pardoning, Forgiving), and at 5:6, which ends in thanks. Necessity-eating (2:173, 5:3, 6:145, 16:115) closes in *ghafūr raḥīm*. Only 2:185 and 5:6 close a concession on thanks.
- **[interpretive]** When someone breaks the fast for illness, or uses earth when there is no water, the text does not call it a lapse to excuse. It names it as God's intent (*yurīdu*), and the right response is the one the rest of the verse names: complete what remains, magnify God (the act is named, no formula), and take the ease as a gift. The verse says nothing about how a person should *feel*, and names no formula for the magnifying.

## 3. Forgiveness as an occasion (data-grounded)

> **2:52** ثُمَّ عَفَوۡنَا عَنكُم مِّنۢ بَعۡدِ ذَٰلِكَ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تَشۡكُرُونَ
> Saheeh: *Then We forgave you after that so perhaps you would be grateful.* Khattab: *Even then We ˹still˺ forgave you so perhaps you would be grateful.*

- **The census pairs `gfr` + `shkr` in 5 ayahs**: 34:15, 35:30, 35:34, 42:23, 64:17. **None is a human forgiven then thanking.** In four, God is *Ghafūr* and *Shakūr/Shākir* together (35:30, 35:34, 42:23, 64:17). The fifth, 34:15, is *a good land and a Forgiving Lord* after the command *eat and thank Him*.
- **`ʿfw` + `shkr`: 1 ayah (2:52). `twb` + `shkr` in the same ayah: 1 (46:15, *I have repented* beside *enable me to thank*).** 2:54 (*He accepted your repentance*) sits two ayahs before 2:56.
- **So the claim "forgiveness is something to thank for" rests on one verse (2:52) as a purpose clause, plus the pairing of names in four.** It is real but narrow. No narrated person thanks after being forgiven.
- **[interpretive]** The pairing *Shakūr–Ghafūr* is God's side of the exchange. The human side is *repent* (46:15) or *the wrongdoers' second chance* (2:52, 2:56). For the next phase: forgiveness is named as an occasion once; thanks and forgiveness meet mostly as two things God does.

## 4. The body and senses (data-grounded)

| Ref | Before | List | Verb | Ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16:78 | out of the womb *knowing nothing* | سمع، أبصار، أفئدة | *jaʿala* | *laʿallakum tashkurūn* |
| 23:78 | none (lines before are on punishment) | same | *anshaʾa* | *qalīlan mā tashkurūn* |
| 32:9 | clay (32:7), semen (32:8), *breathed into him of His spirit* | same | *jaʿala* | *qalīlan…* |
| 67:23 | birds, provision, "who could be an army for you?" (67:19-21) | same | *anshaʾa* | *qalīlan…* |

- **The order is identical in all four**: hearing (singular), sights (plural), hearts (plural). **Nothing else is listed.** The same triad appears at 46:26, where it avails the deniers nothing, and at 17:36 (with *fuʾād*, singular) as what will be questioned.
- **Only one frame carries a promise (16:78); three carry a verdict (*little*).** 16:78 sets the gift against "knowing nothing". The other three set it against the spirit, clay, or provision.
- **23:78-79 and 67:23-24 are almost the same two ayahs**: the senses, then *He multiplied you on the earth, and to Him you will be gathered*.
- **[interpretive]** The senses are how every other gift is received; the text's verdict on thanks for them is "little".

| Rare gift (skill, child, wisdom, guidance): 12:38, 14:37, 21:80, 27:19, 27:40, 31:12, 34:13, 46:15, 7:144, 7:189, 16:121 | | 11 |
| No gift named (God's names, commands, vows) | | 26 |

- **[interpretive classification; counts exact for my sorting]** 22 of the 43 gift-naming ayahs (51%) are the daily or continuous ones; with the law (also recurring) 25 of 43. Rescue and second chances together are 7.
- **Food.** 2:172 and 16:114 are the only *eat… and thank* commands, both "if it is Him you worship"; both are followed by what is forbidden (2:173, 16:115) and end in *ghafūr raḥīm* for necessity. 36:35 and 56:70 argue from origin: *their hands did not produce it*; *is it you who sent it down?* 56:74 closes with *exalt the name of your Lord* (*sabbiḥ*), not a thanks word.
- **Cattle.** 22:36 is the only place where thanks has three set acts: *mention God's name*, *eat*, *feed the needy and the beggar*.
- **Night and day.** 25:62 offers a choice: *for whoever wishes to remember **or** wishes to thank* (*shukūrā*, a noun). 28:71-73 builds the case from the opposite (*if God made the night continuous… who would bring light?*), then gives rest and bounty-seeking as the use.
- **Clothing and shelter.** 16:80-81 lists homes, tents, wool, shade, garments; it ends *tuslimūn*, not thanks. Cattle at 16:5-8 end with no purpose clause. So the commonest items on the Quran's blessing list (16:3-18) are not all tied to *shkr*. Only the sea (16:14) and the senses (16:78) are.
- **16:18**: counting God's favours is impossible, and the closing is *Forgiving, Merciful*, not "ungrateful" (that is 14:34).

## 6. Rescue (data-grounded; the broken vows belong to the sibling file)

- **3:123**: Badr while *few*; the response is *fear God*, in the same clause as thanks. **8:26**: *few and oppressed… He sheltered you, supported you… provided you*; next, 8:27, *do not betray God and the Messenger or your trusts*. Both are told as memory to those already rescued.
- **The vows (6:63, 10:22, 7:189) promise thanks and are followed by a relapse** (6:64, 10:23, 7:190). No narrated person says "I thank You" after a rescue using `shkr`. After rescue, people say *al-ḥamdu li-llāh* (23:28, Noah).
- **[interpretive]** What is asked *after* rescue, in the two verses addressed to believers, is not a phrase: it is *taqwā* (3:123) and faithfulness (8:27). Gratitude is judged by what follows the rescue.

## For practice

- Thank for what repeats: water, food, sight, hearing, the day's end. The text puts more occasions there than in rare windfalls.
- When the law gives a concession, take it as intended (*yurīdu*), finish what remains, and magnify God (2:185).
- Use a gift the way its verse says: seek bounty with the sea and the day (16:14, 28:73), feed the needy with the animal (22:36).
- After being forgiven, the text gives one explicit verse (2:52) and the prayer *enable me to thank, and I repent* (46:15). Pair the two.
- After a rescue, add *fear God* (3:123) to the words.
