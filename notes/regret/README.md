# Regret: investigation index

**The answer in one sentence.** The Quran shows people regretting their own conduct in 56 verses (34 at the Judgement, 10 at death or as punishment arrives, 12 in this life). What they regret is never a pleasure missed or a hardship suffered. It is, in our reading, something owed and not given: the warning they dismissed, whom they followed, what they neglected toward God, wealth kept from the poor, a wrong against kin or against a people. The paths there run mostly through forgetting God and denying the warning. And the Quran never asks anyone to feel regret: it asks them to turn back, before the window closes.

## The list at a glance

56 verses of regret over one's own conduct (49 scenes), plus 3 the Quran treats as misdirected. Full tables: `2-cross-cutting/research-the-regret-catalog.md`.

| | What is regretted | Verses | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | The warning not heard, the Day denied | 12 | *If only we had been listening or reasoning* (67:10) |
| H | The whole conduct, nothing named | 10 | *We were wrongdoers* (7:5) |
| A | Whom one followed, befriended, obeyed | 6 | *I wish I had not taken that one as a friend* (25:28) |
| I | Wishing not to exist | 6 | *I wish that I were dust* (78:40) |
| D | What one neglected toward God and the Hour | 5 | *Over what I neglected in regard to God* (39:56) |
| E | Wealth trusted, misspent, or withheld from the poor | 5 | *Delay me… so I would give charity* (63:10) |
| B | Associating others with God | 4 | *I wish I had not associated with my Lord anyone* (18:42) |
| F | Crimes against kin | 4 | Cain (5:31); Joseph's brothers (12:91) |
| G | One rash act against others | 2 | Acting on an unchecked report (49:6) |
| J | Recognition at the record, no object | 2 | *This book leaves nothing small or great* (18:49) |
| K | Misdirected: a missed gain, another's fortune, the "if only" of fate | 3 | 4:73, 28:79, 3:156 |

Most-stated causes across the 49 scenes: denial 20, lost remembrance 13, world-orientation 9; 9 scenes state no cause.
