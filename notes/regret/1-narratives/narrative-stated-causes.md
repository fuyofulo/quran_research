# Narrative — the stated causes

## Headline finding

Across 19 passages where causes are itemised, the most frequent named cause is **orientation to this world** (love of the immediate, preference for it, deception by it, wealth/increase as distraction): 11 of 19. Denial of signs/Day/warner follows (8). Neglect of the poor or orphan is named in 4; neglect of prayer in 1 (74:43). **The regretful, speaking for themselves, name only actions** (74:43-47, 67:9-10, 39:56). Every inner-state word — *ghurūr, ḥubb, āthara, nisyān, amānī, rayb, hawā, rān* — is spoken by God, the narrator, or believers, never by the regretful about themselves. The only exception in kind is 67:10 (not hearing or reasoning). Caveat: the passage set was chosen because causes are itemised, so these counts describe the set, not the whole Quran.

## 2. Who diagnoses?

| Voice | Passages | Names |
|---|---|---|
| **Self** (regretful speak) | 74:43-47, 67:9-10, 39:56, 68:26-31 | Actions: prayer, poor, plunging, denial, mockery, "Allah sent nothing down"; capacity: no hearing/reasoning; 68:31 (outside my range) *kunnā ṭāghīn* |
| **Peer** (believers) | 57:14 | Four inner states |
| **Divine/narrator** | 69:33-34, 89:17-20, 39:59, 7:51, 45:34-35, 75:20, 76:27, 87:16, 79:37-38, 63:9, 18:28, 102, 83:14, 6:130 | Both actions and states |

*(Data-grounded)* Overlap: **denial** (74:46 = 69:33 = 39:59), and **the poor** (74:44 = 69:34 = 89:18). Divergence: the regretful never say *ghurūr, ḥubb, āthara*; 6:130 is the sharpest case — *they testify against themselves* (*shahidnā ʿalā anfusinā*) but *kāfirīn* is their testimony and *gharrat-hum al-ḥayāt* is the narrator's clause. *[Interpretive]* The dead report what they did; the one who sees from outside reports why. If the inner state were visible from inside, 67:10 (*if only we had been listening or reasoning*) might be the closest self-diagnosis, and it names a faculty switched off, not a feeling.

## 3. The poor — straight count

Neglect of poor/orphan: **4/19 passages** (74:44, 69:34, 89:17-18, 68:24). Prayer: **1/19** (74:43). Denial: 8/19. So the poor outnumber prayer 4:1 in this set, yet trail denial and world-orientation. In 74, 69 the poor-clause is paired with denial (74:46; 69:33 immediately precedes 69:34); in 89 it sits beside inheritance-eating and love of wealth (89:19-20). It never stands as the sole cause. 69:34 and 89:18 name *not urging* (*lā yaḥuḍḍu / lā taḥāḍḍūn*), which exceeds personal giving: it faults the social atmosphere too. 68:24 differs: a deliberate plan to exclude.

## 4. Explicit sequences

- **57:14** *(panel below)*: *fatantum anfusakum → tarabbaṣtum → irtabtum → gharrat-kum al-amānī → ḥattā jāʾa amr Allāh → gharra-kum bi-Allāh al-gharūr*. Linked by *wa-*, not causal particles. Mixed: self-tempting and waiting are acts, doubt and wishes are states. Two deceivers (wishes, then *al-gharūr*).
- **18:28** *(panel)*: heart made heedless (*aghfalnā*) → follows desire (*ittabaʿa hawāhu*) → affairs *furuṭ*. State → action → outcome; first mover is God's.
- **59:19** *(outside range; checked)*: forgot God (*nasū*) → He made them forget themselves (*fa-ansāhum anfusahum*). Action/state → divine inner consequence.
- **7:51 / 45:34**: they forgot the meeting → "We forget you *kamā*" (as). Mirror recompense.
- **83:14**: *rāna ʿalā qulūbihim mā kānū yaksibūn*: earning (action) → rust (state). Action → state.
- **79:37-39**: *ṭaghā → āthara → al-jaḥīm hiya al-maʾwā*. **75:20-21, 76:27**: love of the immediate (state) paired with *taḏarūn al-ākhira* (action). **63:9**: wealth/children → distraction from *dhikr* → *khāsirūn*.
- **18:35-36**: *ẓālim li-nafsihi* → *mā aẓunnu* (Hour not coming). State → belief.
- **68:17-28**: oath without *istithnāʾ* → secret plan against the poor → set out *qādirīn* → *innā laḍāllūn* → *law lā tusabbiḥūn*.
- **ḥattā-terminus** (data-grounded): 57:14 (*jāʾa amr Allāh*), 74:47 (*atānā al-yaqīn*), 102:2 (*zurtum al-maqābir*): the habit runs until an arrival.

Direction *(interpretive tally)*: state → action in 57:14 (partly), 18:28, 75-76, 63:9, 18:35, 102; action → state in 83:14, 59:19, 7:51/45:34 (recompense). **Both**; the text states no rule that one precedes the other. 83:14 is the only passage where deeds are said to *produce* a heart condition, which then keeps producing the same deeds.

## 5. Most common root cause

By my coding, **world-orientation** (11/19: 6:130, 7:51, 45:35, 75:20, 76:27, 87:16, 79:38, 89:20, 18:28, 63:9, 102:1) > **denial** (8) > **poor/orphan** (4) > **forgetting the meeting/remembrance** (4: 7:51, 45:34, 18:28, 63:9) > prayer (1). **Confidence: moderate.** The ranking holds under stricter coding (explicit words only), but gaps are small, categories overlap (7:51 and 45:35 carry both), and the set was selected. Notably, the world-orientation passages usually name the *other* term of a pair: *al-ākhira* left behind (75:21, 76:27, 87:17) or the meeting forgotten. The named cause is a swap, not a love of the world alone.

## Panels

**57:14** يُنَادُونَهُمۡ أَلَمۡ نَكُن مَّعَكُمۡۖ قَالُواْ بَلَىٰ وَلَٰكِنَّكُمۡ فَتَنتُمۡ أَنفُسَكُمۡ وَتَرَبَّصۡتُمۡ وَٱرۡتَبۡتُمۡ وَغَرَّتۡكُمُ ٱلۡأَمَانِيُّ حَتَّىٰ جَآءَ أَمۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَغَرَّكُم بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡغَرُورُ
Saheeh: "...you afflicted yourselves and awaited [misfortune for us] and doubted, and wishful thinking deluded you until the command of Allah came..." Khattab: "you chose to be tempted, eagerly awaited our demise, doubted, and were deluded by false hopes."

**7:51** ٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ دِينَهُمۡ لَهۡوࣰا وَلَعِبࣰا وَغَرَّتۡهُمُ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةُ ٱلدُّنۡيَاۚ فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَنسَىٰهُمۡ كَمَا نَسُواْ لِقَآءَ يَوۡمِهِمۡ هَٰذَا
Saheeh: took their religion as distraction and amusement... "today We will forget them just as they forgot the meeting of this Day." Khattab: "ignore them just as they ignored the coming of this Day."

**18:28** وَلَا تُطِعۡ مَنۡ أَغۡفَلۡنَا قَلۡبَهُۥ عَن ذِكۡرِنَا وَٱتَّبَعَ هَوَىٰهُ وَكَانَ أَمۡرُهُۥ فُرُطࣰا
Saheeh: "do not obey one whose heart We have made heedless of Our remembrance and who follows his desire and whose affair is ever [in] neglect." Khattab: "whose state is [total] loss." (*furuṭ* is contested between neglect/loss/excess.)

**74:44** وَلَمۡ نَكُ نُطۡعِمُ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينَ
Saheeh: "Nor did we used to feed the poor." Khattab: "nor did we feed the poor."

**83:14** كَلَّاۖ بَلۡۜ رَانَ عَلَىٰ قُلُوبِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ
Saheeh: "the stain has covered their hearts of that which they were earning." Khattab: "their hearts have been stained by all [the evil] they used to commit."

## Seams for the next stage
- 89:15-16: the text refutes a reading of one's own fortune; what it names is behaviour toward orphan and poor.
- *ḥattā* endings (57:14, 74:47, 102:2) suggest habits that run unchecked until an arrival.
- 68:29 vs 7:5: same confession, different timing (Phase-0).

## For introspection
1. In the last month, whom did I not feed or urge others to feed? Name the person or the excuse.
2. Which of the four in 57:14 do I do now: tempt myself, wait on others' failure, doubt, live on wishes?
3. What do I prefer to the next life, and do I use "the immediate" as an excuse (75:20)?
4. Do my wealth, children or accumulation (63:9, 102:1) cut into *dhikr*? Check the calendar, not the intention.
5. Could I state my own failings as actions (74:43-46) rather than as moods? Which one is on that list for me?
6. Where have I read a good or bad turn as proof of how God regards me (89:15-16)?
