> The Quran's vocabulary of cross-realm motion across 16 roots, 7 narrative loci, 4 cross-cutting structural studies, and 2 corpus-wide pre-cuts — 29 files in total. This is the master synthesis: what the Quran is *saying* about teleportation when all the analyses are read together.

## 1. Thesis

**The Quran does not describe teleportation; it describes the boundary-conditions under which divine action collapses the interval between two states.** Across 16 roots and seven canonical narratives, the corpus refuses every move that would convert miraculous transit into a *process* — a portal, a vehicle, a flight, a flash, an itinerary, a mechanism. Instead it builds a non-overlapping coordinate system of *axes* (vertical vs horizontal vs in-place), *modes* (one-way vertical removal vs round-trip horizontal accompaniment vs in-place time-displacement), *agents* (God direct, God-with-angel, God-via-knowledge, God-via-subjugation), and *speed-comparators* (gaze-return, eye-flicker, single-blast, *kun fa-yakūn*) — every one of them reporting *that* the interval has been collapsed and refusing to describe *how*. The believer is never given a trajectory; she is given a start-state, an end-state, and the grammatical connective *fa-* (and at-once). The lexical apartheid between vertical (`rfE`, `Erj`, `wfy`) and horizontal (`sry`, `Aty`, `nb*`, `Awy`) vocabulary is total — no root crosses the two extraordinary axes, no co-occurrence at the ayah level. Solomon's continent-spanning throne never uses `Erj`; Muhammad's Mecca-Aqṣā Isrāʾ never uses `rfE`. The fusion that devotional memory performs (Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj as one composite event) is hadith elaboration; the Quranic text refuses the lexical bridge.

Out of this discipline emerge five structural commitments that coordinate every teleportation finding:

1. **A vertical-horizontal lexical apartheid.** Vertical (`rfE`, `Erj`, `wfy` extracting upward) and horizontal (`sry`, `Aty`, `nb*`, `Awy`) vocabularies are partitioned at the ayah level. `rfE` and `Erj` co-occur in zero ayahs across the entire corpus, despite being the two core upward verbs. `sry` and `Erj` likewise share no ayah. The two registers attach to disjoint patient classes, follow disjoint directionality rules, and correlate with disjoint time-frames.
2. **A withdrawal → miracle → re-entry five-phase template.** WITHDRAWAL → ISOLATION → DIVINE INTERVENTION → MIRACLE → SOCIAL RE-ENTRY (with evidence). The Cave Sleepers, Maryam, Noah, Lot, Moses, and the hundred-year man all execute this template in different lexical palettes. Solomon is the structural inverse — the receiving-magnet. The template is also the architecture of Islamic ritual practice (`iʿtikāf`, `tahajjud`, `khalwa`, `hijra`) and of the eschaton (death → `barzakh` → Trumpet → resurrection → Gathering).
3. **A graduated instantaneity vocabulary.** Seven distinct lexical-grammatical formulas — *kun fa-yakūn*, *ka-lamḥ al-baṣar*, *qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk*, *nafkhatun wāḥida*, *sarīʿ al-ḥisāb*, *baghta*, *aw huwa aqrab* — never co-occur as if they were synonyms. They are seven slots in a coordinate system that maps creation, divine command, eschatological trigger, judgment-speed, sudden-arrival, narrative-teleportation, and self-overrunning hyperbole onto orthogonal axes. Every formula is a *comparator without a mechanism*.
4. **A knowledge-over-strength inversion.** The 27:38–40 throne-fetch sequence stages a syntactically parallel competition between `qawiyyun amīn` jinn-strength (delivers the throne *before you rise from your seat*) and `ʿindahu ʿilmun min al-kitāb` Book-knowledge (delivers it *before your gaze returns to you*). Knowledge wins by orders of magnitude. The grammatical-cadence comparator does the work; no doctrinal sentence is uttered. Speed is a function of epistemic access to divine inscription. The 34:14 jinn-blindness-to-Solomon's-death scene caps the hierarchy from below: even the strongest creature-transport agents are bounded by knowledge.
5. **A mechanism-silence as apophatic causation.** The Quran has elaborate vocabulary for *how fast* divine action is and zero vocabulary for *how* divine action works. No portal, no vehicle, no transit-medium, no intermediate states, no agent's interior, no return-trajectory. The withholding is the doctrine: to name a mechanism would name a created agent that does the work of God. The mechanism-slot is left blank because filling it would compromise the unitary divine causation.

The integrating logic: **teleportation in the Quran is not a description of movement but a grammar of sovereignty.** The miracle is not the speed; it is the boundary-crossing. Horizontal motion can be round-trip because no cosmic membrane has been crossed; vertical motion is one-way for prophets because the heaven-earth boundary, once crossed, requires divine sanction to recross. Solomon's throne arrives at gaze-speed across the horizontal plane because knowledge can collapse the interval; Idrīs and Jesus are raised by direct accusative without comitative because the destination is the divine presence itself. Every formula in the catalog is the Quran saying *the interval is closed* without ever saying *and here is what closes it*.

This thesis is not any single agent's claim. It is the **convergent finding** that emerges when 16 root-agents, 7 narrative-agents, 4 cross-cutting agents, and 2 structural-data agents all find pieces of the same architecture from independent angles. The reliability of this synthesis is structural: no individual file proves it, but every individual file fits it.

### 2.2 Five orthogonal partitions

**By spatial axis:**
- **Vertical** (heaven ↔ earth): `rfE`, `Erj`, `wfy` (up); `nzl`, `hbT` (down — outside the 16 but adjacent). Used of: angels, Spirit, souls, revelation, rain, and exactly *two* named human prophets, Idrīs and Jesus.
- **Horizontal** (point-to-point on earth): `sry`, `Aty`, `nb*`, `Awy`. Used of: prophets in motion on earth (Lot, Moses, Muhammad, Maryam), things-brought-to-Solomon, ordinary travel.
- **Position-change-without-travel** (orthogonal to both): `HDr`, `qrr` X (`istaqarra`), `jʿl`. Speed-comparators `Trf` and `lmH` belong here too — they name *that the arrival was instant* without naming a trajectory.

**By directionality:**
- **One-way vertical**: Idrīs (19:57), Jesus (3:55, 4:158). No return.
- **Bidirectional vertical**: angels, Spirit, souls (descend by permission, ascend in time-distorted days).
- **Round-trip horizontal**: Muhammad's Isrāʾ (back by morning), Maryam's withdrawal (returns with infant Jesus).
- **Escape horizontal**: Lot's family, Moses with Israelites (no return point — origin destroyed).
- **In-place time-displacement**: Cave Sleepers (309 years stationary), hundred-year man (100 years stationary), Solomon's death (interval until worm eats staff).

**By agent of subjugation:**
- **God direct, unmediated**: Idrīs's raising (1pl), Jesus's translation (1sg / 3sg with named agent).
- **God via angel / Spirit**: Maryam's annunciation, soul-extraction at death, Trumpet, descent of revelation.
- **God via knowledge** (the Book-knower of 27:40): Solomon's throne.
- **God via natural force** (`sxr`): Solomon's wind, Solomon's jinn, sun and moon, ships and rivers.
- **God via direct comitative-with-prophet** (`asrā bi-`): Lot's family, Moses' Israelites, Muhammad's Isrāʾ.

**By temporal regime:**
- **Earth-clock** (`laylan`, `qiṭʿan min al-layl`, *months*): all horizontal motion stays in earthly time.
- **God-clock** (1,000-year days, 50,000-year days): every cross-temporal-scale verse is pinned to vertical ascent. Crossing the vertical axis = crossing into a different temporal regime.
- **Suspended-clock** (the `labitha` formula): in-place time-displacement collapses subjective duration to "a day or part of a day" while objective duration may be 309 years.
- **Instantaneous** (`kun fa-yakūn`, `fa-iḏā hum`): no interval at all.

**By visibility:**
- **Withdrawn-private**: the divine act inside the seal (cave, ḥijāb, far place, ark, sea-corridor, fish-belly, womb, barzakh).
- **Public-court** (Solomon-only): the divine act in front of the council, with delegations brought in.
- **Trans-cosmic**: the destination is *ilayhi* (to Him); no description of arrival is possible.

## 3. The Five Master Patterns (Cross-Confirmed Across Multiple Agents)

Each pattern is **cross-confirmed**: it surfaced independently in multiple Phase-1 agents *before* any Phase-2 agent named it.

### 3.1 The vertical-horizontal lexical apartheid

The Quran's teleportation lexicon sorts onto two structurally distinct axes that **never blend at the verse level**. The decisive datum is co-occurrence: in the structural pre-cut (`teleportation-cooccurrence.md` §1, §8.2), `rfE` and `Erj` — the two core upward verbs — **co-occur in zero ayahs across the entire corpus**. `sry` (the horizontal Isrāʾ verb) likewise never shares an ayah with `Erj` (the vertical-ascent verb). The lexical apartheid is total.

Five fusion-candidate verses, each test-clean:

- **17:1 (Isrāʾ)** — verb count: `asrā` only. No `rfE`, no `Erj`, no `nzl`. Pure horizontal. Source and destination are both `masjid`.
- **53:1-18 (the second-vision pericope)** — Muhammad's verbs are visual (`raʾā` four times). The motion verbs all attach to *the descending figure* (Jibrīl): *thumma danā fa-tadallā* (53:8 — drew near and lowered himself); *raʾāhu nazlatan ukhrā* (53:13 — *applied to the figure*). **Muhammad does not ascend in 53.** The Erj root is absent from 53 entirely. The noun *miʿrāj* does not appear in the Quran at all (only the plural *maʿārij* at 70:3 of God's stairways and 43:33 of counterfactual silver stairs).
- **19:57 (Idrīs)** — *wa rafaʿnāhu makānan ʿaliyyā*. Three Arabic words. `rfE` only. No companion verbs of any kind.
- **3:55 (Jesus's three-operation stack)** — *innī mutawaffīka wa rāfiʿuka ilayya wa muṭahhiruka*. Three operations — `wfy`, `rfE`, `Thr` — all in the vertical register. **No horizontal verb appears.**
- **27:40 (Solomon's throne)** — *ana ātīka bihi qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk* + *raʾāhu mustaqirran ʿindahu*. The horizontal-bringing verb (`Aty`) collaborates with the position-change-arrival verb (`istaqarra`). **No vertical verb appears.**

Five test sites, five clean separations. The Quran has multiple opportunities to blend — the Isrāʾ, the three-verb Jesus passage, Solomon's continent-spanning throne — and refuses each one. **The lexical apartheid is a designed feature, not an accident.**

The implications cascade:
- **One-way transit clusters with vertical motion.** Idrīs and Jesus do not return. Muhammad and Maryam return. Lot and Moses exit but to a new earthly geography.
- **Time-distortion clusters with vertical motion.** The 1,000-year day at 32:5 and the 50,000-year day at 70:4 are pinned to *yaʿruju ilayh*. Horizontal motion preserves earthly time (`laylan`).
- **The bi-ʿabdihi comitative is HORIZONTAL-ONLY.** Idrīs is just *raised* (direct accusative pronoun, no comitative `bi-`). Jesus is *raised to Me* (direct accusative + directional preposition). All six Form IV `asrā` occurrences carry comitative `bi-` + person.
- **Angels are the only beings that freely cross both axes.** No human appears on more than one axis. The cross-axis specialism is reserved for angels.
- **Vertical motion is policed; horizontal motion is open.** Angels descend *bi-idhni rabbihim* (97:4); jinn who attempt vertical ascent without permission are repelled by missile-stars (37:7-10, 67:5, 72:8). The lowest heaven is *militarized*. There is no horizontal-prevention by cosmic defense.

### 3.2 The withdrawal → miracle five-phase template

The Quran's most extraordinary divine interventions follow a uniform five-phase scaffolding:

> **WITHDRAWAL → ISOLATION → DIVINE INTERVENTION → MIRACLE → SOCIAL RE-ENTRY (with evidence)**

The vocabulary differs by narrative — `awā`/`iʿtazala` in the Cave (18:10, 18:16), `intabadhat` for Maryam (19:16, 19:22), `asrā` for Lot and Moses (11:81, 26:52), `iṣnaʿ al-fulk` for Noah (11:37) — but the phase-sequence is invariant. Six confirmed instances:

| Instance | Withdrawal | Seal | Divine intervention | Re-entry evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cave Sleepers (18:9–26) | `iʿtazala` + `awā` | `al-kahf` (309 lunar yrs) | strike on the EARS (18:11); turn bodies (18:18) | the silver coin (`wariq`) — currency anachronism |
| Maryam (19:16–37) | `intabadhat` | `makānan qaṣiyyan` + `ḥijāb` | Spirit sent + `n-f-x` breath (21:91) | infant Jesus speaks (19:30) |
| Noah's Ark (11:36–48) | `iṣnaʿ al-fulk` + boarding | the Ark | open springs + rain (11:40, 54:11–12) | Ark on Jūdī (11:44) |
| Lot's family (11:81) | `fa-asri bi-ahlika` | night-march | rain stones + flip city (11:82) | the ruined cities (15:75) |
| Moses' Israelites (26:52) | `an asri bi-ʿibādī` | sea-corridor | strike sea (26:63); drown Pharaoh (28:40) | parted-sea memory; springs from rock |
| Hundred-year man (2:259) | `marra ʿalā qaryatin` | the destroyed town (100 yrs) | death + revival; food preserved, donkey reassembled | "We make YOU a sign for the people" |

The negative test is decisive. Run each narrative without the withdrawal phase:
- *Maryam without withdrawal*: the angel-encounter has too many witnesses to be the sealed event the narrative requires.
- *Cave Sleepers without withdrawal*: the youths are persecuted and killed (18:20).
- *Noah without ark*: the rain reaches all bodies equally; selectivity collapses.
- *Lot without night-departure*: the dawn-destruction (15:73) catches the unwithdrawn.
- *Moses without night-march*: no corridor.

**Withdrawal is not a trapping of the miracle; withdrawal is the operating condition of the miracle.** God's most extraordinary acts happen INSIDE a sealed space cleared of unauthorized witnesses.

The sealed-space typology is one of the Quran's richer micro-lexicons: cave (`al-kahf`, `al-ghār`), curtain (`ḥijāb`), distance (`makānan qaṣiyyan`), vessel (`al-fulk`), parted sea, alcove (`miḥrāb`), peak (`al-Ṭūr`), fish-belly (`fī baṭn al-ḥūt`), well (`ghayābat al-jubb`), womb (`qarār makīn`), prison (`al-sijn`), barzakh, and night (`al-layl`). Across all these, the operative property is the same: **the body is taken out of the social-witnessing field**.

The divine-intervention mode is **TARGETED MICRO-INTERVENTION**: God transforms LOCAL conditions, not the world. The Cave Sleepers' ears are struck (not eyes, breath, heart, or brain). Maryam receives the n-f-x breath (not a generic create-a-child). Noah gets two precise hydrological events (open springs + rain). The sea is struck at the staff-strike point, not parted globally.

The evidence-at-re-entry pattern converts the private divine act into a public sign. The silver coin's regnal stamp dates the displacement; infant Jesus speaks; the Ark sits on Jūdī as `āyatan li-l-nās` (25:37); the hundred-year man HIMSELF becomes scripture (`wa-li-najʿalaka āyatan li-l-nās`, 2:259).

Solomon is the structural inverse: STATIONARY MAGNET → COSMIC INSTRUMENTS → PUBLIC MIRACLE → DELEGATIONS BROUGHT IN → CONVERSION ON-SITE. The receiving-magnet template is what KINGSHIP looks like in the Quranic register; the withdrawal-recipient template is what PROPHECY looks like. Solomon is both king and prophet; the Quran assigns him the kingship-template for his miracles and the prophet-template only for his death (34:14, the postmortem deception).

**The template is also the architecture of Islamic ritual practice**: `iʿtikāf` (mosque-seclusion in Ramadan), `khalwa` (Sufi retreat), `hijra` (the foundational withdrawal — YEAR ZERO of Islamic civilization), `tahajjud` (night-vigil), `ṣawm` (fasting as bodily seal against ingestion), the Night of Power (`laylat al-qadr`). What the prophets did once, the believer does annually, monthly, nightly. **The template scales.**

It is also the architecture of the eschaton: humanity is taken from the world (death), placed in `barzakh` (the sealed space between worlds), divinely intervened upon (the trumpet-blast `nafkha`), miracle-event (resurrection), social re-entry (the Gathering, `ḥashr`). **The eschaton is the master template at maximum cardinality. The narrative withdrawals are rehearsals.**

### 3.3 The graduated instantaneity vocabulary

The Quran does not have *a* word for "instantaneous." It has **a graded, domain-partitioned grammar of speed-comparators**, each surgically calibrated to a specific category of divine action. Seven distinct lexical-grammatical formulas:

| Formula | Domain | Anchor verses |
|---|---|---|
| *kun fa-yakūn* (Be! and it is) | CREATION + DECREE | 2:117, 3:47, 3:59, 6:73, 16:40, 19:35, 36:82, 40:68 |
| *ka-lamḥ al-baṣar* (eye-flicker) | DIVINE COMMAND + ESCHATOLOGY (Hour) | 16:77, 54:50 |
| *qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk* (gaze-return) | TELEPORTATION (single narrative event) | 27:40; negated mirror at 14:43 |
| *nafkhatun / Ṣayhatun / zajratun wāḥida* (single-blast) | ESCHATOLOGICAL TRIGGER | 36:29, 36:49, 36:53, 38:15, 54:31, 69:13, 79:13 |
| *sarīʿ al-ḥisāb* (swift in reckoning) | DIVINE ATTRIBUTE (judgment) | 2:202, 3:19, 3:199, 5:4, 13:41, 14:51, 24:39, 40:17 |
| *baghta* (suddenly) | EPISTEMIC MODALITY (recipient's experience) | 6:31, 6:44, 6:47, 7:95, 7:187, 12:107, 21:40, 22:55, 26:202, 29:53, 39:55, 43:66, 47:18 |
| *aw huwa aqrab* (or even closer) | HYPERBOLIC CAPSTONE | 16:77 only |

Plus the syntactic instantaneity-operator *fa-iḏā hum + state* — the Quran's grammar of narrative jump-cut.

**No two formulas overlap in domain** (with one exception, see below). *Kun fa-yakūn* is never used for the Trumpet event. The single-blast formulas are never used for creation. The eye-flicker is never used as a divine attribute. *Sarīʿ al-ḥisāb* is never used for an actual event. *Baghta* is never used for the throne-event (the throne is fast but asked-for).

**The single exception**: **6:73 unifies *kun fa-yakūn* with the Trumpet** in a single ayah — *yawma yaqūlu kun fa-yakūn… yawma yunfaxu fī al-Ṣūr*. This is the only seam between the creation-formula and the eschatological-trigger in the corpus, and it is doctrinally load-bearing: the same *amr* that said "Be!" at creation will be enacted again at the Trumpet. The verse explicitly mentions *al-ghayb wa-l-shahāda* — the two epistemic categories that 27:40 / 34:14 turn on.

Reading the formulas together produces **a graduated speed-ladder**:

```
SLOWER ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► FASTER

[bird-flight]  →  [wind-flight]  →  [jinn-flight]  →  [gaze-return]  →  [eye-flicker]  →  [Be-and-it-is]
   hoopoe         Solomon's wind     ʿifrīt          Book-knower      the Hour          divine command
   (27:20)        (34:12)            (27:39)         (27:40)          (16:77)           (36:82)
                                                                      ↓
                                                                  + aw aqrab
                                                                  (16:77)
```

The bottom three rungs are the **Solomon-suite hierarchy**. The top three are **divine-action formulas**. Each rung is **explicitly faster than the one below** by Quranic juxtaposition. The progression is from comparator-grounded (the lower rungs measure speed against bodily clocks) to comparator-overrun (16:77's *aqrab* outpaces its own image) to comparator-refused (36:82's *kun fa-yakūn*, no comparator because no interval to measure).

Two cleanest grammatical micro-distinctions in the catalog:

**(a) The 27:40 ↔ 14:43 mirror.** *yartadda + ṭarf* is a two-verse exclusive collocation in the entire Quran. 27:40: a divine-knowledge event moves an object faster than the gaze-return can complete. 14:43: a divine-judgment event freezes the gaze so that no return ever happens. Same operator, opposite truth-value — the Quran using one lexical-grammatical mechanism to bracket the endpoints of a single conceptual axis: **what divine action does to perception**.

**(b) The 16:77 vs 54:50 *iḍāfa*-vs-*bi-* distinction.** *ka-lamḥ-i al-baṣar-i* (16:77 — definite construct chain) frames *lamḥ* as *a property of the eye-event*; *ka-lamḥ-in bi-l-baṣar-i* (54:50 — indefinite + bi-) frames *lamḥ* as *an act performed using vision*. The shift tracks descriptive-vs-performative grammar. **No English translation captures the distinction.** All four flatten both verses.

### 3.4 The knowledge-over-strength inversion

The 27:38–40 throne-fetch sequence stages a **speed-contest between strength-based and knowledge-based transport**, and uses the speed-comparator to encode the winner.

> **27:39** — *qāla ʿifrītun min al-jinn anā ātīka bihi qabla an taqūma min maqāmika wa-innī ʿalayhi la-qawiyyun amīn*
> "An ʿifrīt of the jinn said: 'I will bring it to you before you rise from your place, and I am for it strong, trustworthy.'"

> **27:40** — *qāla alladhī ʿindahu ʿilmun min al-kitāb anā ātīka bihi qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk. fa-lammā raʾāhu mustaqirran ʿindahu qāla hādhā min faḍli rabbī*
> "Said the one who had knowledge from the Book: 'I will bring it to you before your gaze returns to you.' Then when he saw it settled before him, he said: 'This is from the favor of my Lord…'"

The framing is *anā ātīka bihi qabla an + verb* in both cases. Same subject pronoun, same main verb, same prepositional structure. Two things differ:

| Element | ʿIfrīt | Book-knower |
|---|---|---|
| Comparator-clock | *taqūma min maqāmika* (rise from your place) | *yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk* (gaze returns to you) |
| Credentials | *qawiyyun amīn* (strong, trustworthy) — TWO | *ʿindahu ʿilmun min al-kitāb* (knowledge from the Book) — ONE |

The narrative does not stage a contest, does not name a winner. The next clause is *fa-lammā raʾāhu mustaqirran ʿindahu* — the throne is *already there, already at rest*. The Book-knower's claim has been adjudicated by the result.

What this encodes:
1. **Knowledge outpaces strength by orders of magnitude.** The ʿifrīt is *qawiyyun amīn*; the Book-knower has only *ʿilm min al-kitāb*. The strong-and-trustworthy is outranked.
2. **The hierarchy is encoded in the speed-comparator itself**, not in any propositional statement.
3. **The Book-knower is anonymous** — a category-of-one, no proper name. He is one node in a larger anonymity-of-key-figures pattern (al-Khiḍr at 18:65; the hundred-year man at 2:259; the Cave Sleepers; the *bashar sawiyy* form Gabriel takes for Maryam at 19:17). Anonymity preserves the function as the focus.
4. **Strength is bodily; knowledge is sub-bodily.** The ʿifrīt's comparator is a macro-bodily act (rising). The Book-knower's is the smallest unit of human perception (the eye-flicker).

The 34:14 death-of-Solomon scene caps the hierarchy from below. The jinn — who can build sanctuaries, dive for pearls, fly across continents, and fetch a Sheba throne in less than a man's standing-up — **cannot tell their own master is dead**. They keep working. A worm eats the staff; the body falls; *then* they know. The Quran's commentary: *law kānū yaʿlamūna al-ghayba mā labithū fī al-ʿadhābi al-muhīn* — "had they known the unseen, they would not have remained in humiliating servitude." This is a **refutation, in narrative, of the doctrine that jinn have access to *al-ghayb***.

**The pair 27:40 + 34:14 is the Quran's tightest argument for the knowledge-mediation theory.** Up: more knowledge yields faster transport. Down: less knowledge yields irreducible blindness even at high transport-power. **Transport-power is bounded by knowledge-access, both up and down.**

The corollary: the Quran's cosmology of divine causation is **fundamentally epistemic, not energetic**. Power, force, and strength are real categories — but they are not the categories that determine divine-action velocity. Knowledge is. *al-ghayb / al-shahāda* is the master variable.

### 3.5 The mechanism-silence as apophatic causation

Across every teleportation event in the Quran — Solomon's throne, Idrīs's raising, Jesus's translation, Maryam's provisions, the angels' arrivals, the Cave Sleepers' duration — **the trajectory is unstated**. The Quran reports juxtaposition, never traversal. There is no portal, no flight, no flash, no transit-haze. Two endpoints; nothing between them.

A catalog of cases:

- **Solomon's throne (27:40)**: *fa-lammā raʾāhu mustaqirran ʿindahu* — "when he saw it settled before him." The verb *istaqarra* is a state-verb; the throne is *at rest at B*. No transit-haze, no portal-effect, no flash, no dematerialization, no description of the trajectory between Sheba and Jerusalem.
- **Idrīs's raising (19:57)**: *wa rafaʿnāhu makānan ʿaliyyā*. Three Arabic words for the entire event. No description of the ascent, no vehicle, no companions, no duration, no description of the destination beyond *makānan ʿaliyyā*.
- **Jesus's raising (3:55, 4:158)**: *innī mutawaffīka wa rāfiʿuka ilayya* / *bal rafaʿahu Allāhu ilayh*. Same minimalism. The verbs operate; the destination is named (*ilayya / ilayh*); the mechanism is silent.
- **Maryam's provisions (3:37)**: *kullamā dakhala ʿalayhā Zakariyyā al-miḥrāba wajada ʿindahā rizqā*. Zachariah enters; provisions are *there*. No description of how they arrived. No angelic delivery is staged; no door is opened; no basket descends.
- **Angels visiting Abraham (11:69, 15:51, 51:25)**: the angels *arrive*, *enter*, *speak*. No description of their journey to Abraham's tent. No flight, no light, no materialization. They simply *are there*. This is the **unmarked arrival**, the Quran's signature mode for angelic appearance.
- **The Cave Sleepers' duration (18:25)**: 309 years, the verse states the duration. It does not describe what happened during those years. No dreams, no decay, no preservation-mechanism, no metabolic miracle.
- **The hundred-year man (2:259)**: *amātahu llāhu miʾata ʿāmin thumma baʿathahu* — two verbs naming start and end. Between them: a hundred years compressed to nothing.

In every single case, the Quran:
1. Names the start-state.
2. Names the end-state.
3. Names the speed (when speed matters) by comparator or simple temporal bound.
4. **Refuses to populate the mechanism slot.**

The gap between start and end is collapsed by **juxtaposition**. The Arabic *fa-* of immediate consequence carries the syntactic load. The connective is grammatical, not mechanical.

**This is the Quran's apophatic theology of causation.** To describe a mechanism would be to suggest that the mechanism has its own agency. If the throne arrives via a portal, the portal becomes the agent that moves the throne. If Idrīs is raised on a chariot, the chariot is the agent that lifts Idrīs. If angels arrive by flight, the flight-medium becomes the agent that brings the angels. **In each case, naming a mechanism would be naming a created agent that does the work of God.**

The mechanism-slot is left blank because filling it would compromise the theology of unitary divine causation. The withholding does three theological jobs simultaneously:
1. **Preserves divine sovereignty** (no created mechanism gets co-credit).
2. **Refuses the category-error** (process-vocabulary belongs to creaturely action).
3. **Marks the limit of human description** (mechanism falls on the *al-ghayb* side).

**The Quran has elaborate vocabulary for HOW FAST divine action is, and zero vocabulary for HOW divine action works. The withholding is the doctrine.**

## 4. The Hierarchy of Transport-Power

The Solomon suite encodes a hierarchy of transport-power across four agent-classes that appear within Solomon's own court. Reading this hierarchy through the agency-mechanism-knowledge frame produces the corpus's full speed-ladder:

| Rung | Agent | Comparator | Knowledge-class | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hoopoe (animal flight) | bird-physics, time-bound | Knows distant kingdoms by sight; reports back; no access to *al-ghayb* | 27:20–28 |
| 2 | Wind (cosmic-scale natural force) | 1 month per half-day | No knowledge attribute; pure delegated power | 34:12 |
| 3 | Jinn / ʿifrīt (spirits) | "before you rise from your seat" | Strong, trustworthy; access to *al-shahāda* but *not al-ghayb* | 27:39, 34:14 |
| 4 | Knowledge-from-the-Book (Book-knower) | "before your gaze returns" | Partial knowledge of divine inscription; *ghayb*-adjacent | 27:40 |
| 5 | Direct divine command (lamḥ family) | *ka-lamḥ al-baṣar* | The Hour, the divine command — same speed-class as Book-knower but uncomparated by any creature-substrate | 16:77, 54:50 |
| 6 | *Kun fa-yakūn* | no comparator | Closed grammatical loop; no interval at all | 36:82, 2:117 |

The hierarchy reads from bottom to top as *increasing access to divine knowledge*. The bottom rung (hoopoe) operates by *animal observation*; the top rung (*kun fa-yakūn*) operates by *closed grammatical loop with no medium*.

**Speed is a function of epistemic access to divine inscription.** The wind is fast (30× ordinary travel) but bounded to the calendar; the jinn are fast but bounded to bodily volition; the Book-knower is fast at the cadence of an eye-flicker; the Hour outpaces the eye-flicker itself; *kun fa-yakūn* abandons the comparator entirely.

**The Book-knower at 27:40 is the highest creaturely rung — the boundary between created-agent transport and divine-direct operation.** Above him, only direct divine action.

This hierarchy is the Quran's account of how divine action propagates through created agents. It propagates *via knowledge*. The transport-power of any agent is bounded by the agent's epistemic access. **The Quran's cosmology of divine causation is fundamentally epistemic, not energetic.**

## 5. The Eschatological & Cosmological Architecture

### 5.1 Heaven-earth as two-way highway with traffic rules

The Quran's cosmology of cross-realm motion is a *governed* architecture. The vertical stack:

- **The Throne** (`al-ʿarsh`) at the apex — God *istawā ʿalā al-ʿarsh* (7:54, 10:3). The Throne is *carried* by angels (40:7, 69:17 — eight on the Day) and is *fawqahum* (above them).
- **The Lote-Tree of the Boundary** (`sidrat al-muntahā`, 53:14) — the limit of created creatures' upward access.
- **The seven heavens** (`sabʿ samāwāt`, 67:3, 71:15, 78:12) stacked in layers (`ṭibāqā`, 67:3). The lowest heaven (`al-samāʾ al-dunyā`) is the **militarized perimeter** — its lamps are weapons (*jaʿalnāhā rujūman lil-shayāṭīn*, 67:5).
- **The earth** — the horizontal plane.
- **Hell** — below in the baseline; *brought up* at the eschaton via `aḥḍara` (the same verb-class that is used eschatologically of deeds being made-present).

**The vertical axis is policed; the horizontal is open.** Angels descend *bi-idhni rabbihim* — by their Lord's permission (97:4). Jinn who attempt vertical ascent without permission are repelled by missile-stars (37:7-10, 67:5, 72:8). The lowest heaven is militarized. There is no Quranic case of someone being prevented from horizontal motion by a cosmic defense; the only horizontal-prevention is *human pursuit* (Pharaoh chasing Moses).

This is consistent with Idrīs's and Jesus's silent translation. They pass through the policed vertical axis without resistance — no missile-fire, no permission-clause, no gatekeeping language. **Any human vertical motion in the Quran requires either (a) divine direct agency unmediated, or (b) angelic mediation by permission.**

### 5.2 Souls ascend at death; the dead are raised at the eschaton

The Quran's grammar of soul-mobility is partitioned across two roots:

- **wfy** (`tawaffā`) — soul-extraction at death and during sleep. Form V causative: "to take fully, to take in completion." The soul is taken *upward*. The verb is used of: the angel of death (32:11); Allah taking souls in death and sleep (39:42); Allah's *mutawaffīka* of Jesus (3:55). This is the **vertical-up** verb of soul.
- **bEv** — raising up at the eschaton. *Yawm al-baʿth*. The same verb names: prophetic dispatch, awakening from ordinary sleep (6:60), the Cave Sleepers' awakening, the hundred-year man's revival, and the universal resurrection. The Quranic grammar treats all four as *the same act*.

The **Cave-awakening is, lexically, an eschaton in miniature**. The same verb that ends the 309-year sleep also ends the universal sleep of barzakh.

The two roots meet at the bridge: at death the soul is *taken* (`tawaffā`) and at the eschaton the body is *raised* (`baʿath`). Between them lies barzakh — the universal sealed space.

### 5.3 The Throne carried by angels (cosmic infrastructure)

The verb `Hml` (carry) is applied to the Throne (40:7, 69:17 — *yaḥmilu ʿarsh rabbika fawqahum yawmaʾidhin thamāniya*). This makes the Throne *carryable*, parallel to Solomon's throne in 27:38 (*bi-ʿarshihā*). The cosmic architecture has *two* thrones in the same lexical category — one divine, one royal. **God's Throne is the original of which Solomon's is the type. The teleportation of Solomon's throne is a creaturely echo of the angelic Throne-carrying.**

### 5.4 The Hour at lamḥ-al-baṣar speed; the Trumpet as single blast

The Hour (`al-sāʿa`) is the threshold-instant at which world-time terminates. Its speed is *ka-lamḥ al-baṣar aw huwa aqrab* (16:77) — the Quran's only hyperbolic capstone, the only formula that explicitly outpaces its own image.

The Trumpet (`al-Ṣūr`, *nfx*) is the cinematic-narrative trigger. The agent is unstated (Israfīl is not in the Quran). The Trumpet and the Hour **never share an ayah** (verified in the time investigation). The Trumpet is the *witnessed apparatus*; the Hour is the *named-but-hidden trigger*. The Quran refuses to splice them into the same syntactic clause.

The Trumpet's blast is *nafkhatun wāḥida* — a single blast — paired with *fa-iḏā hum + state*. The connective is grammatical: a single blast, then immediately the universal state-change. The verb of in-blowing (`n-f-x`) is the same verb used for Adam's creation (15:29), Maryam's conception (21:91), and the Trumpet at the eschaton (39:68). **The Trumpet at the cosmic scale is the ear-strike at the individual scale** (cf. the Cave Sleepers, where God *struck upon their ears* to seal the wake-up channel). One auditory event, universal effect.

### 5.5 The convergent destination: *ilayhi*

Both vertical-up registers — prophet-translation (3:55, 4:158) and angelic-ascent (70:4) — terminate at *ilayhi*. The Quran's two upward registers converge on the *same terminus*, the same preposition, the same directionality. **The destination is not a place but a person**: God Himself.

This is one of the cleanest patterns in the corpus. **Vertical translation in the Quran is "going to a person" (God), not "going to a place."** The horizontal corollary is geographic (al-Aqṣā, *makānan qaṣiyyan*); the vertical is relational (*ilayya / ilayh*).

## 6. The Grammar of Divine Action

### 6.1 Knowledge as medium

The 27:38–40 sequence establishes that divine action through created agents is *knowledge-mediated*. The fastest mode of cosmic operation is not strength but Book-knowledge. The strong-and-trustworthy ʿifrīt is outranked by the *ʿindahu ʿilmun min al-kitāb*. The credentialing-asymmetry (two credentials yielding slower transport, one credential of higher class yielding faster transport) encodes a theological principle: more is not better; *kind* matters more than *quantity*.

**Knowledge of the BOOK specifically — not knowledge in general.** The text is precise: *ʿilmun min al-kitāb*. This is not generic intellectual knowledge; it is partial-knowledge from a divine register. The fastest mode of cosmic operation is access (even partial) to divine inscription.

### 6.2 Comitative *bi-* as accompaniment-marker (horizontal-only)

Every Form-IV occurrence of `asrā` carries a comitative *bi-* prepositional phrase: God moves *with* the human, not *of* or *for* the human:

| Verse | Subject | Comitative phrase | Carried party |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17:1 | God | *bi-ʿabdihi* (with His servant) | Muhammad |
| 11:81, 15:65 | God (imperative to Lot) | *bi-ahlika* | Lot's family |
| 20:77, 26:52, 44:23 | God (imperative to Moses) | *bi-ʿibādī* | Moses' Israelites |

Six occurrences, six comitatives, zero exceptions. The grammatical force of the comitative *bi-* in Arabic is **accompaniment with carrying**. It is not "for" (which would be *li-*), not "to" (which would be *ilā*), not "from" (which would be *min*). The comitative *bi-* makes the human the *attached-object* of divine motion.

**This pattern appears nowhere in the vertical register.** Idrīs is just *raised* (`rafaʿnāhu` — direct accusative pronoun, no comitative). Jesus is *raised to Me* (`rāfiʿuka ilayya` — direct accusative + directional preposition). The vertical-translation verbs do not use the comitative.

The grammatical contrast:
- **Horizontal extraordinary**: God moves *WITH* the human (comitative *bi-*). Foregrounds the accompaniment-relation.
- **Vertical extraordinary**: God *raises* the human (direct accusative). Foregrounds the patient-relation.

The comitative encodes a *protective accompaniment*; the carried party is shielded by being-with-God. In every `asrā bi-` event the carried party survives. The vertical register does not need this protection because *the destination is the divine presence itself*. **The comitative is a continuing-relation construction; the direct accusative + *ilayya* is a destination-as-terminus construction.**

### 6.3 Apophatic causation — mechanism is silenced

Every teleportation event is reported as start-state + end-state + speed-comparator + carried party, with no description of the mechanism that closes the interval. The Quran has *zero vocabulary* for:
- Portal, gateway, threshold, or aperture between realms.
- Dematerialization, rematerialization, or any state-change mechanism.
- Vehicle, mount, or conveyance for divine-action events.
- Transit-medium (light, fire, water, air) used for cross-realm motion.
- Energy, force, or substance that effects the motion.
- Intermediate states between start and end.
- Agent's own subjective experience of the transit.

The silence is enforced by grammatical partition. **The Quran's apophatic theology is a vocabulary-architecture, not just a thematic stance.**

### 6.4 The "one with knowledge from the Book" as the anonymous category

The Book-knower at 27:40 is named only as *al-ladhī ʿindahu ʿilmun min al-kitāb*. The Quran does not give a personal name. This is one node in a recurring **anonymity-of-key-figures pattern**:

| Anonymous figure | Verse | Function |
|---|---|---|
| The one with knowledge from the Book | 27:40 | Brings Solomon's throne |
| The servant Moses meets (al-Khiḍr) | 18:65 | Possesses *ʿilm min ladunnā*; demonstrates apparent injustices |
| The man who passes by the destroyed town | 2:259 | Doubts resurrection; is made-to-die 100 years |
| The Sleepers of the Cave | 18:9–26 | Sleep 309 years; their names not given (the text rebukes those who guess: 18:22) |
| The "perfect human" form Gabriel takes for Maryam | 19:17 | *Bashar sawiyy* — well-formed human; brings the annunciation |

Tradition rushes to name them; the Quran resists. **Anonymity preserves the function as the focus.** If the Book-knower had a name, the reader would attach the speed-claim to the agent's biography. With no name, the speed-claim attaches to the *category* — knowledge-from-the-Book. The *type* is more important than the *character*.

## 7. The Maryam Loci and the Female-Teleportation Question

Maryam is the densest miracle-locus in the Quran. Five-plus distinct supernatural events occur on her body and in her space:

1. **The miḥrāb provisions (3:37)** — every time Zachariah enters, food is *there*. Mechanism unstated.
2. **The annunciation (19:17)** — the Spirit takes the form of *bashar sawiyy* (well-formed human). The form-shifting itself is a teleportation event.
3. **The conception (19:20, 21:91)** — *fa-nafakhnā fīhā min rūḥinā*. The same `n-f-x` verb used for Adam's creation and the eschatological Trumpet.
4. **The withdrawal (19:22)** — *intabadhat bihi makānan qaṣiyyan*. **Maryam is the only person in the Quran who self-ejects** — the Form-VIII reflexive of `nb*` carries the violence of self-throwing (translators systematically obscure this; "withdrew" in English loses the ejective force of *intabadhat*).
5. **The infant Jesus's speech (19:30)** — testimony in newborn body.
6. **The shelter on the rabwa (23:50)** — *wa āwaynāhumā ilā rabwatin dhāti qarārin wa-maʿīn*. Divine sheltering with food and water.

Maryam runs the entire withdrawal-miracle template across her own body and biography. She withdraws (`intabadhat`); she is sealed (`makānan qaṣiyyan` + `ḥijāb`); she is divinely intervened upon (the Spirit, the n-f-x breath); the miracle occurs (virginal conception); she returns (19:27 `taḥmiluhu`) carrying the infant who speaks. **She is the one figure in the Quran who instantiates the template at maximum density on a single body.**

The implicit female-prophet question: the Quran does not explicitly name Maryam a prophet. But she receives the same divine-side knowledge-mediation pattern that prophets receive. The Spirit (`al-Rūḥ`) appears to her in the same form-shifting that elsewhere indicates angelic communication. She is divinely sheltered (`āwaynāhumā` — the Anṣār-of-the-prophets verb). She is named explicitly as a sign (*wa-jaʿalnāhā wa-ibnahā āyatan li-l-ʿālamīn*, 21:91). The structural typology aligns her with the prophetic-template at every node. The text does not call her a *nabī* (the masculine form would be the marked case); the Quran is silent on what to call a woman who runs the prophetic template.

## 8. The Duration-Collapse Phenomenology

The "yawman aw baʿḍa yawm" formula is the Quran's signature phenomenology of suspended time. It appears at three sites:

- **2:259** — the man revived after 100 years says it.
- **18:19** — the Cave Sleepers say it.
- **23:113** — the resurrected at judgment say it.

Three scenarios — death-revival, cave-sleep-revival, eschatological resurrection — produce *the same exact phrase*. The Quran is naming a phenomenology: **whoever wakes from a long suspension reports the same drastic underestimation.** The Cave-passage and the eschaton-passage are linked by this lexical fingerprint.

Compare also:
- **10:45 / 46:35** — *kaʾan lam yalbathū illā sāʿatan min nahār* ("as if they had not tarried but an hour of a day").
- **30:55** — the criminals swear *mā labithū ghayra sāʿah*; the believers correct in 30:56.
- **79:46** — they will see the Hour as *ʿashiyyatan aw ḍuḥāhā* ("an evening or its morning").

Every instance: vast objective duration → minimal subjective duration → epistemic surrender (*Allāh aʿlamu bimā labithtum*). The Cave is the *spatially confined* instance; the eschaton is the *cosmically scaled* instance. **Same phenomenology, different scale.**

The 309 years of the Cave Sleepers is itself a precision-encoded calendrical conversion: 300 solar years × (365.2422/354.367) ≈ 309.207 lunar years. The "+9" of [18:25] is the exact lunar-conversion correction. The Quran *gives* the precise number and *withdraws* its own claim on it within a single ayah: *quli llāhu aʿlamu bimā labithū* (18:26). **God's count differs from human count even when the human count is calendrically precise.**

The duration-collapse formula is the Quran's most explicit concession to subjective time inside its broader objective-time architecture. It is also the most explicit hint that the eschatological *experience* of time will be radically discontinuous with the dunyā experience. The Cave is rehearsal.

## 10. Connection to the Time Investigation

The teleportation investigation **confirms and extends** the time investigation's structural findings. Five parallel patterns recur:

### 10.1 Programmatic indeterminacy around large-scale phenomena

The time investigation found that the Quran refuses to date the year, century, or millennium; the Hour's date is *ʿinda rabbī ʿilmuhā*; calculated indeterminacy is the Quran's general signature for time. **The teleportation investigation finds the same**: the trajectory of every teleportation event is unstated; the mechanism of every cross-realm transit is reserved; the destination of vertical translation (Idrīs's *makānan ʿaliyyā*) is named only by adjective; the Cave's location is unspecified; the Book-knower's identity is anonymized. **Indeterminacy operates as the structural signature on both axes.**

### 10.2 Lexical apartheid between divine and creature vocabulary

The time investigation found 8 roots that are never predicated of God; divine eternity is built from negation, bracketing, and contrastive remaining. **The teleportation investigation finds the same architecture in two registers**: (a) the vertical-vs-horizontal apartheid (`rfE` and `Erj` in zero co-occurring ayahs; `sry` never with vertical verbs); (b) the comitative-vs-accusative split between horizontal `asrā bi-` (God-with-prophet) and vertical `rafaʿa` + accusative + `ilayhi` (God-acts-on-prophet). **The Quran systematically partitions vocabulary-spaces by ontological domain.**

### 10.3 Binary grammar (no middle, no during for time; no trajectory for transport)

The time investigation found that the Quran lexicalises endpoints (*qabl/baʿd*, *awwal/ākhir*) with formidable density but does not lexicalise middle, during, throughout, in-process, halfway. **The teleportation investigation finds the same grammar at the spatial level**: the Quran reports endpoints (start, destination) with maximal precision and refuses to populate the trajectory-slot. There is no spatial *during*; there is only *fa-* (and at-once). The withdrawal-template's two-mode split (withdrawn-private / public-social) is the spatial analog of the cyclical/linear binary in time.

### 10.4 Calculated minimalism as a structural feature

The time investigation noted the *fanā* hapax (the entire perishing-doctrine hangs on one occurrence) and the named-day catalog's 22 hapax coinages. **The teleportation investigation finds the same minimalism**: Idrīs's raising is *three Arabic words*; Jesus's translation is fewer than thirty Arabic words across the entire corpus; the Book-knower is named only by function; the Trumpet's blower is unnamed. **The Quran's signature calculated minimalism applied to its most metaphysically extreme claims.**

### 10.5 The *al-ghayb / al-shahāda* partition as master variable

The time investigation found that the reckoning of the eschaton is bounded by *al-ghayb* — *ʿinda rabbī ʿilmuhā*. **The teleportation investigation finds that transport-power is bounded by *al-ghayb*-access**: the Hour is unknown because it is *al-ghayb*; the Book-knower's transport is fast because he has *ʿilm min al-kitāb* (which approaches *al-ghayb* knowledge); the jinn's failure to detect Solomon's death is because they lack *al-ghayb*-access. **Both investigations converge on knowledge-of-the-unseen as the determining variable.**

The cross-investigation finding: **the Quran's apophatic theology operates as a vocabulary-architecture across multiple domains**. Time-vocabulary, transport-vocabulary, cosmological-vocabulary, eschatological-vocabulary all share the same structural moves: partition by ontological domain, withhold the agent in the mechanism-slot, mark the limit of human description, allow comparators (speed, duration) but refuse mechanisms (process, medium). **The teleportation investigation extends and confirms the time investigation. The two together describe the Quran's signature method of theological grammar.**

### 12.1 Smaller root-agent count, larger narrative-agent count

The time investigation deployed 22 root-agents because time is a root-anchored concept (the lexicon is the topic). Teleportation is a thematic concept — a *function* recruited from many lexical registers. Accordingly: **16 root-agents** (smaller because the topic is less root-anchored) plus a new agent-class — **7 narrative-agents** that read canonical passages as integrated wholes.

The Maryam loci required treating the surah-19 mobility-cluster as one event with multiple aspects.

**Narrative agents are now baseline for thematic investigations.** Future investigations of love, justice, mercy, war, fasting, charity, prophecy, etc., should follow the same pattern: root-agents *and* narrative-agents in Phase 1.

## Closing — Arrival Without Journey

The Quran's grammar of cross-realm motion has only two endpoints: the start-state and the end-state. There is no third grammatical category sweeping continuously between them. There is only the moment in which the two endpoints meet — and that moment is collapsed by *fa-*, the connective of immediate consequence.

The throne is *not at Solomon's court*; then it is *at Solomon's court at rest*. Idrīs is *here*; then he is *at a lofty place*. Jesus is *being persecuted*; then he is *raised to Me*. Maryam is *with her people*; then she is *in a far place*; then she is *back, carrying the infant who speaks*. Muhammad is *in Mecca*; then he is *in Aqṣā*; then he is *back by morning*. The Cave Sleepers are *in the cave at year zero*; then they are *in the cave at year 309*. The hundred-year man is *passing by the town*; then he is *dead a hundred years*; then he is *alive, with food unspoiled*.

Between every pair of endpoints, the Quran's narrative grammar is silent. There is no portal, no flight, no flash, no transit-haze. The mechanism does not appear in the text because it would compromise the doctrine. The agent of every teleportation event is God; no created mechanism gets co-credit. The medium is knowledge; the grammar is comitative for horizontal accompaniment and direct accusative for vertical translation; the speed is reported by comparator; the trajectory is reserved.

What remains, when every mechanism has been refused, is the one thing the Quran will commit to: **the interval is closed**. The throne arrives. The prophet is raised. The body is sealed. The dead are awakened. The Hour is *baghta*. The blast is *wāḥida*. The command is *kun*; the existence is *yakūn*; nothing is between them but a *fa-*.

The Quran's grammar of teleportation tells the believer that no boundary is fixed except the one God maintains, no distance is unbridgeable except the distance God preserves, no duration is permanent except the duration God enforces, no mechanism is necessary except the speech that closes the loop. **The miracle is not the speed. The miracle is that the boundary is sovereign — and the only sovereign over the boundary is the One whose word is its closing.**
