# Cross-Cutting: The Withdrawal-Miracle Template

**Phase-2 cross-cutting research.** Inputs: the Phase-1 root deep-reads on `Awy`, `nb*`, `Hml`; the Phase-1 narrative deep-reads on the Cave Sleepers, Maryam's mobility, the resurrection-demonstrations, the angels-and-jinn cosmography, and the Solomon suite. The question this file answers: **does the Quran have a single master template for divine intervention, expressed in different surahs through different lexical palettes but the same five-phase scaffolding?** The answer is yes; this file maps the template, surveys its instances, isolates the inverse case (Solomon), tests its edges (Yūnus, Muḥammad-in-Hira), and shows how the template is INSTITUTIONALIZED in Islamic ritual life.

---

## 1. Headline — the Quran's master template

The Quran's most extraordinary divine interventions follow a uniform five-phase scaffolding:

> **WITHDRAWAL → ISOLATION → DIVINE INTERVENTION → MIRACLE → SOCIAL RE-ENTRY (with evidence)**

The vocabulary differs by narrative — `awā`/`iʿtazala` in the Cave (18:10, 18:16), `intabadhat` for Maryam (19:16, 19:22), `asrā` for Noah and Lot and Moses (11:81, 26:52), `kān fī l-fulk` for Noah (11:42), `nājaynāhum` for the Israelites (26:65) — but the PHASE-SEQUENCE is invariant. The protagonist self-removes; a sealed space contains the body; God acts on the body inside the seal; the supernatural event occurs without external witnesses; the protagonist returns to the social field carrying community-visible evidence.

The template is what `concept-Hml.md` and `concept-Awy.md` have been pointing to in their cross-cutting sections. It is also what `narrative-cave-sleepers.md` calls "the Cave is the Hour, scaled down and placed inside history as evidence" — the same structural argument, generalized: **every withdrawal-miracle narrative is the Quran's eschatological architecture, scaled down and placed inside history as a sign**. The Hour itself follows the template at cosmic scale: humanity is taken from the world (death), placed in `barzakh` (the sealed space between worlds), divinely intervened upon (the trumpet-blast `nafkha`), miracle-event (resurrection), social re-entry (the Gathering, `ḥashr`). The eschaton is the master template at maximum cardinality. The narrative withdrawals are rehearsals.

This file tabulates instances, defends the necessity-test (withdrawal is required, not incidental), catalogs the sealed-space types, characterizes the divine-intervention mode (always TARGETED MICRO-INTERVENTION, never global), shows the evidence-at-re-entry pattern, distinguishes Solomon's INVERSE template (receiving-magnet), tests the edge cases, and connects the pattern to the time-investigation finding that night and dawn are also institutionally bracketed.

The list compresses to a single observation: **wherever the Quran narrates a major divine intervention on a body or a community, the withdrawal-isolation phase is present**. Where the phase is absent (Solomon, see §7), the narrative is inverse-templated.

## 3. Withdrawal as trigger — the necessity test

Is withdrawal a NECESSARY precondition for the miracle, or merely a frequent stylistic convention? The data argues necessity.

**The negative test.** Run each narrative without the withdrawal phase and ask whether the miracle remains coherent.

- *Maryam without withdrawal.* If Maryam stays in her family compound, the angelic visitation is publicly witnessed; the conception happens in front of relatives; the virginity-claim is unverifiable because the angel-encounter has too many witnesses to be the sealed event the narrative requires. The `ḥijāb` (19:17) and the `intabadhat` (19:22) are not decorative — they construct the verifiable-by-its-isolation epistemic shape of the miracle. The `bashar sawiyy` (19:17) appears to a single witness; the conception happens in single-witness space; the cradle-speech happens upon SOCIAL RE-ENTRY, where the community can witness it. **The structure is: private divine act → public testimony.** Take out the withdrawal and the structure collapses into a public act with no testimony.

- *Cave Sleepers without withdrawal.* Stay in the city: the youths are persecuted and killed (the surah's own framing in 18:20 — `aw yuʿīdūkum fī millatihim`, "or return you to their religion"). The withdrawal is the survival-condition. Furthermore, the time-displacement requires a SEALED SPACE with no observers — observers would either age along with the Sleepers or wake them. The cave is the seal; without it, no displacement. 18:16 spells this out: `wa-idhā ʿtazaltumūhum … fa-ʾwū ilā l-kahf` — "when you have withdrawn from them … then take refuge in the cave." Two withdrawal-verbs stacked, then the cave. The cave is the second move; the withdrawal-from-people is the first.

- *Noah without ark.* Stay in the village: the rain reaches all bodies equally; the selectivity of preservation cannot be enacted. The Ark is the seal; the boarding is the withdrawal. 11:42–43 dramatizes this: Noah's son refuses to board, climbs a mountain, and is taken by the wave. The withdrawal is the BOUNDARY between the saved and the destroyed. Without it, the Flood is undifferentiated.

- *Lot without night-departure.* The dawn-destruction (15:73 `fa-akhadhathum al-ṣayḥatu mushriqīn`) catches the unwithdrawn. Lot's family is told `fa-asri bi-ahlika bi-qiṭʿin min al-layli wa-lā yaltafit minkum aḥad` (11:81) — depart by night with your family, none of you may look back. The night-march is the withdrawal-window before the dawn-cataclysm. Lot's wife, who looks back, is destroyed (`illa-mraʾataka innahu muṣībuhā mā aṣābahum`, 11:81). The looking-back is incomplete withdrawal. The narrative is rigorously structural: complete withdrawal saves; partial withdrawal fails.

- *Moses' Israelites without night-march.* `An asri bi-ʿibādī` (26:52) — DEPART BY NIGHT with My servants. Pharaoh follows at dawn; the sea opens at the boundary; the corridor closes on Pharaoh. The night-departure produces the timing that makes the sea-event work. Without the withdrawal, no corridor.

The conclusion: **withdrawal is not a trapping of the miracle; withdrawal is the operating condition of the miracle.** The Quran's grammar of divine intervention requires the prior clearing of the social field. God's most extraordinary acts happen INSIDE a sealed space cleared of unauthorized witnesses.

This is consistent with the Quran's broader theology of `ghayb` (the unseen). The miracle is enacted in private (in the `ghayb`-equivalent of social space); the testimony is public. The community knows the result, not the act. The withdrawal phase IS the construction of the `ghayb`-sealed condition under which divine action occurs. (Cross-reference `narrative-solomon-suite.md` §7 — the jinn cannot access `al-ghayb` even when working inside Solomon's court; the same epistemic boundary that limits jinn-knowledge governs the privacy of miracles.)

## 4. The sealed-space typology

The withdrawal phase ends in a sealed space. The Quran has a varied vocabulary for these. Catalog:

| Sealed-space type | Lexeme(s) | Instance(s) | Properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cave** | `al-kahf` (18:9, etc.); `al-ghār` (9:40 — Muḥammad's hijra-cave) | Cave Sleepers; Muḥammad in Thawr | Geological enclosure; one entrance; dark; the prototypical Quranic seal |
| **Curtain** | `ḥijāb` (19:17, 33:53, 38:32, 42:51, 7:46) | Maryam's annunciation; the Prophet's wives' chamber | Fabric/architectural enclosure; an interior cleared by drape |
| **Far place** | `makānan qaṣiyyan` (19:22); `makānan sharqiyyan` (19:16) | Maryam's birth-location | Distance creates the seal; no walls needed |
| **Ark** | `al-fulk` (passim); `al-juddī` (11:44 — landing place) | Noah | Floating sealed vessel; both transport and shelter |
| **Sea-corridor** | `al-baḥr / al-yamm` parted (26:63 `fa-nfalaqa fa-kāna kullu firqin ka-l-ṭawd al-ʿaẓīm`) | Moses' Israelites | The walls of water act as the seal; movement-through-seal |
| **Tent / khalwa-equivalent** | not lexicalized in narrative form, but `bayt` (the sacred house); the `miḥrāb` of Maryam (3:37), Zachariah (3:39), David (38:21) | Maryam, Zachariah, David | Architectural alcove; the Quran's hidden site of supernatural-encounter |
| **Solitary peak / mountain** | `al-Ṭūr` (Sinai, 28:29, 28:46, 19:52, 95:2); `Jabal` for Mūsā at the burning bush (20:9–14) | Moses' Sinai retreats | Geographical isolation by elevation |
| **Fish-belly** | `fī baṭn al-ḥūt` (37:142) | Yūnus | Living biological seal |
| **Well** | `ghayābat al-jubb` (12:10, 12:15) | Joseph | Underground enclosure; vertical descent |
| **Womb** | `arḥām` (passim); the `qarār makīn` (23:13, 77:21) | Embryogenesis (the implicit template) | Biological seal; the model for all withdrawal-miracle space |
| **Prison** | `al-sijn` (12:33, 12:36, 12:42, 12:100) | Joseph; the prophets in extension | Social/legal seal |
| **Death/`barzakh`** | `barzakh` (23:100) | All humanity between death and resurrection | The cosmic-scale sealed space; eschatology's withdrawal phase |
| **Night** | `al-layl` (passim) | Lot's exodus; Moses' Exodus; Muḥammad's `isrāʾ`; tahajjud | TEMPORAL seal — the time-equivalent of spatial isolation |

The list is striking in its breadth. The Quran's vocabulary for sealed spaces is one of its richer micro-lexicons: caves, curtains, distance, vessels, parted seas, alcoves, peaks, biological cavities, vertical descents, social confinements, post-mortem holding. Across all these, the operative property is the same: **the body is taken out of the social-witnessing field**. Whatever does the taking-out — geology, fabric, distance, water-walls, architecture, elevation, biology, gravity, law, death, time-of-day — counts as a sealed space if it produces the privacy condition.

The most theologically dense of these is `barzakh` (23:100) — the postmortem partition between death and resurrection. `Barzakh` is the eschatological generalization: every body undergoes a withdrawal at death, into a sealed space, before the cosmic miracle (resurrection). The pattern that runs every narrative also runs the universe.

The most common narrative seal is the cave plus the night. Cave is the spatial seal; night is the temporal seal. Where they co-occur (Muḥammad's hijra to the cave of Thawr, 9:40, traveling by night and hiding by day), the redundancy is structural — both seals at once. The `isrāʾ` (17:1) opens with `subḥāna lladhī asrā bi-ʿabdihi laylan` — Glory to Him who took His servant by NIGHT. Night is the temporal sealed space inside which the spatial sealed space (the Furthest Mosque) is reached. The two seals nest.

Negative space: the Quran does not narrate withdrawal in cities, palaces, marketplaces, pulpits, courtrooms, or assemblies. These are PUBLIC spaces; they cannot host the private divine act. The Solomon court appears to be an exception, but is not — see §7.

## 5. The divine-intervention mode — TARGETED MICRO-INTERVENTION

A consistent feature across the inventory is that God's act inside the sealed space is precisely targeted. The Quran does not narrate global divine interventions in these scenarios; it narrates LOCAL, MICRO-PRECISE operations on specific physical conditions. Catalog:

- **Cave Sleepers — strike on the EARS (18:11).** Not on the eyes, not on the breath, not on the heart, not on the brain. Specifically the auditory channel. Hearing is the wake-up sense; sealing it produces indefinite sleep. The Quran's vocabulary is mechanically specific. (See `narrative-cave-sleepers.md` §3 — "The Quran is engineering its miracle.")

- **Maryam — send the Spirit (19:17) and breathe into her (21:91).** Not "create a child"; not "remove the husband requirement"; specifically: SEND the Spirit, BREATHE-IN. The verb `n-f-x` is the same verb used for Adam's creation (15:29) and the eschatological Trumpet (39:68). Targeted micro-intervention: the smallest possible animating action.

- **Noah — open the springs (11:40 `wa-fāra l-tannūr`) and send rain.** Two precise hydrological events: subterranean water rising AND atmospheric water falling. The Quran is hydrologically specific (54:11 `fa-fataḥnā abwāba l-samāʾ bi-māʾin munhamir, wa-fajjarnā l-arḍa ʿuyūnan` — "We opened the gates of heaven with cascading water, and We caused the earth to gush springs"). The flood is engineered as the controlled merging of two water-sources; God targets two specific operations, not a generic "raise the sea."

- **Lot — flip the city (11:82 `jaʿalnā ʿāliyahā sāfilahā`) and rain stones (`amṭarnā ʿalayhā ḥijāratan min sijjīl`).** Two surgical operations: geographical inversion AND lithic precipitation. Not a generic "destroy"; specifically these two physical acts.

- **Moses' sea-parting — strike the sea with the staff (26:63 `iḍrib bi-ʿaṣāka l-baḥr`).** A targeted impact at a specific point. The water RESPONDS by parting (`fa-nfalaqa fa-kāna kullu firqin ka-l-ṭawd al-ʿaẓīm` — "and it split, and each part became like a great mountain"). Targeted micro-intervention on a specific location yields a localized geographical change. The sea elsewhere is undisturbed.

- **Israelites — manna and quail (2:57 `wa-anzalnā ʿalaykumu l-manna wa-l-salwā`); twelve springs (2:60 `iḍrib bi-ʿaṣāka l-ḥajara fa-nfajarat minhu thnatā ʿasharata ʿaynan`).** Provisions calibrated to need; springs at specified count (twelve, one per tribe). Provisioning is not a generic abundance but a calibrated supply.

- **Hundred-year man — the food and drink preserved (`lam yatasannah`); the donkey decomposed and reassembled bones-then-flesh (`nunshizuhā thumma naksūhā laḥmā`).** Two opposite outcomes from the same hundred years (`narrative-resurrection-demonstrations.md` §2): preservation AND decomposition-then-reassembly applied differentially to different objects in the same scene. Maximum targeting precision.

- **Yūnus — fish-belly preservation (37:144 — implicit) and the cast onto the bare shore (37:145 `nabaḏnāhu bi-l-ʿarāʾ`); the gourd-tree grown over him (37:146 `wa-anbatnā ʿalayhi shajaratan min yaqṭīn`).** Three discrete operations: preservation, ejection, vegetation. Each a targeted micro-act.

The pattern: **God transforms LOCAL conditions, not the world.** The hot zone of intervention is delineated; everything outside the zone proceeds normally. The Cave Sleepers wake into a city that has continued ageing for 309 years; Maryam emerges from her far place into a society that has continued unchanged; Noah lands at Jūdī and the post-flood world is repopulated from a remnant; Moses leads Israelites across a sea and the Egyptian dynasty continues without Pharaoh.

This is the mirror of the Quran's broader theology of creation: God's daily acts are local — the night-and-day cycle, the rain falling on this field and not that one (35:9 `wa-llāhu lladhī arsala l-riyāḥa fa-tuthīru saḥāban fa-suqnāhu ilā baladin mayyitin`), the sex of the fetus (3:6 `huwa lladhī yuṣawwirukum fī l-arḥām kayfa yashāʾu`). The divine act is precise. Withdrawal-miracle interventions ARE the same God doing the same local-precise thing; what differs is that the condition has been engineered (via withdrawal) so the intervention's effects are perceivable as anomalous.

This connects to `concept-Trf.md` (the gaze-blink as the speed of divine action) and the broader principle that Quranic divine action is INSTANTANEOUS and LOCAL. Solomon's throne arrives `mustaqirran ʿindahu` (27:40) — settled in place, no traversal narrated. The miracle is at-rest at a single point. The sea parts at the staff-strike (a point of impact). The Cave Sleepers are turned right and left repeatedly (a localized rotation). Targeted micro-intervention is the mode of divine action throughout; the withdrawal phase is what allows it to be PERCEIVED as miraculous.

## 6. Evidence at re-entry — the testimony pattern

The miracle becomes COMMUNITY-VISIBLE only at re-entry. This is the hinge that converts a private divine act into a public sign. Catalog:

- **Cave Sleepers — the silver coin (`wariq`, 18:19).** The scout sent to buy food carries currency that bears 309-year-old regnal stamps. The COIN is the temporal evidence; the city, examining it, infers the time-displacement. (See `narrative-cave-sleepers.md` §8 — "The Sleepers' time-displacement is exposed by money.") The evidence is everyday and economic, but it carries a DATE, and the date is the testimony.

- **Maryam — the infant Jesus speaks (19:30–33).** Maryam herself is bound by silence (`innī nadhartu li-l-Raḥmāni ṣawman fa-lan ukallima l-yawma insiyyan`, 19:26). The community asks accusatory questions; Maryam points at the infant; the infant produces the prophet-formula `innī ʿabdu llāhi ātāniya l-kitāb…` The miracle re-enters social space as VOCAL TESTIMONY in the most improbable possible body. The newborn IS the evidence and the witness simultaneously.

- **Noah — the Ark on Jūdī (11:44 `wa-stawat ʿalā l-jūdī`).** The ark stops on a specific mountain; the post-flood community sees the vessel as a permanent terrestrial witness. 25:37 makes the sign-function explicit: `wa-jaʿalnāhā āyatan li-l-nās`. The Ark is the Cave-coin's analog at landscape scale.

- **Lot — the ruined cities and the salt-figure (15:75 `inna fī dhālika la-āyātin li-l-mutawassimīn`).** The destroyed cities are visible to those who pass; the inverted topography testifies. Lot's wife (`imraʾatuhu`) becomes part of the evidentiary landscape (tradition: salt pillar; Quran-silent). The destruction-zone is the testimony.

- **Moses' Israelites — the parted-sea memory enshrined as covenant (2:50 `wa-idh faraqnā bikumu l-baḥra fa-anjaynākum wa-aghraqnā āla firʿawna wa-antum tanẓurūn` — "and you watched"). The Israelites SAW the corridor; the seeing IS the evidence; the memory-of-seeing is what `idh` (when) recurringly reactivates. The springs from the rock and the manna-and-quail provisioning are likewise re-entry evidence.

- **Hundred-year man — fresh food beside skeleton donkey (2:259).** The two-state evidence is the testimony: the food shows brief-time, the donkey shows long-time, the same scene contains both. Most importantly, the Quran says `wa-li-najʿalaka āyatan li-l-nās` — "that We may make YOU a sign for the people." The man himself becomes a visible verse; his survival, his fresh provisions, the donkey's bones are the textual evidence. (See `narrative-resurrection-demonstrations.md` §2 — "The man becomes scripture.")

- **Yūnus — sent to a hundred thousand or more after the cast (37:147 `wa-arsalnāhu ilā miʾati alfin aw yazīdūn`).** The community-visible result is the prophetic mission to Nineveh; the city that converts is the evidence-recipient. The miracle becomes a conversion-event.

The pattern: **the miracle does not merely happen; it RE-ENTERS.** Re-entry is its own phase, and it is the phase where the sign-function is enacted. Without re-entry, the miracle is private divine play. With re-entry, the miracle is testimony. The Quran is rigorously structural about this. Even Solomon — who does not undergo withdrawal, see §7 — does not get to keep his miracles private; the Queen of Sheba is the social re-entry pole; the throne's arrival is shown to her; the floor-of-glass is exhibited to her; the result is `aslamtu maʿa Sulaymāna li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn` (27:44).

The only narratives without social re-entry are the WITHDRAWAL-WITHOUT-RETURN cases — Idrīs raised (19:57), Jesus raised (4:158), and Maryam's eventual disappearance from the Quranic frame. These are template-truncated. They suggest a fourth phase-state beyond re-entry: ASCENT-WITHOUT-RETURN. We treat these as compressions of the eschatological architecture: the body that is withdrawn-and-not-returned IS the body whose status as "raised" is itself the testimony. In each case, the Quran flags the raising explicitly; the absence of a return is itself the sign.

The evidence pattern also clarifies the Quran's overall semiotics: **a sign (`āya`) is something that re-enters social space carrying the trace of an event the community did not directly witness**. This is the same definition that applies to scripture (the Quran itself is `āyāt` — verses-as-signs that re-enter the social field carrying trace of revelation). Withdrawal-miracle re-entry and revelation-text are structurally identical.

## 7. Solomon doesn't fit — and why (the inverse template)

Solomon (Sulaymān) breaks the pattern, and the breakage is itself diagnostic. He never withdraws. He stays in his court (`maqām`) and the world is brought TO him. (See `narrative-solomon-suite.md` headline: "Solomon is a magnet, not a projectile.")

| Phase | Withdrawal-template (Cave, Maryam, etc.) | Inverse-template (Solomon) |
|---|---|---|
| Withdrawal | Protagonist self-removes | Protagonist stays put |
| Isolation | Sealed space contains the body | Open court receives delegations |
| Divine intervention | God acts on the isolated body | God grants delegated cosmic instruments (wind, jinn, Book-knower) |
| Miracle | Private supernatural event | Public-court supernatural event |
| Social re-entry | Protagonist returns with evidence | The miracle's beneficiary (Queen of Sheba) enters and is converted |

Solomon's miracles are PUBLIC IN THE COURT. The throne arrives in front of the council (`malaʾ`, 27:38). The wind blows him visibly (34:12 — `ghuduwwuhā shahrun wa-rawāḥuhā shahrun`, the morning-going and evening-returning). The jinn build sanctuaries and statues in the open. There is no `ḥijāb`, no `qaṣiyy`, no `kahf`. The site of the miracle is the king's audience chamber.

This is the INVERSE template:

> **STATIONARY MAGNET → COSMIC INSTRUMENTS → PUBLIC MIRACLE → DELEGATIONS BROUGHT IN → CONVERSION ON-SITE**

The Quran encodes BOTH templates. The withdrawal-recipient template applies to prophets-as-isolated-figures (Cave Sleepers; Maryam; Noah; Lot; Moses; Yūnus; Muḥammad in Hira and on the `isrāʾ`). The receiving-magnet template applies uniquely to Solomon — and it is bounded by his own death-scene (34:14), where the limit of the inverse template is exposed. Once Solomon dies, the cosmic instruments (jinn) keep working but cannot detect his death — the receiving-magnet pattern requires a LIVING commanding intentionality at its center, and when that goes dark, the system fails. (This is the bridge between the two templates: the withdrawal template's `ghayb`-respecting privacy and the receiving-magnet template's `shahāda`-bounded epistemics are two sides of the same economy of divine knowledge.)

The deeper finding: **the receiving-magnet template is what KINGSHIP looks like in the Quranic register**. Solomon is `khalīfa` (cf. 38:26 for David); the king's body is the fixed point; the cosmos rearranges itself for his audience. The withdrawal-recipient template is what PROPHECY looks like: the prophet's body is mobile, the cosmos stays put, the prophet must clear the social field to receive. Solomon is BOTH king and prophet; the Quran assigns him the kingship-template for his miracles and the prophet-template only for his death (34:14 — the death of the king who functioned as a prophet, where the transition from public miracle to private death exposes the prior public-miracle posture as anomalous in the prophetic register).

Solomon is also the Quran's only example of a prophet who is given direct command of the cosmic instruments routinely deployed in the withdrawal-template. The wind that carries him (34:12) is the wind that destroys ʿĀd (51:41). The jinn that build for him (34:13) are the jinn that try to ascend to heaven (72:8). The Book-knower of 27:40 has `ʿilm min al-kitāb` — knowledge that touches the unseen. Solomon receives the COSMIC-INSTRUMENT side of the divine-intervention apparatus; the prophets receive the BODY-AS-RECEIVER side. Two roles, two templates.

## 8. Edge cases — Yūnus and Muḥammad-in-Hira

### Yūnus — withdrawal in distress

Yūnus (Jonah) at first glance looks like a counter-example: he flees from his prophetic mission (37:140 `idh ʾabaqa ilā l-fulki l-mash'ḥūn` — "when he fled to the laden ship"). The withdrawal is panic, not piety. But the template still fits.

- WITHDRAWAL: he flees (`ʾabaqa` — runaway-slave verb; `dhahaba mughāḍiban` 21:87 — "departed angry"). The withdrawal is unauthorized, but it is a withdrawal.
- ISOLATION: the fish-belly. `Fa-ltaqamahu l-ḥūt` (37:142, "the fish swallowed him"); `fī baṭn al-ḥūt` (37:144, "in the belly of the fish"). The biological seal is one of the Quran's cleanest cases of sealed-space — a living organism holding a living human inside. 21:87 emphasizes the darkness: `fa-nādā fī l-ẓulumāti` — "he called out in the layered darknesses" (plural ẓulumāt).
- DIVINE INTERVENTION: God responds to Yūnus's confession (`lā ilāha illā anta subḥānaka innī kuntu mina l-ẓālimīn`, 21:87) and orders the fish.
- MIRACLE: preservation in fish-belly + ejection. `Fa-nabaḏnāhu bi-l-ʿarāʾ wa-huwa saqīm` (37:145, "We cast him onto the bare shore, and he was sick"). Note the verb `nabaḏnā` — the same root that names Maryam's reflexive `intabadhat` (19:22). God CASTS Yūnus onto the bare shore; Maryam CASTS HERSELF into the far place. The same root governs both ejective movements. (See `concept-nb*.md` §4 — Yūnus and Maryam's bare-place receptacles are paired in the root's data.)
- SOCIAL RE-ENTRY: The gourd-tree shelters him (37:146); he is then sent back to the people he had originally tried to flee (37:147 `wa-arsalnāhu ilā miʾati alfin aw yazīdūn` — "We sent him to a hundred thousand or more"). The mission resumes; the city converts.

The Yūnus story is the template applied to a RELUCTANT prophet. The withdrawal is moral failure (running from the mission), but the template's machinery operates on it anyway: ejection, sealed space, divine intervention, miracle, re-entry. The Quran shows that the template can be IMPOSED on a body that did not choose it. Even unauthorized withdrawal triggers the cycle.

The corollary: **withdrawal is a structural condition, not a moral act**. Whether the protagonist piously withdraws (Maryam, Cave Sleepers), flees in panic (Yūnus), or is forced out (Lot's family fleeing the dawn-strike), the template runs. What matters is that the body crosses out of the ordinary social field; the moral character of the crossing is secondary.

### Muḥammad — Hira, Thawr, and the Night Journey

The Cave of Hira (where the first revelation reportedly occurred) is extra-Quranic; tradition places Muḥammad in `taḥannuth` (devotional retreat) when 96:1–5 was revealed. Although the in-text Quran does not narrate this, the in-text companions are clear:

- **9:40** — Muḥammad and Abū Bakr in the cave of Thawr during the hijra: `idh humā fī l-ghāri idh yaqūlu li-ṣāḥibihi lā taḥzan inna llāha maʿanā` — "when the two of them were in the cave, when he said to his companion, 'Do not grieve; God is with us.'" Cave (`al-ghār`) — withdrawal — divine intervention (the spider's web, the dove's nest, in tradition; Quran-silent on the mechanism but explicit on `inna llāha maʿanā`) — miracle (escape from pursuers) — re-entry (the hijra completes, the ummah is established at Madīna).
- **17:1** — `subḥāna lladhī asrā bi-ʿabdihi laylan mina l-masjidi l-ḥarāmi ilā l-masjidi l-aqṣā` — Glory to Him who took His servant by night from the Sacred Mosque to the Furthest Mosque. The Night Journey runs the template at maximum compression: WITHDRAWAL (taken from his bed by night), ISOLATION (the journey itself, mediated agent traditionally Burāq, Quran-silent), DIVINE INTERVENTION (the journey's purpose `li-nuriyahu min āyātinā`), MIRACLE (the journey itself, plus the further ascent in 53:13–18), SOCIAL RE-ENTRY (back to Mecca by morning, `subḥāna` formula opens the surah). The `laylan` (by night) is the temporal seal; the destination is the spatial seal; the body is moved through both.
- **74:1–2** — `yā ayyuhā l-muddaththir, qum fa-andhir`. The Prophet is wrapped (`al-muddaththir` — the wrapped-in-his-cloak); the wrapping is itself a body-scale withdrawal (a textile seal — `dithār` is a heavy outer garment); the divine command "rise and warn" is the social re-entry imperative.

The pattern HOLDS for the Prophet's own initiation. The Quran's grammar of revelation matches the grammar of the older miracle-narratives. **The Prophet is not exempt from the template; he is its most recent instance.** This is consistent with the Quran's self-presentation as continuous with prior prophecy (the `wa-aydan ka-dhālika kāna` constructions — and so it was, similarly).

The composite finding: every PROPHET in the Quran undergoes some form of withdrawal-isolation as the gateway to their commissioning or to a major miracle. Where the in-text record is silent (Hira), tradition fills in; where the in-text record is present (Thawr, `isrāʾ`, `muddaththir`, the early surahs of seclusion), the template is overt. The withdrawal-pattern is the Quranic METHOD of prophetic commissioning.

## 9. Withdrawal as Quranic METHOD — institutionalization in Islamic ritual

The template is not narrative-bound. The Quran (and the larger Islamic tradition built around it) has INSTITUTIONALIZED the withdrawal pattern as recurring spiritual practice. Catalog:

- **`Iʿtikāf` (mosque-seclusion in Ramadan).** 2:187 — `wa-lā tubāshirūhunna wa-antum ʿākifūna fī l-masājid` — "do not have intercourse with them while you are in seclusion in the mosques." `Iʿtikāf` is the spouse-suspending, social-suspending retreat into the mosque, especially in the last ten nights of Ramadan (which include the Night of Power, 97:1 — itself a NIGHT-of-divine-descent). The practice is a structural mini-rehearsal of the withdrawal template: leave the social field, occupy a sealed space (the mosque), wait for divine descent (the Night of Power), re-enter at Eid with the spiritual evidence of the retreat.

- **`Khalwa` (Sufi retreat).** Outside the formal Quranic legislation but built on the same template: the seeker withdraws for a fixed period (40 days is the canonical Sufi number, modeled on Mūsā's 40 days at Sinai, 7:142). The `khalwa` is the prophetic-template applied to the spiritual seeker. The practice's name (`khalwa` — being-alone) is itself the withdrawal-condition.

- **`Hijra` (the foundational withdrawal).** The community-wide migration from Mecca to Madīna in 622 is the Quran's most elaborate single instance of the withdrawal template, scaled to a community. 9:40 names the cave-of-Thawr withdrawal; 22:39 names the permission to fight; 4:97 — `qālū fīma kuntum qālū kunnā mustaḍʿafīna fī l-arḍ qālū a-lam takun arḍu llāhi wāsiʿatan fa-tuhājirū fīhā` — the angel's question to those who failed to migrate. **The hijra is the Quran's foundational application of the withdrawal template to the entire Muslim community.** The Anṣār of Madīna shelter (`āwaw wa-naṣarū`, 8:74 — the form-IV `āwā` from `concept-Awy.md`); the muhājirūn are sheltered. The Awy root that names the Cave Sleepers' refuge names the Anṣār's hospitality. Tradition then dates the Islamic calendar from this withdrawal — the hijra is YEAR ZERO. The withdrawal-as-template is the foundational temporal anchor of Islamic civilization.

- **`Tahajjud` (night-prayer).** 17:79 — `wa-mina l-layli fa-tahajjad bihi nāfilatan laka` — "and from a part of the night, pray with [the Quran] as a voluntary act for you." Tahajjud is the institutionalized night-vigil; the night provides the temporal seal; the household sleeps; the worshipper occupies a sealed time-of-day. 73:1–7 — `yā ayyuhā l-muzzammil, qum al-layla illā qalīlan` — "O wrapped one, stand the night except a little." The Quran addresses Muḥammad as the WRAPPED one and commands night-rising. The wrapping (`muzzammil`) and the night (`al-layl`) are both seals; the prayer is the divine-intervention-receiving act inside the doubled seal.

- **`Ṣawm` (fasting).** The fast suspends ordinary metabolic dependence; the body is sealed against ingestion. Ramadan-fast is the COLLECTIVE seasonal application — a month-long social withdrawal-from-eating that mirrors the structural withdrawal-from-society of `iʿtikāf` and `khalwa`.

- **The Night of Power (`laylat al-qadr`, 97:1–5).** The annual repetition of the descent. `Tanazzalu l-malāʾikatu wa-l-rūḥu fīhā bi-idhni rabbihim min kulli amr` (97:4) — "the angels and the Spirit descend in it by their Lord's permission, for every matter." A night-time, recurring, divine-descent event located in the last ten of Ramadan (i.e., during the institutional `iʿtikāf` window). The Night of Power is the LITURGICAL re-enactment of the original revelation moment; the believers' nightly vigils across those ten nights are the ritual rehearsal of the withdrawal-template.

The composite finding: **the Quran's withdrawal-miracle template is also its method of spiritual practice.** What the prophets DID once (withdraw → receive → return), the believer does annually (`iʿtikāf`, fast), monthly (the lunar cycle's silences), nightly (`tahajjud`), and once-in-a-lifetime (`ḥajj` — the pilgrimage to a SACRED sealed space, the `ḥaram`, which suspends ordinary social/economic activity for the duration). Islamic ritual is the prophetic withdrawal-template scaled down to repeating cycles inhabitable by ordinary practitioners.

This is theologically consequential. If the withdrawal-template is the operating condition for divine intervention, then the institutionalized practices (`iʿtikāf`, `khalwa`, `tahajjud`, fasting, hijra-commemoration) are positioned as ENABLING CONDITIONS for analogous (smaller-scale) divine action. The Quran does not promise miracles to every `iʿtikāf` retreatant; but it does say `wa-lladhīna jāhadū fīnā la-nahdiyannahum subulanā` (29:69) — "those who strive in Us, We will surely guide them to Our paths." The institutional withdrawal-practices are the way ordinary believers position themselves for divinely-given guidance, even when the form of guidance is not the spectacular sea-parting or angelic descent of the prophetic narratives. **The template scales.**

## 10. Connection to the time investigation — TWO MODES THAT DON'T MERGE

The time investigation's `research-cyclical-vs-linear.md` (in `notes/time/2-cross-cutting/`) makes a structurally parallel argument: the Quran has TWO temporal modes (cyclical liturgical time and linear eschatological time) that DO NOT MERGE. The teleportation-template here is the spatial-mode analog: TWO SPATIAL MODES that don't merge — the WITHDRAWN-PRIVATE space and the SOCIAL-PUBLIC space. Divine intervention happens IN the withdrawn space, but its evidence MUST travel into the public space. The two spaces are bridged by the re-entry phase, but they remain categorically distinct: the public space cannot itself be the site of the private divine act, and the private divine act would be unverified without re-entry into the public space.

`Research-dawn-destruction.md` (in `notes/time/3-rabbit-holes/`) makes the further point: NIGHT is the time-of-withdrawal; DAWN is the time-of-judgment. The Lot exodus is the canonical case: depart by night (`bi-qiṭʿin min al-layl`, 11:81); the destruction strikes at dawn (`mushriqīn`, 15:73). Temporal-withdrawal and spatial-withdrawal CO-OCCUR in Lot's narrative. The same is true for Moses' Exodus (depart by night, sea opens at the boundary, Pharaoh drowns at the dawn-pursuit).

This suggests a deeper unifying claim: **the Quran's withdrawal-template is BOTH spatial AND temporal**. The body must be removed both from a place AND from a time. The night-march of the Lot family removes them BOTH from Sodom's social space AND from Sodom's daytime. Maryam's far place is BOTH a `makān qaṣiyy` AND apparently a temporally-dilated event (the pregnancy may be compressed in tradition). The Cave Sleepers' cave is a SPATIAL seal that produces a 309-year TEMPORAL seal. Solomon, who has no spatial withdrawal, also has no temporal anomaly until his death (34:14, the postmortem deception).

The temporal seals catalog matches the spatial seals catalog:

- Night (`al-layl`) — daily seal.
- The 10 nights of Ramadan and the Night of Power — annual liturgical seal.
- The 40 days (Mūsā at Sinai, 7:142; the Cave of Thawr is 3 days in tradition) — discrete-event seal.
- The 309 years of the Cave Sleepers — long-duration seal.
- The 100 years of the hundred-year man — individual-mortality seal.
- The lifetime in `barzakh` — postmortem seal.

The longest temporal seal is `barzakh`; the shortest is the gaze-blink (`yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk`, 27:40) — and the Quran's instantaneity-comparators (the gaze-blink, the nearer-than-the-blink) are themselves descriptions of seals so brief that ordinary perception cannot register them. The full continuum: gaze-blink → night → days → years → centuries → barzakh. Across all scales, the same template runs: clear the field, host the divine act, re-enter with evidence.

The cross-investigation finding: **time and space behave SYMMETRICALLY in the Quran's withdrawal-grammar**. The same seal-architecture works in either dimension; the same five-phase template runs at any scale; the same evidence-at-re-entry pattern adjudicates. This is consistent with the time investigation's observation that the Quran does not maintain a strict modality boundary between cyclical and linear time — the two co-exist in the same passages. The teleportation cluster shows the same behavior on the spatial side: withdrawn-private and social-public co-exist in the same narrative, separated only by the re-entry threshold.

## 11. Translation divergence on withdrawal-verbs

How consistently do the four translators handle the cross-narrative withdrawal-verb cluster? Comparison across the key verses:

| Verse | Verb | Saheeh | Pickthall | Khattab | Arberry |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18:10 | `awā` (took refuge) | "retreated to the cave" | "fled for refuge to the Cave" | "took refuge in the cave" | "took refuge in the Cave" |
| 18:16 | `ʾwū` (imp. take-refuge) | "retreat" | "seek refuge" | "take refuge" | "take refuge" |
| 19:16 | `ntabadhat` | "secluded herself … to a place toward the east" | "withdrew … to a chamber looking East" | "withdrew … to a place in the east" | "withdrew … to an eastern place" |
| 19:22 | `ntabadhat bihi` | "withdrew with him" | "withdrew with him to a far place" | "withdrew with him" | "withdrew with him to a distant place" |
| 17:1 | `asrā bi-ʿabdihi` | "took His Servant by night" | "carried His servant by night" | "took His servant by night" | "carried His servant by night" |
| 11:81 | `fa-asri bi-ahlika` | "set out with your family" | "travel with thy people" | "leave with your family" | "set forth with thy family" |
| 26:52 | `an asri bi-ʿibādī` | "Travel by night with My servants" | "Take away My slaves by night" | "Leave with My servants" | "Go forth with My servants by night" |

Patterns:

1. **18:10 — Saheeh's "retreated" is the OUTLIER**, with military overtones the Arabic does not specify; the others converge on "refuge." Pickthall's "fled for refuge" injects urgency. Saheeh and Pickthall together deliver the most active, dramatic readings; Khattab and Arberry stay closest to the Arabic's neutral take-refuge sense.

2. **19:16 — translators diverge on `makānan sharqiyyan`**: Pickthall alone reads it architecturally ("a chamber looking East"). The others read it as bare geography. Saheeh inserts the modifier "secluded HERSELF" — making explicit what the form-VIII reflexive encodes morphologically.

3. **19:22 — LOW divergence** (combined=0.272 from the Phase-1 data). All four translators agree on "withdrew with him." Only divergence is on `qaṣiyyan` (far/remote/distant) — minor lexical variance.

4. **17:1, 11:81, 26:52 — the `asrā` family** is rendered consistently as "by night" (with Pickthall's `take away My slaves` for 26:52 being a register-outlier). The night-temporal-seal is preserved across translators.

The cross-narrative consistency check: **translators ARE consistent across narratives in recognizing these as withdrawal-verbs.** The English vocabulary used is uniform: "withdraw," "take refuge," "depart by night," "set forth," "carry [by night]." The structural uniformity of the withdrawal-template is REINFORCED by the translation-uniformity at the verb-level.

Where translators diverge most is on the SEAL-DESCRIPTORS (the place-qualifiers): is the place a chamber or an open eastern space? Is `qaṣiyy` "far" or "distant" or "remote"? The verbs of withdrawal are stable; the descriptions of the seal are unstable. This is consistent with the Phase-1 finding that the Quran is precise on TRANSITIONS and underspecified on PLACES — translators inherit both characteristics.

A second pattern: **translators systematically OBSCURE the morphological violence of `intabadhat`.** The form-VIII reflexive of `nb*` carries the violence of self-throwing (the same root that hurls Pharaoh into the sea, 28:40). All four translators render `intabadhat` as the polite "withdrew" — losing the ejective violence. Maryam SELF-EJECTS in the Arabic; she WITHDRAWS in English. The structural finding (Maryam as the only person in the Quran who self-ejects) survives only at the morphological level; the surface translations homogenize it. (See `concept-nb*.md` §2 — "Maryam is the only person in the Quran who self-ejects.")

A third pattern: **translators do NOT cross-link the withdrawal-verb cluster.** Each verse is translated in isolation; the structural identity between Maryam's `intabadhat` (19:22), the Cave Sleepers' `awā` (18:10), and the Israelites' `asrā` (26:52) is preserved only at the level of meaning ("withdraw," "take refuge," "depart by night") and not at the level of morphological-family awareness. A reader of the four English translations CAN deduce the template by following the meaning; a reader can NOT see the lexical-family network the way an Arabic reader can see `awā / ʾwū / āwā / maʾwā` (Awy root) operating across surahs and forms.

## 12. Rabbit holes for Phase 3

1. **The wrapped-figure topology.** `Al-muddaththir` (74:1) and `al-muzzammil` (73:1) — the Prophet wrapped in his cloak. The textile-seal is a rare withdrawal-vehicle in the Quran. Compare to Maryam's `ḥijāb` (19:17) — also a textile-seal. Compile every textile-seal occurrence and ask: when is fabric the boundary?

2. **The `bayn al-saddayn` and Dhū l-Qarnayn (18:93–98).** The two-walled valley is itself a sealed space; Dhū l-Qarnayn's wall-building at 18:96 is a RECONSTRUCTION of seal-architecture; Yājūj-and-Mājūj are sealed BEHIND the wall until the Hour. This is a withdrawal-template applied to a corrupting community in reverse (THEY are the ones isolated, against their will, for the protection of the wider community). Worth a focused study.

3. **The `barzakh` as eschatological withdrawal (23:100, 25:53, 55:20).** The partition between worlds. Map all `barzakh` occurrences and integrate with the withdrawal-template. Hypothesis: `barzakh` is the universal generalization of the withdrawal-phase; every individual death is a withdrawal; the Hour is the universal re-entry-with-evidence.

4. **Joseph's well, prison, and palace as a withdrawal-cycle compressed into one biography.** Three sealed spaces in sequence; three re-entries with evidence; the final reunion in Egypt as the master re-entry. Joseph's biography as the EXTENDED withdrawal-template — worth a dedicated narrative deep-read in Phase-2.

5. **Mūsā's biographical withdrawal-cycles.** Drawn from the Nile (the basket as sealed-vessel, 20:39 / 28:7); fled to Madyan (28:22, withdrawal); 40-night Sinai retreat (7:142); the night-march from Egypt (26:52). Mūsā's biography is the withdrawal-template ITERATED across decades. A focused study would map each iteration and ask whether the Quran is presenting Mūsā's life as the model of recurring withdrawal-pattern application.

6. **The `āwā / āwaynāhumā` divine-sheltering cluster.** 23:50 — God shelters Maryam-and-Jesus on the rabwa. 8:26 — God shelters the muhājirūn. 93:6 — God shelters orphan Muḥammad. Form-IV `āwā` with God as subject is the divine HOST verb. Compile every form-IV `āwā` occurrence and study the divine-as-host theology.

7. **The 17–18–19 surah triple.** Surah 17 (al-Isrāʾ) opens with the Night Journey; surah 18 (al-Kahf) houses the Cave Sleepers; surah 19 (Maryam) houses Maryam's withdrawal. Three consecutive surahs each anchored to a withdrawal-class miracle, each with a different mobility-verb. (See `narrative-maryam-mobility.md` rabbit hole 9.) The surah-ordering is suggestive; worth a focused study on whether the ordering is intentional and what theological work the trilogy does.

8. **`Ḥijra` legislation and the withdrawal-template.** 4:100 — `wa-man yuhājir fī sabīli llāhi yajid fī l-arḍi murāghaman kathīran wa-saʿatan` — "whoever migrates in God's way will find on earth many refuges and abundance." 9:20 — the higher rank of the muhājirūn. Compile the `hjr` (migrate) corpus and study how the Quran codifies the WITHDRAWAL-AS-VIRTUE doctrine after Madīna.

9. **The `khalwa` Hadith literature.** The cave of Hira tradition; the 40-day `khalwa` of the Sufi orders. The hadith corpus institutionalizes the withdrawal-template far beyond what the Quran narrates. Worth a comparative study to see whether the Sufi practice is a faithful extension or an over-reading.

10. **Solomon's death (34:14) as the inverse-template's failure mode.** What happens when the receiving-magnet template is the protagonist's only mode and the magnet's commanding intentionality goes silent? The Quran's answer: the cosmic instruments (jinn) keep operating in front of a corpse until the smallest physical evidence (worm-eaten staff) gives the death away. This is the structural inversion of the withdrawal-template's evidence-at-re-entry: in Solomon's case, the EVIDENCE (worm + collapsed staff) marks not re-entry but UNDETECTED EXIT. Worth a parallel study comparing Cave Sleepers' coin (evidence of unseen displacement) to Solomon's worm (evidence of unseen death).

11. **The `nafkha` (Trumpet) as the cosmic-scale divine intervention.** 39:68 — `wa-nufikha fī l-ṣūr fa-ṣaʿiqa man fī l-samāwāti wa-man fī l-arḍ`. Same verb (`n-f-x`) as Maryam's conception (21:91) and Adam's creation (15:29). The Trumpet is the cosmic-scale targeted micro-intervention: a single AUDITORY event (paralleling the Cave Sleepers' EAR-strike) triggers universal effect. The Trumpet at the cosmic scale is the ear-strike at the individual scale. Cross-cutting study warranted.

12. **The category of "sign for the people" (`āyatan li-l-nās`) across the corpus.** Compile every occurrence: hundred-year man (2:259); Maryam-and-Jesus (21:91, 23:50); Noah's people (25:37); the saved on the Ark (29:15). Each instance is a re-entry-with-evidence event. The `āya`-naming converts the bearer's body or community into evidence. Worth a focused study on what makes a body or community eligible to be named `āya`.
