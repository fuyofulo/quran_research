# Cross-cutting research: the vertical / horizontal axis split in Quranic teleportation

> **Phase-2 synthesis.** Pulling Phase-1 root files (rfE, Erj, sry, wfy, HDr, Trf, lmH) and narrative deep-reads (Idrīs/Jesus, Maryam, Solomon, Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj, Cave Sleepers, Angels-and-Jinn) into a single cross-cutting question: **does the Quran's teleportation vocabulary partition into two perpendicular axes that do not blend?** The data is unambiguous — yes. Vertical (heaven-earth) and horizontal (earth-bound point-to-point) recruit disjoint root-sets, attach to disjoint patient classes, follow disjoint directionality rules, and correlate with disjoint time-frames. The fusion that devotional memory performs (Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj as a single horizontal-then-vertical event) is hadith elaboration; the Quranic text refuses the lexical bridge.

---

## 1. Headline finding

The Quran's teleportation lexicon sorts onto **two structurally distinct axes**:

- **VERTICAL (heaven ↔ earth).** Up: `rfE` (raise), `Erj` (climb), `wfy` (take-fully — soul extracted upward at death). Down: `nzl` (descend), `hbT` (go down — Adam's fall). Used of: angels, the Spirit, souls, revelation, rain — and exactly **two** named human prophets: Idrīs (19:57) and Jesus (3:55, 4:158).
- **HORIZONTAL (point-to-point on earth, however supernaturally fast).** `sry` (Form IV asrā = divine-comitative night-transport), `Aty` (come / bring), `nb*` (withdraw, Maryam at 19:22), `Awy` (take refuge), `ḏhb` (go). Used of: prophets in motion on earth (Lot's family, Moses with his followers, Muhammad's Isrāʾ, Maryam's withdrawal), things-brought-to-Solomon, ordinary travel.

A third register — **POSITION-CHANGE-WITHOUT-TRAVEL** — sits orthogonal to both: `HDr` (be-brought-into-presence, *muḥḍar*), `qrr` X (`istaqarra`, settle), `jʿl` (render). These are arrival-without-traversal verbs that do not specify which axis the arrival was on. Speed-comparators `Trf` and `lmH` belong here too — they name *that the arrival was instant* without naming a trajectory.

The decisive data point is co-occurrence. In the Phase-1 structural pre-cut (`teleportation-cooccurrence.md` §1, §8.2), **rfE and Erj co-occur in zero ayahs across the entire corpus**. The two core upward verbs never share a single verse. And `sry` (the horizontal Isrāʾ verb) likewise never shares an ayah with `Erj` (the vertical-ascent verb). The lexical apartheid is total. Whatever the Quran is doing with these vocabularies, **it is not letting them mix at the verse level**. This is the structural fact this document begins from.

The implications cascade. One-way transit clusters with vertical motion (Idrīs and Jesus do not return); round-trip clusters with horizontal motion (Muhammad is back by morning, Maryam returns to her people). Time-distortion clusters with vertical motion (1,000-year days at 32:5 and 50,000-year days at 70:4 are pinned to *yaʿruju ilayh*); horizontal motion preserves earthly time (`laylan` — by night). The bi-ʿabdihi comitative (*God moves WITH* the human) is a horizontal-only construction. Angels are the only beings who freely cross both axes. The cosmological architecture — seven layered heavens, the earth, the Throne above, Hell below — encodes this geometry. **The miracle of Quranic teleportation is not the speed but the boundary-crossing**, and the heaven-earth boundary is the one that requires divine sanction.

A compact inventory.

The two-axis split is visible: vertical-up uses three core roots (`rfE`, `Erj`, `wfy`); vertical-down two (`nzl`, `hbT`); horizontal-extraordinary four (`sry`, `Aty`, `nb*`, `Awy`). **No root crosses the two extraordinary axes.** `sry` is never used vertically; `rfE` is never used horizontally. Position-change verbs (HDr, qrr) and speed-comparators (Trf, lmH) appear orthogonally — they describe *how* an arrival happens (instantly, by being-brought) without committing to an axis.

## 3. Lexical apartheid — does the boundary hold?

Five fusion-candidate verses, each tested against the question: *do the vertical and horizontal vocabularies actually co-occur?*

**17:1 (Isrāʾ).** Verb count: `asrā` only. Source and destination are both `masjid`. No `rfE`, no `Erj`, no `nzl`. Pure horizontal.

**53:1-18 (the second-vision pericope).** The motion verbs in the passage all attach to *the descending figure* (Jibrīl), not to Muhammad. 53:8 *thumma danā fa-tadallā* (he drew near and lowered himself). 53:13 *raʾāhu nazlatan ukhrā* (saw him in another *descent* — `nzl` root, *applied to the figure*). Muhammad's verbs are visual: `raʾā` four times. **Muhammad does not ascend in 53.** He sees a descender. The Erj root is absent from 53 entirely, and the noun `miʿrāj` does not appear in the Quran at all (only the plural `maʿārij` at 70:3 of God's stairways and 43:33 of counterfactual silver stairs — never of Muhammad).

**19:57 (Idrīs).** *wa rafaʿnāhu makānan ʿaliyyā*. Three Arabic words for the entire event. `rfE` only. No companion verbs of any kind. The raising is bare.

**3:55 (Jesus's three-operation stack).** *innī mutawaffīka wa rāfiʿuka ilayya wa muṭahhiruka*. Three operations — `wfy`, `rfE`, `Thr` — **all in the vertical register**. `wfy` is the soul-extraction verb (vertical-up); `rfE` is the raising verb (vertical-up); `ṭhr` is purification toward the destination *ilayya*. **No horizontal verb appears.**

**27:40 (Solomon's throne).** *ana ātīka bihi qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuk* + *raʾāhu mustaqirran ʿindahu*. The horizontal-bringing verb (`Aty`) collaborates with the position-change-arrival verb (`istaqarra`). **No vertical verb appears.** The throne does not descend; it is brought-and-settled. Sheba is on the ground; Solomon's court is on the ground; the trajectory is horizontal even though the speed is super-creaturely.

Five test sites, five clean separations. The fusion candidates do not fuse at the lexical level. Where a verse needs vertical motion, it uses vertical verbs *exclusively*. Where horizontal, horizontal *exclusively*. The Quran has multiple opportunities to blend — the Isrāʾ, the three-verb Jesus passage, Solomon's continent-spanning throne — and refuses each one. **The lexical apartheid is a designed feature, not an accident.**

## 4. The trans-cosmic 3-verse subset

`Concept-rfE.md` §C identifies three verses where `rfE` behaves differently from its other 26 occurrences:

1. **3:55** — Jesus: *wa rāfiʿuka ilayya*
2. **4:158** — Jesus: *bal rafaʿahu Allāhu ilayh*
3. **19:57** — Idrīs: *wa rafaʿnāhu makānan ʿaliyyā*

What unites them:

- **All three apply to a NAMED PROPHET as patient.** The other 26 rfE-occurrences attach to heavens (13:2, 79:28), mountains (2:63, 2:93, 4:154), the Kaʿba's foundations (2:127), prophet-ranks-collectively (2:253), Joseph's parents on a throne (12:100), good speech (35:10), reputation (94:4), or paradise furniture (56:34, 88:13). Only these three have a *single named prophet body* as the patient.
- **All three are ONE-WAY.** Idrīs is raised; he does not return. Jesus is raised; the Quran does not narrate his return. (The eschatological return-of-Jesus tradition uses `nzl`, not Quranic.) Compare to all other vertical motion in the corpus — angels descend AND ascend (97:4 down + 70:4 up); Spirit descends AND ascends (16:102 + 70:4); souls are taken at death AND `thumma ilā rabbikum turjaʿūn` (32:11 — they are returned for resurrection). Idrīs and Jesus are the two exceptions to bidirectional vertical traffic.
- **All three have GOD as agent**, unmediated. No angel of death, no Spirit, no instrument. The active participle `rāfiʿ` occurs only at 3:55, of God toward Jesus. Humans are never agents of trans-cosmic rfE.
- **All three have UNSPECIFIED MECHANISM.** No vehicle, accompanying party, timing-statement, or interior cosmography. Just verb + destination.

Three verses out of 6,236. By word-count, the human-vertical-transport canon is roughly **0.05% of the Quran**. The Quran's most cosmologically loaded miracle gets fewer than thirty Arabic words across the entire corpus. This is the Quran's signature minimalism applied to its most metaphysically extreme claim.

What the subset does NOT include is also load-bearing. Muhammad is *not* in this group. Despite the Miʿrāj tradition's elaborate vertical itinerary, no Quranic verse has Muhammad as the patient of `rafaʿa` in a trans-cosmic sense. The only Muhammad-rafaʿ verse is 94:4 — *wa rafaʿnā laka dhikrak* — the *honorific* register (raising fame), not the trans-cosmic one. **The trans-cosmic 3-verse subset is reserved for the two prophets whom the tradition holds to be still alive.**

## 5. ONE-WAY vertical vs ROUND-TRIP horizontal

| Event | Axis | Verb-class | Direction | Return? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Idrīs raised | Vertical | rfE | Up | No |
| Jesus raised | Vertical | rfE (+ wfy at 3:55) | Up to God | No (eschatological return is hadith) |
| Souls at death | Vertical | wfy (tawaffā) | Up | Yes (at resurrection) |
| Souls in sleep | Vertical | wfy partial (39:42) | Up | Yes (at waking) |
| Angels & Spirit | Vertical | nzl down, Erj up | Both | Bidirectional |
| Quran descends | Vertical | nzl | Down | One-way |
| Adam falls | Vertical | hbT | Down | One-way (the Fall) |
| **Muhammad's Isrāʾ** | **Horizontal** | sry (asrā) | Mecca → Aqṣā | Implicit (laylan = within one night) |
| **Maryam's withdrawal** | **Horizontal** | nb* (intabadhat) | To `makānan qaṣiyyan` | Yes — returns with infant Jesus 19:27 |
| **Lot's family** | **Horizontal** | sry (asrā) | Out of Sodom | One-way (Sodom is destroyed; no return point) |
| **Moses with Israelites** | **Horizontal** | sry (asrā) | Across the sea | One-way exit from Egypt |
| **Solomon's throne** | **Horizontal** | Aty + istaqarra | Sheba → Jerusalem | n/a (object, not person) |
| **Cave Sleepers** | **In-place** | Awy + lbv + bʿv | Stationary; 309-year displacement | Yes (they wake) |
| **Hundred-year sleeper** | **In-place** | mwt + bʿv | Stationary; 100-year displacement | Yes (revived) |

The pattern is sharp:
- **Vertical UP for prophets**: NO RETURN (Idrīs, Jesus).
- **Vertical UP for souls/angels/Spirit**: bidirectional.
- **Horizontal for prophets**: round-trip OR escape (Muhammad and Maryam return; Lot and Moses exit a destroyed origin).
- **In-place time-displacement**: yes — but as *demonstration apparatus* (cf. `narrative-cave-sleepers.md` §1, the Sleepers as eschatological-resurrection rehearsal).

The structural fact: **the axis predicts the directionality**. Vertical-permanent UP is reserved for prophets-the-Quran-removes-from-history. Horizontal is reserved for prophets-who-remain-in-history. The two axes encode inverse default directionalities for prophets: vertical = one-way (exit from earthly bounds); horizontal = round-trip (extraordinary intra-earthly transit). The structural reason: leaving the earthly time-frame is irreversible without divine action; staying within the earthly time-frame allows ordinary return.

## 6. Time-distortion correlates with vertical motion

The 1,000-year day at 32:5 and the 50,000-year day at 70:4 are the Quran's two explicit cross-temporal-scale verses. Both are pinned to *vertical ascent* (Erj root):

> 32:5 — *yudabbiru al-amra mina al-samāʾi ila al-arḍ thumma yaʿruju ilayhi fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhu alfa sanatin mimmā taʿuddūn*

> 70:4 — *taʿruju al-malāʾikatu wa al-rūḥu ilayhi fī yawmin kāna miqdāruhu khamsīna alfa sanah*

Both use *yaʿruju / taʿruju*, both terminate at *ilayhi*, both pin a time-distorted day to that ascent. **Crossing the vertical axis = crossing into a different temporal regime.**

By contrast, every horizontal event stays within earthly time. Muhammad's Isrāʾ is *laylan* (one earthly night). Lot's family travels in *qiṭʿ min al-layl* (a portion of the night). Solomon's throne arrives faster than *yartadda ṭarfuk* (an earthly micro-interval). Solomon's wind multiplies travel-distance (one month per leg, 34:12) but stays measured in *months* — the earthly calendar unit, not God's day. Cross-link: the time-investigation's `research-year-and-scale.md` documents the same partition from the time side: *sana* (long-painful-uncountable year) is the unit of cross-cosmological duration, while horizontal-axis events use earthly time-units (night, day, month).

The structural prediction: **Idrīs and Jesus, once raised, are not just elsewhere in space but elsewhere on the time-axis**. The Quran does not say this directly, but the architecture demands it. If vertical traversal crosses time-scales (32:5, 70:4), and Idrīs/Jesus have undergone vertical traversal (19:57, 4:158), then they have crossed time-scales. This is the structural reason both can be *alive in tradition* without contradicting the earthly mortality timeline — they are at a different position on the time-axis, not just on the height-axis.

The horizontal corollary: **Muhammad's Isrāʾ does not invoke time-distortion vocabulary**. The journey is `laylan` — one earthly night. Even covering a continent's worth of distance, it stays on the earthly clock. This is a structural feature, not a narrative oversight: if Muhammad had crossed the vertical boundary, the Quran would (by its own pattern) have invoked time-distortion vocabulary. It does not.

## 7. The bi-ʿabdihi comitative pattern is HORIZONTAL-only

`Concept-sry.md` §3 and `narrative-isra-miraj.md` §2.3 establish that every Form-IV occurrence of `asrā` carries a comitative `bi-` prepositional phrase: God moves a human *with* divine motion, not as an independent traveler:

- 11:81 (Lot) — *fa-asri bi-ahlika*
- 15:65 (Lot) — same
- 17:1 (Muhammad) — *asrā bi-ʿabdihi*
- 20:77, 26:52, 44:23 (Moses) — *asri bi-ʿibādī*

The agent of motion is God; the human is the carried.

**This pattern appears nowhere in the vertical register.** Idrīs is just *raised* (`rafaʿnāhu` — direct accusative pronoun, no comitative `bi-`). Jesus is *raised to Me* (`rāfiʿuka ilayya` — direct accusative + directional preposition). The vertical-translation verbs do not use the comitative. The pattern is:

- **Horizontal extraordinary**: God moves *WITH* the human (comitative `bi-`).
- **Vertical extraordinary**: God *raises* the human (direct accusative, no comitative).

Hypothesis: in horizontal extraordinary motion the human is *accompanied* by divine action — God moves alongside, the human is carried. In vertical translation the human is *acted upon* — God lifts. The horizontal verb foregrounds the comitative relation; the vertical verb foregrounds the patient relation. Both are grammars of divine agency, but they encode different *modes*: walking-alongside (sry) vs lifting-up (rfE).

A second observation: the comitative correlates with *survival*. In every `asrā bi-` event the carried party survives. The grammatical comitative is a *protective* construction; the carried party is shielded by being-with-God. Vertical translation does not need this protection because the destination *is* God (*ilayya*). Two different modes of divine action toward the human, encoded in two different grammatical constructions.

The pattern is one more piece of evidence for the lexical apartheid. A Quranic editor wanting to fuse Isrāʾ and Miʿrāj would need either to extend `asrā bi-` to a vertical leg (generating the never-attested `asrā bi-ʿabdihi ila al-samāʾ`) or to use a vertical verb with a comitative `bi-` (generating the never-attested `rafaʿahu bi-` or `aʿraja bi-`). The grammar refuses both moves.

## 8. The Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj fusion is HADITH elaboration

The most consequential test of the axis-split. Devotional memory holds the Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj as a single composite event: Muhammad is taken horizontally Mecca→al-Aqṣā (Isrāʾ leg), then ascends vertically through seven heavens to the Lote-Tree, meets prior prophets at each level, negotiates the prayer-count down from fifty to five with Moses' counsel, returns by morning. **This entire architecture is hadith-anchored, not Quranic.**

What the Quran provides:

1. **17:1** — pure horizontal. *asrā bi-ʿabdihi* from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām to al-Masjid al-Aqṣā. No verb of vertical motion. Purpose-clause *li-nuriyahu min āyātinā*.
2. **53:1-18** — a vision pericope. Four uses of `raʾā` (see). The vertical motion in the passage is the *figure's* descent (`nazlatan ukhrā`, 53:13), not Muhammad's ascent.
3. **Shared syntactic spine.** Both passages use sign-disclosure architecture: *li-nuriyahu min āyātinā* (17:1) and *la-qad raʾā min āyāti rabbihi al-kubrā* (53:18). This is the *internal* hook the tradition uses — but the fusion requires going beyond what the Quran says.

What the Quran does NOT provide:

- **No Erj of Muhammad anywhere.** Of the five `yaʿruju` occurrences (15:14, 32:5, 34:2, 57:4, 70:4), none has Muhammad as subject.
- **No noun *miʿrāj***. The plural `maʿārij` occurs at 70:3 (divine epithet) and 43:33 (counterfactual silver stairs). The singular `miʿrāj` is never used. **The Miʿrāj is a noun the Quran doesn't have, named after a tradition the Quran doesn't narrate, attached to verses that don't use the verb.**
- **No travel from al-Aqṣā upward.** 17:1 ends at al-Aqṣā. The traditional fusion's vertical leg has no Quranic verbal anchor.
- **No staged seven-heaven cosmography.** The seven-heavens schema is in the Quran (67:3, 71:15, 78:12) as a *cosmological structure*; it is never attached to 17:1 or 53 as a *travel itinerary*.
- **No prophet-meetings, no Burāq, no prayer-bargaining.** Entirely hadith.

The data-grounded reading: the Quran has the *vocabulary* for a Miʿrāj (Erj cluster, maʿārij stairways, seven heavens, Lote-Tree of the Boundary) and the *materials* for an Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj fusion (the shared `raʾā / nuriya min āyāt` purpose-architecture). It does not perform the fusion. The hadith corpus performs it by drawing on the Quran's *Erj-vocabulary* without having a Quranic *Erj-verbal anchor* attached to Muhammad. This is a clean case of a tradition extending the scriptural text by recombination of its own materials. We do not take a dogmatic position on whether the Miʿrāj happened — we document only that the Quran does not narrate it with the vocabulary the tradition gives it.

## 9. Angels as cross-axis specialists

Angels are the only beings whose teleportation freely crosses both axes. Compact inventory:

- Vertical down: descend on Laylat al-Qadr (97:4 *tanazzala*); en masse on Day of Judgment (25:25 *nuzzila*); to Maryam (19:17 *arsalnā ilayhā*); revelation to the Prophet (16:102, 26:193).
- Vertical up: ascend with the Spirit (70:4 *taʿruju*); take souls upward (32:11 *yatawaffākum*; 6:93 *bāsiṭū aydīhim*).
- Horizontal: move between Abraham and Lot (11:69 → 11:77; 15:51 → 15:61); position-keeping front-and-back guards (13:11 *muʿaqqibāt*).
- Vertical-position-keeping: carry the Throne (40:7, 69:17 *yaḥmilu ʿarsh rabbika fawqahum yawmaʾidhin thamāniya*); stand in rows at the eschaton (78:38 *yaqūmu ṣaffan*).

The angels appear on every row. **No human appears on more than one row.** Idrīs and Jesus appear once each (vertical up, one-way). Muhammad once (horizontal, asrā). Lot, Moses, Maryam horizontally only. Cave Sleepers in-place only. Solomon never moves himself — he is the receiver-pole around which other agents (jinn, wind, hoopoe, Book-knower) execute *horizontal* transits.

The structural fact: **humans are confined to one axis at a time.** The cross-axis specialism is reserved for angels. This makes angels the operational infrastructure of all Quranic cross-realm motion. The two trans-cosmic prophet cases (Idrīs, Jesus) are unusual precisely because they bypass the angelic mediation — God acts as direct agent (1pl in 19:57; 1sg in 3:55; 3sg with named agent in 4:158). No angel is named as the carrier.

The corollary: **vertical motion is policed; horizontal motion is open.** `Narrative-angels-and-jinn.md` §1: angels descend *bi-idhni rabbihim* (97:4 — by their Lord's permission); jinn who attempt vertical ascent without permission are repelled by missile-stars (37:7-10, 67:5, 72:8). The lowest heaven is *militarized* — its lamps are weapons (*jaʿalnāhā rujūman lil-shayāṭīn*, 67:5). The vertical axis is a *guarded* axis; the horizontal has no equivalent gatekeeping vocabulary. There is no Quranic case of someone being prevented from horizontal motion by a cosmic defense; the only horizontal-prevention is *human pursuit* (Pharaoh chasing Moses), not cosmic interception.

This is consistent with Idrīs's and Jesus's silent translation. They pass through the policed vertical axis without resistance — no missile-fire, no permission-clause, no gatekeeping language. Any human vertical motion in the Quran requires either (a) divine direct agency unmediated, or (b) angelic mediation by permission.

## 10. Cosmological architecture (7 heavens + earth + Throne + Hell)

The two-axis split is anchored in a specific Quranic cosmological geometry.

**The vertical stack:**
- **The Throne** (`al-ʿarsh`) at the apex — God *istawā ʿalā al-ʿarsh* (7:54, 10:3); the Throne is carried by angels (40:7, 69:17 — eight on the Day) and is *fawqahum* (above them, 69:17).
- **The Lote-Tree of the Boundary** (`sidrat al-muntahā`, 53:14) — the limit of created creatures' upward access.
- **The seven heavens** (`sabʿ samāwāt`, 67:3, 71:15, 78:12) stacked in layers (`ṭibāqā`, 67:3). The lowest (`al-samāʾ al-dunyā`, 37:6, 67:5) is the militarized perimeter.
- **The earth** — the horizontal plane.
- **Hell** — below, in the baseline; *brought up* at the eschaton.

**Teleportation respects this geometry:**

1. **Up to God** = the destination of trans-cosmic prophet-translation (Jesus's *ilayya / ilayh*, 3:55, 4:158) and angelic ascent (70:4 *ilayhi*). The Quran's two upward registers converge on the *same terminus* (`ilayhi`), the same preposition, the same directionality.

2. **Down from the heavens** = the source of revelation, angels, rain, provision. `nzl` is one-way for revelation/rain, bidirectional for angels (paired with Erj at 70:4).

3. **The earth-horizon** = the domain of `sry` and `Aty`. Within this domain, motion can be ordinary or extraordinary, but stays in earthly geography and earthly time.

4. **Hell brought near** = a vertical-axis miracle reserved for the eschaton. *wa uḥḍirat al-jaḥīm* (79:36, 81:12); *wa jīʾa yawmaʾidhin bi-jahannam* (89:23). The verb `aḥḍara` (Form IV of `HDr`) is the same verb used eschatologically of deeds being made-present (3:30 *yawma tajidu kullu nafsin mā ʿamilat min khayrin muḥḍaran*). **The same architecture that takes Idrīs upward is also the architecture that brings Hell upward at the end-time.** The vertical axis is the eschatological-translation axis.

5. **The Throne is mobile in principle.** The verb `Hml` (carry) is applied to the Throne (40:7, 69:17). This makes the Throne carryable, parallel to Solomon's throne in 27:38 (*bi-ʿarshihā*). The cosmic architecture has *two* thrones in the same lexical category — one divine, one royal. God's Throne is the original of which Solomon's is the type. The teleportation of Solomon's throne is a creaturely echo of the angelic Throne-carrying.

The cosmological architecture is what makes the two-axis split coherent. There IS a real boundary between heaven and earth (the seven layered heavens, the militarized perimeter, the Lote-Tree). Crossing this boundary requires divine sanction. There is no equivalent boundary on the horizontal plane — Mecca to Jerusalem is far but does not require crossing a cosmic membrane. This is the structural reason horizontal motion can be round-trip while vertical motion is one-way for prophets.

## 11. Implications

**The miracle of Quranic teleportation is not the speed but the boundary-crossing.** This reframes the investigation. The Quran has multiple speed-comparators (`qabla an yartadda ṭarfuk`, 27:40; `ka-lamḥi al-baṣar`, 16:77, 54:50). These are deployed for events that require fast traversal but no boundary-crossing. The throne moves at gaze-speed across the horizontal plane; the Hour comes at blink-speed. Speed is the wrong category for understanding what is theologically at stake. The throne could have arrived in a year and the miracle would still be *arrival from a foreign jurisdiction without intermediate transit*.

What the speed-comparators *prevent* is the perception of a process. The throne is not seen *en route*; the narrative does not describe *traversal*. The arrival is reported as a state-change: it was not here; now it is here-and-settled. This is the same narrative grammar as the angelic visits to Abraham (the angels appear at the door without arrival being narrated; cf. `narrative-angels-and-jinn.md` §3 on "unmarked arrival"). The grammar of arrival without trajectory is the Quran's signature for cross-realm transit, and it applies on both axes.

**Horizontal teleportation is mechanically extraordinary; vertical teleportation is metaphysically extraordinary.** Horizontal events operate within earthly bounds; the miracle is *fast* or *at all*. Vertical events operate *across the cosmic boundary*; the miracle is the *crossing*. The asymmetry of attention in the corpus tracks this: most teleportation is HORIZONTAL — Solomon, Maryam, Lot, Moses, Isrāʾ. Vertical translation is RARE — three verses for the human-vertical canon. **Most teleportation is horizontal; vertical is exceptional and one-way.**

The implication for the Quran's theology of motion: **God moves the world to the prophet on earth (horizontal); God moves the prophet to Himself out of the world (vertical).** Horizontal is the register of *gathering signs* for prophets-still-in-mission. Vertical is the register of *taking the prophet out of mission*. Muhammad's Isrāʾ — horizontal — fits the first pattern: he is shown signs and returned. Idrīs's and Jesus's raisings — vertical — fit the second: removed from earthly mission and not returned in the canonical text.

The Quran refuses the Isrāʾ-Miʿrāj fusion because the fusion would put Muhammad on the wrong axis. If the Isrāʾ were extended into a vertical Miʿrāj, Muhammad would be the *third* trans-cosmic prophet — and his earthly mission would be implicitly closed by the same architecture that closes Idrīs's and Jesus's. The Quran preserves Muhammad's continuing earthly mission by holding him to the horizontal axis. The hadith tradition's fusion — which keeps the return — is the tradition's way of having both: a vertical leg (cosmic significance) plus a return-to-earthly-mission. The Quran simply gives the horizontal asrā and lets the vertical remain at vision-level (53's `raʾā` events), without performing the translation.

## 12. Rabbit holes for Phase 3

1. **The convergent destination.** Both vertical-up registers (prophet-translation 3:55/4:158 and angelic-ascent 70:4) terminate at *ilayhi*. What is the relation between "to Him" and "the Throne" (`al-ʿarsh`)? Is the Throne a literal location or a relational designator? Worth a focused investigation cross-referencing every `ilayhi`-of-vertical-motion with every `ʿarsh`-occurrence.

2. **Idrīs vs Jesus asymmetry.** `Narrative-idris-jesus.md` §9 argues the difference (bare `rafaʿa` vs `tawaffā + rafaʿa` stack) is *polemical context shaping the verbal stack*. Test corpus-wide: bare verb where consensual; verb-stack where contested. Abraham at 21:69 (no counter-claim) gets bare verbs; Moses at the Red Sea (with competing Egyptian narrative) gets a stack.

3. **Hell brought near as a vertical-axis miracle.** *uḥḍirat al-jaḥīm* (79:36, 81:12) and *jīʾa yawmaʾidhin bi-jahannam* (89:23) use position-change-without-travel verbs. Cross-link with `concept-HDr.md`: is the "bringing-near" of Hell structurally parallel to the "bringing-near" of the throne in 27:40? Both use the same verb-class. Worth a focused study of `aḥḍara`-events as a *meta-axis* category.

4. **Solomon's wind as the borderline case.** Why does the wind get a 30× multiplier rather than a `qabla an yartadda ṭarfuk` arrival? Hypothesis: the wind is a *natural force* subjugated (sxr) to a prophet, not a cosmic-instrument like the Book-knower's knowledge; the natural-force category is bounded to earthly time even at acceleration. Worth a sxr-study to test.

5. **The Spirit as universal vertical-traverser.** The Spirit appears in both descent (16:102, 26:193, 97:4) and ascent (70:4) contexts, and as agent of Maryam's annunciation (19:17). It is the only individual entity (singular) the Quran credits with bidirectional vertical traffic. Cross-link `concept-Erj.md` rabbit hole 4.

6. **The `ilayhi` operator as theological compass.** Vertical translation in the Quran is "going to a person" (God), not "going to a place." Worth a corpus-wide study of `ilayhi`-of-motion, distinguishing physical motion (ascent) from relational motion (turning-to-God in repentance).

7. **The `makān + adjective` construction as a teleportation marker.** The Quran uses *makānan + adjective* a few times: *makānan sharqiyyā* (Maryam, 19:16), *makānan qaṣiyyā* (Maryam, 19:22), *makānan ʿaliyyā* (Idrīs, 19:57), *makānan suwā* (Moses-meeting-place, 20:58). All are destinations of withdrawal or translation. The places are not toponymic; they are functional. Is this a *teleportation-destination grammar*?

8. **Muhammad's vertical absence as a structural feature, not a gap.** The Quran systematically does *not* give Muhammad a vertical translation event. Hypothesis: because Muhammad's mission is to live and die on earth (*innaka mayyitun wa-innahum mayyitūn*, 39:30), and a vertical translation would compromise this mortality-architecture. Worth investigating every Quranic verse that *limits* Muhammad's status — the *qul innamā ana basharun mithlukum* (18:110) cluster — and how it correlates with the absence of vertical-translation vocabulary for Muhammad.

9. **Negative space — no positive-valence prophet-descent verb.** The Quran has no narrative of a prophet *coming down* from a raised position. Adam *falls* (`hbT`) — but this is negative-valence descent into a lower state. The systematic absence of a positive-valence prophet-descent verb is striking. Cross-reference with the hadith tradition's `nzl` of Jesus at the eschaton: the tradition fills the gap with a verb the Quran reserves for non-prophet downward motion. Worth tracing how the eschatological-Jesus tradition develops its `nzl`-vocabulary against the Quranic absence.
