# Research: The Integrated Multi-Axis Failure Typology

## 1. Headline Finding

The Quran's failure-vocabulary is not 32 parallel sins or 6 unrelated clusters. It is a **single failure-event** projected onto **six independent axes** (TIME, DOMAIN, SEVERITY, VOCABULARY-CLUSTER, RESPONSE, DIVINE-ACTION). The 6 root-clusters identified in Phase 1 are not 6 *kinds* of failure — they are 6 *stages* of a single temporally-ordered failure-arc that runs: **jhl/gfl → ErD → Dll → kbr/kfr/$rk → qsw/xtm → Tgy/bgy/fsd → hlk/xsr/xyb**. At any stage, RESPONSE bifurcates the trajectory into the Adam-type (admit → forgiveness → reset) and Iblīs-type (defend → progression → destruction). This is the integrated map onto which every Pharaoh, every Qārūn, every Thamūd, and every individual sinner is locatable.

---

## 2. The 6-Axis Typology — Overview

| AXIS | What it answers | Range |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| **1 · TIME** | When in the failure-process? | initial heedlessness → orientation-loss → defensive posture → heart-disablement → behavioral expression → consequence-arrival |
| **2 · DOMAIN** | Where in the person does the failure live? | heart / mind / will / behavior / social-cosmic / outcome |
| **3 · SEVERITY** | How serious? | recoverable Adam-type → moderate (dhanb/ithm) → severe (Tgy/kbr) → cardinal-reversible (kfr) → cardinal-irreversible ($rk) → worst-rated (nfq) |
| **4 · VOCABULARY-CLUSTER** | Which root-family? | loss / orientation / belief / attitude / sin / heart |
| **5 · RESPONSE** | How does the actor respond once exposed? | Adam-type (admit + repent) vs Iblīs-type (defend + persist) |
| **6 · DIVINE-ACTION** | What does God do? | forgive (ghfr / tāba ʿalayhi) → harden (qsw / xtm) → respite-then-destroy (mhl + hlk) → curse (laʿna) |

The axes are **independent** — they cross-cut. A single failure has a coordinate on every axis. Two failures can match on 5 axes but diverge on the 6th and have totally different theological verdicts. This is what Iblīs and Adam demonstrate: same act-class (disobedience to direct command), same cluster-axis (kbr vs. ʿaṣā/ghawā), same domain (will), but RESPONSE-axis differs — and the difference rewrites the entire verdict.

## 3. Pharaoh — A Single Failure on All 6 Axes

Pharaoh is the test case because he hits every axis at maximum:

**TIME:** Spans his entire life. Pharaoh is introduced [28:4] as already-transgressing (*ʿalā fī al-arḍ*) before Moses arrives — so his TIME-axis coordinate is "complete arc, fully traversed." The Moses encounter occupies the middle stages (rejection → defensive doubling-down → heart-hardened → tyranny intensifies); the death-bed [10:90] is the consequence-arrival stage. Pharaoh's failure is not a moment but a CUMULATIVE compression of all 7 stages into one biography.

**DOMAIN:** All five domains active simultaneously. Heart: sealed by his own pattern of refusal. Mind: he reasons in bad faith — tells Moses "build me a tower so I may look at Moses' god" [28:38] while claiming he himself is god. Will: refuses every command (release the Israelites; submit to the signs). Behavior: child-killing [28:4], plague-cycle reneging [7:135], pursuit *baghyan wa-ʿadwan* [10:90]. Social-cosmic: he is *mina al-mufsidīn* — a cosmic-scale corrupter [28:4]. Outcome: drowned, corpse preserved as a SIGN [10:92].

**SEVERITY:** Cosmic-cardinal. *anā rabbukum al-aʿlā* [79:24] is the maximal $rk — claiming a Divine Name (*al-Aʿlā*) as one's own. This is not just $rk-as-misallocation; it is $rk-as-self-deification, which exhausts the upper bound of the SEVERITY axis.

**VOCABULARY-CLUSTER:** Hits all six. Tgy: *idhhab ilā firʿawna innahu ṭaghā* [79:17]. kbr: *istakbarū fī al-arḍ* [29:39]. kfr: *kadhdhabū bi-āyātinā* [7:136]. fsd: *kāna mina al-mufsidīn* [10:91]. hlk: *aghraqnāhum* [7:136]. ẓālimūn: standard descriptor throughout. Pharaoh is the FULL-SPECTRUM failure-figure — used precisely because he exhibits every cluster.

**RESPONSE:** Iblīs-type throughout. Defensive at every confrontation. Promises during plague, reneges when relieved [7:135]. The death-bed *āmantu* [10:90] is rejected with one-word divine interrogative *āl-āna?* — "Now?" The Quranic principle: faith offered when consequence is visible is not faith but observation. RESPONSE-axis verdict: maximally Iblīs-type.

**DIVINE-ACTION:** Hardened (he hardened himself; God confirmed it) → respite (a full plague-cycle is given) → destruction (drowned) → corpse preserved as sign for posterity [10:92]. The DIVINE-ACTION mirrors RESPONSE: Iblīs-response gets Iblīs-treatment, with one signature feature — the corpse-as-monument, unique to Pharaoh.

The 6 axes give 6 INDEPENDENT pieces of information about Pharaoh's failure. Removing any one reduces analytical resolution. The typology is not redundant.

## 4. The TIME Axis — Failure as a Chain

The directional-failure root agent established the following sequence (and Phase 1 across other clusters confirmed it from multiple angles):

| Stage | Vocabulary | What happens |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **0 · COGNITIVE ABSENCE** | jhl, gfl | The default. No reminder has yet been received; or it has been received but not processed. The "ghāfilūn" are 77% nominal — a STATE, not an act. |
| **1 · REJECTION** | ErD (aʿraḍa) | The reminder comes; the actor turns away. ErD's dominant lemma *aʿraḍa* (Form IV active verb, 32 occ) is the active turning-of-the-shoulder. |
| **2 · DEVIATION** | Dll | Direction is now set wrong. *ḍalla* (intransitive) for the human's wandering; *aḍalla* (causative IV, 64 occ) for divine or satanic agency completing the misorientation. |
| **3 · DEFENSIVE POSTURE** | kbr, kfr, $rk, jHd | Once direction is set wrong, the actor refuses to course-correct. *istakbara* (40 occ) is the cosmic signature — Iblīs, Pharaoh, Thamūd elite all use it. kfr (covering) and $rk (mis-allocating) are the explicit theological forms. jHd is the rarest and most damning — denial against one's own internal certainty [27:14]. |
| **4 · HEART-DISABLEMENT** | qsw, xtm, qfl, mrD, glf, knn | The divine response to persistent refusal: heart hardens, seals, locks, gets diseased. The causal chain is consistent: PRIOR FAILURE → HARDENING (2:74's *thumma qasat* — "then" — marks the consequent nature). |
| **5 · BEHAVIORAL EXPRESSION** | Tgy, bgy, srf, fjr, fsd, Zlm-of-others | The disabled heart now produces visible action: tyranny (Tgy), insolence/rebellion (bgy), excess (srf), wickedness (fjr), corruption-on-earth (fsd), oppression of others (Zlm-of-others). |
| **6 · CONSEQUENCE** | hlk, xsr, xyb, bwr, DyE, bTl | The end-state. hlk (active divine destruction, 91% causative-IV "We destroyed"); xsr (transactional self-loss); xyb (frustrated potential); bwr (passive withering); DyE (wasted deeds); bTl (ontological void). |

The chain is not strictly chronological for every individual — but it IS the structure the Quran assumes when explaining how a person/people arrives at hlk. Each Phase-1 root-cluster is anchored to a different stage. The vocabulary is **temporally structured**, which is why "the 6 clusters" feel related but distinct.

**Recursive feature:** the chain can re-enter at any point. The hypocrites (munāfiqūn) traverse stages 1-4 *repeatedly* — entering and exiting belief — until [63:3] "they believed, then they disbelieved, so their hearts were sealed." Hypocrisy is failure on a LOOP, not a single transit.

## 5. The DOMAIN Axis — Heart → Mind → Will → Behavior → Outcome

The TIME axis runs forward through the person; the DOMAIN axis is a CROSS-SECTION of where in the person the failure currently lives.

| DOMAIN | Failure-roots that live here | Key formula |
|--------|------------------------------|-------------|
| **HEART (qalb)** | qsw, xtm, qfl, mrD, glf, knn — entire heart-failure cluster | "khatama Allāhu ʿalā qulūbihim" [2:7]; "fī qulūbihim maraḍ" [2:10] |
| **MIND / REASONING** | jhl; misapplied-qiyās of Iblīs ("anā khayrun minhu" [7:12]); Qārūn's *innamā ūtītuhu ʿalā ʿilm ʿindī* [28:78] | Reason is not absent — it is applied to the wrong axioms |
| **WILL / CHOICE** | kbr (refusing to bow), abā (refusing the command — Iblīs at 2:34), the *yaʾbā* class | "abā wa-stakbara" [2:34] |
| **BEHAVIOR** | bgy, Tgy, srf, fjr, Zlm-of-others, the act of fasād | *baghyan wa-ʿadwan* [10:90] for Pharaoh's pursuit |
| **SOCIAL / COSMIC** | fsd (corruption *fī al-arḍ* — on earth); the destroyed-peoples scale | *al-mufsidūna fī al-arḍ* — failure that propagates |
| **OUTCOME** | hlk, xsr, xyb, bwr, DyE, bTl — entire loss/destruction cluster | "khasirū anfusahum" [6:12 et al.] |

**Direction through the person:** Heart-failures PRECEDE behavior-failures; behavior-failures PRECEDE consequence-failures. This is why the Quran's diagnostic strategy is to look at the heart first ([2:7] before any behavior is described) and the consequence last ([7:136] after the whole Pharaoh narrative).

**The hypocrite's domain-signature** is unique: heart (mrD), mind (yukhādiʿūna — they think they are deceiving), will (split — public faith / private rejection), behavior (cowardice + masjid ḍirār), with NO destruction in this life — the consequence is deferred to *al-dark al-asfal* [4:145]. This is why nfq is rated worst: the OUTCOME-domain is hidden until eschatology, allowing maximum in-life damage.

## 6. The SEVERITY Axis — The Failure Gradient

The Quran orders failures by severity, but the ordering is non-monotonic with respect to the act:

| Tier | Type | Marker verses |
|------|------|---------------|
| **Worst-rated** | nfq (hypocrisy) | [4:145] *al-dark al-asfal min al-nār* — lowest depth of Fire |
| **Cardinal-irreversible** | $rk (associating partners with God) | [4:48 = 4:116] *innallāha lā yaghfiru an yushraka bihi* — never forgiven |
| **Cardinal-reversible** | kfr (covering received truth) | Forgivable on conversion; included in *mā dūna dhālik* [4:48] |
| **Severe** | Tgy (Pharaoh-tier transgression), kbr (Iblīs-tier arrogance) | *idhhab ilā firʿawna innahu ṭaghā* [79:17] |
| **Moderate** | dhanb, ithm, sūʾ — everyday wrong | Forgivable through repentance and good deeds |
| **Recoverable-default** | ẓulm-an-nafs (wronging oneself, Adam-type) | [7:23] Adam's confession formula |

**The non-monotonicity:** nfq outranks $rk in severity DESPITE $rk being unforgivable. The reasoning (compressed from Phase 1 belief-failure agent): kfr is dishonest with God about what one knows; $rk is a category-violation about reality; nfq is dishonest with God AND with the community while *using community trust as cover*. The ranking weighs deceit + parasitism above explicit metaphysical error.

**Severity is not act-determined alone — it is RESPONSE-modified.** A "moderate" sin (dhanb) held without repentance becomes severe. A "cardinal" sin ($rk) reversed by repentance becomes recoverable (this is why every prophet's call is "leave $rk and turn"). The SEVERITY axis cannot be read off the act-vocabulary alone; it must be cross-referenced with the RESPONSE-axis.

This explains the apparent paradox of [3:135]: those who *fāḥisha aw ẓalamū anfusahum* (commit indecency or wrong themselves) are praised IF they remember God and repent. The act-content places them in the moderate-tier; the response moves them to the recoverable-default tier — and they end up in the saved class.

## 7. The VOCABULARY-CLUSTER Axis — The 6 Clusters as 6 Stages

Tabulating the Phase-1 root-clusters with their primary TIME-stage:

| Phase-1 cluster | Roots | Primary TIME-stage | Primary DOMAIN |
|----------------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| Loss / destruction | xsr, xyb, hlk, bwr, DyE, bTl | Stage 6 (CONSEQUENCE) | OUTCOME |
| Directional / orientation | jhl, gfl, ErD, Dll, nks | Stages 0-2 (ABSENCE → REJECTION → DEVIATION) | MIND + WILL |
| Belief failure | kfr, $rk, nfq, jHd | Stage 3 (DEFENSIVE / CARDINAL) | WILL + STANCE |
| Attitude / transgression | kbr, Tgy, bgy, srf, fjr | Stage 3 (POSTURE) → Stage 5 (BEHAVIOR) | WILL → BEHAVIOR |
| Sin / corruption | Zlm, sūʾ, fsq, fsd, ithm, dhanb | Stage 5 (BEHAVIORAL EXPRESSION) | BEHAVIOR + SOCIAL |
| Heart-disablement | qsw, xtm, qfl, mrD, glf, knn | Stage 4 (DIVINE INSULATION) | HEART |

Three observations:

1. **The 6 clusters ARE 6 stages of the failure-arc.** They are not random groupings — they are the Quran's vocabulary for different *moments* in the failure-life-arc. This is why they feel parallel but distinct.

2. **The hub-position of kfr is structural.** Kfr appears in 30 of 31 co-occurrence pairings (failure-structurals). This is because Stage 3 (defensive posture) is the HINGE — once a person is here, every other stage either led to it (0-2) or follows from it (4-6). Kfr's centrality in the co-occurrence graph mirrors its centrality in the temporal arc.

3. **The 6-axis typology subsumes the 32-root catalog.** Every individual failure-root locates onto a TIME-stage, a DOMAIN, and a SEVERITY tier. The clusters are labels for STAGE-DOMAIN bundles. There are not 32 independent failures; there are 32 vocabulary-instruments distributed across 6 stages and 6 domains.

## 8. The RESPONSE Axis — Iblīs vs Adam (cross-reference)

Covered fully in the Iblīs-Adam narrative. Compressed here:

- **Iblīs-type response:** *defends* the failure by reasoning. Iblīs's *anā khayrun minhu* [7:12] is intelligent qiyās applied from wrong axioms. No admission, no repentance, persistent across eternity. Result: cursed, expelled, classified as kāfir.
- **Adam-type response:** *admits* the failure immediately. *qālā rabbanā ẓalamnā anfusanā* [7:23] — first-person plural confession, explicit naming of what was done (ẓulm-an-nafs), no rationalization. Result: forgiven, *ʿallama* (taught) the words of repentance, descended to earth as a vicegerent, not as a curse.

**The RESPONSE-axis is the OVERARCHING classifier.** Every other axis is cross-cut by it. A heart can be hardened (DOMAIN: heart) BUT respond by repenting and being softened ([39:22-23] contrasts hardened hearts with those whose skins shudder and hearts soften at God's remembrance). A cardinal $rk failure (SEVERITY: maximal) can be reversed by repentance (Pharaoh's magicians at [7:120-122] convert from $rk to īmān in the same scene — and Pharaoh's threats cannot move them). The TIME-axis can be reset to Stage 0 by the Adam-type response at any stage prior to Stage 6 (consequence-arrival) — which is why the only response barred is the death-bed one [4:18, 10:91].

The response-axis carries the moral weight; the other axes carry the diagnostic content.

## 9. The DIVINE-ACTION Axis — God's Response Mirrors Human Response

The DIVINE-ACTION axis is the OUTPUT side of the typology. It mirrors the RESPONSE-axis with mathematical precision:

| Human RESPONSE | DIVINE-ACTION | Quranic markers |
|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Adam-type (admit + repent) | Forgiveness (*ghfr*); turning-toward (*tāba ʿalayhim*); guidance (*hadāhu*) | [7:23 → 7:24] Adam confesses → descends with promise of guidance; [2:37] Allah accepts repentance |
| Iblīs-type (defend + persist) | Hardening (qsw / xtm of heart); cursing (*laʿna*); respite then destruction (*mhl* + *hlk*) | [2:34-35] Iblīs's curse; [5:13] God MADE their hearts hard after covenant-breaking |
| Sustained Iblīs-type at scale | Civilizational destruction with tailored means | [69:6-7] ʿĀd's screaming wind; [15:74] Lūṭ's inverted cities |
| Death-bed Iblīs-type | Acknowledgment REJECTED | [10:91] *āl-āna?* — "Now?" to drowning Pharaoh |

**Two iron justice-constraints frame the whole axis:**
1. **No punishment without warning** — [17:15] *wa-mā kunnā muʿadhdhibīna ḥattā nabʿatha rasūlā*; [28:59] God never destroys towns until messengers are sent. The destroyed-peoples narratives ALL include the prophet-warning stage.
2. **The "yashāʾu" sovereignty** — God ultimately decides who gets the Adam-treatment vs Iblīs-treatment. This is the residual mystery the typology doesn't resolve. Even within the typology's clean structure, the *adalla man yashāʾu wa-yahdī man yashāʾu* clause [16:93] keeps human typologizing under divine prerogative.

## 10. The Integrated Mental Model — The Grid

```
FAILURE-ARC (TIME-axis):
  Stage 0: Cognitive absence ............... jhl, gfl
  Stage 1: Reminder comes — REJECTION ...... ErD (aʿraḍa)
  Stage 2: Direction set wrong ............. Dll (ḍalla, aḍalla)
  Stage 3: Defensive posture ............... kbr / kfr / $rk / jHd
  Stage 4: Heart-disablement ............... qsw / xtm / qfl / mrD / glf / knn
  Stage 5: Behavioral expression ........... Tgy / bgy / srf / fjr / fsd / Zlm-of-others
  Stage 6: Consequence ..................... hlk / xsr / xyb / bwr / DyE / bTl

AT EVERY STAGE — RESPONSE BIFURCATES:
  Adam-type  (admit + repent)  → forgiveness  → reset to Stage 0 (with new wisdom)
  Iblīs-type (defend + persist) → progression  → next stage of failure-arc

DIVINE-ACTION (mirrors RESPONSE):
  Adam-type  → ghfr / tāba ʿalayhi / hidāya
  Iblīs-type → qsw / xtm / mhl + hlk / laʿna

FAILURE-NARRATIVES (instantiations on the grid):
  Cosmic original (Iblīs / Adam)           — establishes the binary itself
  Cosmic-power archetypes (Phr / Hāmān / Qārūn) — power-domain instantiations
  Historical destroyed peoples (ʿĀd, Thamūd, Lūṭ, etc.) — collective Iblīs-type at scale
  Medinan-intra-communal (munāfiqūn)       — failure inside the believing community (looped Stage 1-4)
  Theological deviations (Christians/Jews) — failure-with-revelation (covenant Iblīs-type)
```

**Every failure in the Quran can be located on this grid.** Pharaoh: full traversal, all stages, Iblīs-type response, drowned. Adam: stages 1-3 partial, Adam-type response, forgiven. Israelite covenant-breakers: stages 0-5 with covenant access, mixed response, partial destruction. Munāfiqūn: stages 1-4 on a loop, Iblīs-type response under Adam-type appearance, deferred consequence.

## 11. Cross-Investigation — Failure-Classes Mirror Success-Classes

The success investigation surfaced 20+ classes of "the saved" with a 3-axis hierarchy: **dispositional** (muflihūn, abrār, ṣāliḥūn — by trait), **favored** (4:69 nabiyyīn / ṣiddīqīn / shuhadāʾ / ṣāliḥīn — by office), **cosmic** (56:7-14 sābiqūn / aṣḥāb al-yamīn — by proximity to God).

Failure has the parallel triple-hierarchy:

| Success-axis | Failure mirror |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Dispositional** classes (muflihūn, ṣāliḥūn — trait-based) | **Behavioral-failure classes**: ẓālimūn, fāsiqūn, mufsidūn, mujrimūn, musrifūn — defined by repeated act-pattern |
| **Favored** classes (4:69 — office-based) | **Cardinal-failure classes**: kāfirūn, mushrikūn, munāfiqūn — defined by stance toward revelation |
| **Cosmic** classes (56 right-hand companions) | **Cosmic-failure positions**: aṣḥāb al-mashʾama (left-hand companions, 56:9, 90:19); aṣḥāb al-nār; aṣḥāb al-saʿīr |

**The mirroring is exact.** 56:7-14 names BOTH right-hand and left-hand companions in the same passage — the cosmic-axis is bilaterally symmetric. The dispositional-failure classes (ẓālimūn etc.) are ALL agentive nouns, parallel to muflihūn — class identities, not events. The cardinal-failure classes (kāfirūn etc.) parallel the favored-success classes (nabiyyīn etc.) in being defined by STANCE toward revelation.

**One asymmetry:** the success-side has the *muqarrabūn* (foremost, brought-near) at the top — a positive superlative with no exact failure mirror. There is no "closest to Hell" class named with the same intensity. The failure-side bottom is *al-dark al-asfal min al-nār* — a LOCATION, not a class — occupied by hypocrites. The success-pinnacle is RELATIONAL (closeness to God); the failure-pinnacle is SPATIAL-REMOTE. The asymmetry is theological: closeness to God is the success-currency; failure does not have a corresponding "closeness" — only distance.

## 12. Negative Space

- **No vocabulary for "morally neutral"** — the typology is binary in modality (Adam-type vs Iblīs-type response). There is no third response that produces a third outcome.
- **No "tragic" failure** — every Quranic failure is RESPONSE-attributable. There is no failure caused by genuine ignorance that the actor cannot escape; jhl (ignorance) is a STATE before the reminder, not an excuse after it.
- **No accumulation-without-response** — the SEVERITY axis is not just "amount of sin." A million sins repented are forgiven; one shirk persisted-in is unforgivable. Quantity without response-axis is meaningless.
- **No collective Adam-type response at civilizational scale** — every destroyed people is destroyed AS a people. There is no narrative of a city collectively repenting and being spared, except Yūnus's people [10:98] — the single named exception, marked precisely by its rarity.
- **No "tier" between forgiven and destroyed** — once consequence-arrival (Stage 6) happens, there is no partial restoration. The grid has no intermediate state between *tāba ʿalayhi* and *aghraqnā*.

## 13. Verses for Direct Study

1. **[2:34]** *fasajadū illā Iblīsa abā wa-stakbara wa-kāna mina al-kāfirīn* — the cosmic verb-chain abā → istakbara → kufr; the typology's RESPONSE-axis origin.
2. **[7:23]** *qālā rabbanā ẓalamnā anfusanā* — Adam's confession formula; the alternative to Iblīs's defense.
3. **[10:90-92]** Pharaoh's death-bed faith REJECTED with *āl-āna?* and the corpse-as-sign coda — the consequence-arrival barrier on the RESPONSE axis.
4. **[4:48]** *innallāha lā yaghfiru an yushraka bihi wa-yaghfiru mā dūna dhālik* — the SEVERITY-axis hard ceiling.
5. **[2:7-10]** The triple-class opening: muttaqūn (working heart) / kāfirūn (sealed heart) / munāfiqūn (diseased heart) — the DOMAIN-axis applied to three populations in 4 ayahs.
6. **[91:7-10]** *qad aflaḥa man zakkāhā / wa-qad khāba man dassāhā* — the binary with falāḥ (success) vs khāba (frustrated potential, xyb-cluster); the OUTCOME side of the integrated grid.
7. **[63:3]** *āmanū thumma kafarū fa-ṭubiʿa ʿalā qulūbihim fa-hum lā yafqahūn* — the looped failure-arc that produces nfq's worst-rated status.

## 14. Open Questions / Rabbit Holes

- **Stage-skipping cases** — does any Quranic failure jump stages (e.g., Stage 0 → Stage 6 without intermediates)? The destroyed-peoples narratives compress the full arc into a few generations; individual cases like Qārūn may compress further. Worth a dedicated check.
- **The Yūnus-people exception** [10:98] — the only case of collective Adam-type response. What about that community made the bifurcation go the other way? Investigate the *kashafnā ʿanhum ʿadhāba al-khizyi fī al-ḥayāti al-dunyā* formula.
- **Apparent Iblīs-type response without destruction** — the People of the Book are repeatedly diagnosed as Iblīs-type (covenant-breaking, kbr, kfr-after-īmān) without civilizational destruction. Why does the destruction-stage hold for ʿĀd/Thamūd but defer for Israel?
- **The mukhlasīn exemption** [38:83 — Iblīs's own admission] — Iblīs declares he cannot reach the *mukhlasīn*. Is there a parallel exemption-class on the failure-side that NO Adam-type repentance can reach (a permanent Iblīs-class)? Cross-reference with the *kāna mina al-kāfirīn* tag.
- **The $rk-as-ẓulm-ʿaẓīm framing** [31:13] — shirk is named the *greatest ẓulm*, putting the SEVERITY-axis maximum INSIDE the wrong-doing cluster (Zlm) rather than as a separate category. Does this mean the SEVERITY axis is a sub-axis of DOMAIN (Zlm of God-allocation)?
- **The DIVINE-ACTION axis's "yashāʾu" residual** — every formula ends with *liman yashāʾu*. Is the typology fully predictive, or is there always a sovereign exception built in? Map the *yashāʾu* clauses across the failure-vocabulary.
- **Hypocrisy as the typology's edge case** — nfq breaks the standard arc by LOOPING. Is hypocrisy the only repeated-traversal failure, or are there others (e.g., the "wavering between two" of [4:143] *mudhabdhabīn*)? A focused study on looped failure-states would test whether the linear arc is the full picture or just the modal one.
