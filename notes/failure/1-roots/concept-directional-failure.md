# Directional / Attentional Failure — Five Roots

## 1. Headline Finding

The five roots Dll, ErD, gfl, nks, and jhl are not five separate failures — they are five postures of the same underlying deficit: orientation collapse. Where the success investigation found that guidance (hdy) is divine and produces right-orientation, these five are its mirror: all five describe failure as an attentional or directional condition, not a capability deficit. The Quran names five failure-postures: going toward the wrong destination (Dll), turning the shoulder away from the message (ErD), absent attention (gfl), collapsed head-down submission to shame (nks), and ignorance as a civilizational condition (jhl). Together they constitute the Quran's anatomy of how beings fail to orient.

## 3. The Dll/hdy Divine Binary

The success investigation established: God guides (yahdī man yashāʾ), the guided are the successful. The failure investigation completes the circuit: God also leads astray, and the led-astray find no help.

**[Panel 1 — 18:17]**  
مَن يَهۡدِ ٱللَّهُ فَهُوَ ٱلۡمُهۡتَدِۖ وَمَن يُضۡلِلۡ فَلَن تَجِدَ لَهُۥ وَلِيّاً مُّرۡشِداً  
Saheeh: "He whom Allah guides is the [rightly] guided, but he whom He leaves astray — never will you find for him a protecting guide."  
Khattab: "Whoever Allah guides is truly guided. But whoever He leaves to stray, you will never find for them a guiding mentor."

The structure: yahdī → muhtadī / yuḍlil → lā walī murshid. Categorical partition, not a gradient. Parallel at 7:178 replaces "no guiding ally" with "those are the losers" (al-khāsirūn).

Dll/hdy co-occurrence (52 ayahs, 31% of all Dll) is the corpus's strongest directional binary. At 6:125: God expands the breast for Islam (guidance) / makes it tight like ascending into the sky (misguidance). The failure is interior — chest compression, not just a wrong turn.

The causative aḍalla (64 occ) means the Quran does not reserve misguidance for autonomous human decision. Both guidance and misguidance are divinely framed, relational, pointing upward before outward.

## 4. ErD vs. kfr — The Gentle vs. Active Rejection Distinction

The hypothesis holds: ErD is the GESTURAL rejection (turning the shoulder); kfr is the COGNITIVE rejection (active denial). Data supports the distinction.

ErD's signature: "fa-aʿraḍa aktharu-hum fa-hum lā yasmaʿūn" [41:4] — most turn away, so they do not hear. ErD produces perceptual shutdown: posture precedes incapacity. At 20:124: ErD ʿan dhikrī (turning from My remembrance) → maʿīsha ḍanka (depressed/constricted life) → raised blind at resurrection. ErD triggers a cascade across time.

kfr co-occurs with Dll in 28 ayahs (17%) but with ErD in only 6 (8%). You can aʿraḍa before you kfr — ErD is the earlier-stage, pre-cognitive failure.

At 54:2: "whenever they see a sign, they turn away and say, 'Same old magic.'" ErD is paired with an interpretive reframe — but the body turns first. The Quran encodes a sequence: posture precedes rationalization.

The polysemy is structurally ironic: ʿarḍ (width) describes Paradise ("its width is the heavens and earth" — 3:133) using the same root as the gesture of refusing what is offered.

## 5. gfl + ErD — The Compound Failure

The most important co-occurrence in this cluster: gfl and ErD appear together in the same ayah and have each other in their co-root lists. The canonical verse is 21:1:

**[Panel 2 — 21:1]**  
ٱقۡتَرَبَ لِلنَّاسِ حِسَابُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ فِي غَفۡلَةٍ مُّعۡرِضُونَ  
Saheeh: "[The time of] their account has approached for the people, while they are in heedlessness turning away."  
Khattab: "The time of people's judgment has drawn near, yet they are heedlessly turning away."

The grammar layers the two failure modes: fī ghafla (in heedlessness — a structural state) + muʿriḍūn (turning away — ongoing active participle). gfl is the background condition; ErD is the foreground action.

At 7:179, the deepest analysis: hearts that do not understand, eyes that do not see, ears that do not hear — "like cattle, indeed more astray; those are al-ghāfilūn." Every perceptual organ is present but non-functional. "More astray than cattle" directly connects gfl to Dll: heedlessness is a form of being lost.

The shared co-root of both gfl and ErD is *kr (dhikr/remembrance). Both failures are defined in relation to a specific stimulus: the reminder. They are not general capability deficits — they are orientation-failures toward the divine signal.

## 6. nks — Head-Down Regression

Three occurrences, three distinct images of inversion, all Meccan:

**21:65** — After Abraham destroys the idols, his accusers briefly concede "you know these idols do not speak" — then nukisū ʿalā ruʾūsihim: they flip back to "you know they cannot talk!" Intellectual regression mid-argument.

**[Panel 3 — 32:12]**  
وَلَوۡ تَرَىٰٓ إِذِ ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ نَاكِسُواْ رُءُوسِهِمۡ عِندَ رَبِّهِمۡ  
Saheeh: "If you could but see when the criminals are hanging their heads before their Lord."  
Khattab: "If only you could see the wicked hanging their heads in shame before their Lord."

Nākisū ruʾūsihim is the body's confession: the orientation maintained in life — upright, turned away — was wrong. At the moment reality is finally unavoidable, the body inverts.

**36:68** — "Whoever We extend in life, We reverse them in creation (nunakkishu fī al-khalq) — will they not understand?" Old age as developmental regression, closing with a-fa-lā yaʿqilūn: nks is a sign that should produce understanding.

The co-root with rās (head — 67% of nks ayahs) is definitional: the head is the directional organ. When it goes down, direction collapses.

## 7. jhl + al-Jāhiliyya as Historical Category

The jhl lemma table reveals a gradient from behavioral to civilizational:

- **Jahāla** (4 occ) — episodic ignorance: bi-jahāla (in rashness — 4:17, 6:54, 16:119, 49:6). Forgivable; God accepts repentance from those who act in it.
- **Jāhil / al-jāhilūn** (10 occ) — ignorance as identity. At 7:199: "turn away from al-jāhilīn" — a social group to be disengaged from, not reasoned with.
- **Jahūl** (1 occ, 33:72) — intensive: ẓalūman jahūlān (greatly oppressive, deeply ignorant). Harshest jhl epithet.
- **Al-jāhiliyya** (4 occ: 3 PN + 1 N) — the pre-Islamic civilizational order, tagged as proper noun.

The four jāhiliyya domains:
- **3:154** — ẓann al-jāhiliyya (assumption/epistemology)
- **5:50** — ḥukm al-jāhiliyya (law/judgment)
- **33:33** — tabarruj al-jāhiliyya al-ūlā (social performance)
- **48:26** — ḥamiyyat al-jāhiliyya (tribal psychology)

Four domains of jāhiliyya: epistemology (ẓann), law (ḥukm), social performance (tabarruj), tribal psychology (ḥamiyya). The critique is structural — reform of all four simultaneously.

The contrast at 5:50 is decisive: the opposite of jāhiliyya-judgment is not better judgment — it is certainty (yūqinūn). Ignorance-civilization operates on assumption (ẓann); Quranic civilization operates on yaqīn.

## 8. The Five-Fold Orientation-Failure Architecture

From the success investigation: success is RIGHT ORIENTATION, not capability. These five roots are its exact mirror:

| Root | Failure posture | Orientation status | Key formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dll | Toward wrong destination | Active misdirection | yuḍlil / ḍāllūn (man yahdī, man yuḍlil) |
| ErD | Shoulder turned from message | Active deflection | aʿraḍa ʿan dhikrī |
| gfl | Attention absent | Passive non-engagement | fī ghafla muʿriḍūn |
| nks | Head pointing down | Collapsed/inverted | nākisū ruʾūsihim |
| jhl | No orienting knowledge | Structural ignorance | ḥukm al-jāhiliyya |

The five are not synonyms. They locate failure at different stages of the attentional process:

1. **jhl** — the failure BEFORE the message arrives (no orientation because no knowledge-infrastructure)
2. **gfl** — the failure AT RECEPTION (message arrives, attention is absent)
3. **ErD** — the failure AT ENGAGEMENT (message seen, body turns away)
4. **Dll** — the failure OF DIRECTION (engaging something, but pointed wrong)
5. **nks** — the failure AT ENCOUNTER WITH REALITY (head collapses when reality is finally unavoidable)

The sequence is roughly temporal: jāhiliyya creates conditions for ghafla; ghafla enables iʿrāḍ; iʿrāḍ produces ḍalāl; ḍalāl ends in the nks posture at judgment. From civilizational ignorance to bodily shame — one arc.

The architecturally decisive verse is 7:179: three perceptual instruments (heart, eyes, ears) declared non-functional → "more astray than cattle" (Dll) → "those are al-ghāfilūn" (gfl). In one verse, Dll and gfl fuse through sealed perception.

**Cross-root finding:** dhikr (*kr — remembrance) is the pivot shared by ErD and gfl. The five failure-postures are failures of orientation toward one specific signal: the divine reminder. The Quran's failure-vocabulary is built around what happens when that signal is blocked, refused, or ignored.

---

## 9. Negative Space

Every form of gfl and jhl is negative without exception; jāhiliyya has zero positive occurrences. Unlike ṣabr (patience in affliction), where the difficult state is redeemed by orientation toward God, the five orientation-failure postures have no redemptive valence anywhere in the corpus.

## 10. Verses for Direct Study

1. **21:1** — The compound failure: fī ghafla muʿriḍūn. Heedlessness + turning-away while judgment draws near. The dual-root verse.
2. **7:179** — Sealed perception, compared to cattle, declared more astray, named heedless. Fuses Dll + gfl through perception-failure.
3. **18:17** — The divine binary stated maximally: guidance → muhtadī; misguidance → no guiding ally. The symmetric architecture.
4. **5:50** — ḥukm al-jāhiliyya vs. God's judgment for people of certainty. The civilizational framing of jhl.
5. **32:12** — Criminals bowing heads (nākisū ruʾūsihim) before God, begging to return. The terminus of orientation-failure: shame-posture when reality finally cannot be deflected.

## 11. Rabbit Holes

- **The ḍalāl → tawba corridor.** Can someone who is ḍāll repent and return? Check the qwb (tawba/repentance) root against Dll co-occurrences. The Quran says God lets astray AND God guides — is there a verse where the same person moves from ḍalāl to hudā? Or is the binary always terminal?

- **Jahūl at 33:72.** The human being described as ẓalūman jahūlān (oppressive + deeply ignorant) in the context of the Trust (amāna) offered to the heavens and earth. Why does the human who accepted the Trust get described with jhl's harshest form? Is ignorance the condition that made acceptance of the Trust possible (because a wiser being would have refused)?

- **ErD's width/presentation cluster.** The same root encodes both "turning away from signs" and "the width of Paradise." Does the Quran ever exploit this double meaning — e.g., turn away from the very thing whose breadth (ʿarḍ) is the heavens and earth?

- **nks and aging (36:68).** The old-age regression image (nunakkishu fī al-khalq) followed by a-fa-lā yaʿqilūn (will they not reason?) is a call to use decline as evidence. Map this against the death-and-signs cluster in the Meccan corpus — is bodily regression a recurring sign-type?

- **The dhikr pivot.** Both ErD and gfl are structured as failures in relation to dhikr. The root *kr has its own profile (high frequency, covers both "mention" and "reminder"). A dedicated *kr analysis would reveal whether the entire failure-cluster is architecturally downstream of a single concept — the divine reminder that humans systematically miss.
