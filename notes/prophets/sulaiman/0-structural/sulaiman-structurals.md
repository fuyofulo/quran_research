## Headline

**Solomon is named 17 times in 16 ayahs across 7 surahs. His story runs through 5 passages, 50 verses in all, and 30 of them are in Sūrat al-Naml.** The text never calls him "a prophet" or "a messenger" in a sentence of his own. His prophethood rests on two lists: *We revealed to … Solomon* (4:163), and the 18 names of 6:83-86, closed by *those are the ones We gave the Book, judgment and prophethood* (6:89). **Thanks is the densest theme.** 7 of the Quran's 75 *shukr* tokens fall in his 50 verses, and so do 2 of the only 3 first-person *shukr* in the Quran (27:19, 27:40). Two surahs take their names from his story: an-Naml (the ant, 27:18) and Sabaʾ (the queen's land, 27:22, 34:15).

## 3. The words that carry it (data-grounded)

- **Knowledge** *ʿilm*: 16 tokens in 50 verses. Also judgment *ḥukm* (3), book and letter (3), the unseen (2), and speech *manṭiq* (1, 27:16).
- **Thanks** *shukr*: **7 of 75 in the Quran (9.3%)**, in about 0.8% of its verses. They are at 27:19, 27:40 (×3), 34:13 (×2), and 21:80 (David's armour: *will you then be grateful?*). Two of the Quran's three first-person *shukr* are Solomon's: *enable me to be grateful* (27:19) and *to test me whether I will be grateful* (27:40). The third is the man at forty (46:15).
- **Test and trial**: *li-yabluwanī* (27:40), *fatannā Sulaymān* (38:34).
- **Turning back**:
  - *awwāb* (38:30), *anāba* (38:34), *forgive me* (38:35), *I have wronged myself* (27:44, the queen).
  - *awwāb* occurs **5× in the Quran, and 4 are in Ṣād**: David (38:17), the mountains and birds with David (38:19), Solomon (38:30), Job (38:44). The fifth is the people of Paradise (50:32). *(CORRECTED by the judgment agent: that is the singular only. The lemma adds the plural* awwābīn *(17:25), and the verb* awwibī*, "repeat praise with him", is said to the mountains with David (34:10).)*

## 4. Formulas shared with David and Job (data-grounded)

- ***niʿma l-ʿabdu innahu awwāb*** ("what an excellent servant, he was always turning back") occurs **only twice: Solomon (38:30) and Job (38:44)**.
- ***wa inna lahu ʿindanā la-zulfā wa ḥusna maʾāb*** ("he has nearness to Us and a good return") occurs **only twice: David (38:25) and Solomon (38:40)**. In both, it comes right after the man's trial and his turning back. This is the story's only entry in `ayah-echoes.json` near-duplicates (similarity 0.5).

## 6. Prophethood status (data-grounded, from the series census)

Of the 25 people traditionally counted as prophets:
- **18 are given the title in the text** (*nabī*, *rasūl*, or *among the messengers*).
- **5 get it only through the lists of 6:83-89 and 4:163**: Solomon, David, Job, Elisha and Zechariah.
- **2 are never titled**: Adam and Dhū l-Kifl.

For David, 17:55 is near: *We favoured some of the prophets over others, and gave David the Zabūr.*

## 7. What Phase 1 should test

1. **What was Solomon given, and what did he say about it?** The pattern is gift → acknowledgement. Track every gift and the line that follows it (27:16, 27:19, 27:40, 38:35, 38:39).
2. **The horses (38:31-33).** The pronouns (*tawārat*, *ruddūhā*), *ḥubb al-khayr ʿan dhikri rabbī*, and *masḥan bi-l-sūqi wa-l-aʿnāq*: striking or stroking? Decide by grammar and context, and show both readings.
3. **The trial and the body (38:34).** What does the text say, and what does it not? Use the four *jasad* uses (7:148, 20:88, 21:8, 38:34) to bound the meaning.
4. **The queen.** How does the Quran portray her (council, judgment of kings, the gift, the disguised throne, *I submit with Solomon*)? What does it leave out (her name, marriage)?
5. **The non-human world.** The ant, the hoopoe (who argues theology, 27:24-26), the jinn, the *ʿifrīt*. Who knows what, and who does not (34:14: the jinn did not know the unseen)?
6. **Father and son.** *Solomon inherited David* (27:16): inherited what? The field case: Solomon *made to understand*, *and to each We gave judgment and knowledge* (21:79). The shared formulas of Ṣād.
7. **The defence in 2:102.** Why the Quran stops to clear his name, and the grammar of *wa mā unzila* (relative vs negative *mā*).

**Divergence top**: 38:40, 38:31, 27:37, 27:43, 27:32, 27:29, 27:17, 38:32.
