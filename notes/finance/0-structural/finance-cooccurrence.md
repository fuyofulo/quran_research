# Finance Root Co-occurrence Matrix

## Headline Finding

**Key insight:** The 37 finance roots coalesce into three semantic clusters. *ktb* (writing/contracts) dominates, appearing with 23 other finance roots—establishing it as the constitutional hub. *Sdq* (charity/alms) and *$hd* (testimony/witnessing) cluster tightly together (20 co-occurrences with ktb, 8 with each other), reflecting legal/ethical witness requirements. Notably absent: zero co-occurrences between *rbw* (usury) and *zkw* (purification/zakat), despite theological opposition. Most surprising: *kyl* (measuring) and *wzn* (weighing) co-occur only 6 times despite symmetrical metaphoric usage in "giving full measure." This suggests distinct semantic domains: measurement for commerce vs. justice.
