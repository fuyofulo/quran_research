# The Last Five Years — sourced record (2021–2026)

Raw material for the intro. The thesis it supports: **the suffering of the last 5 years is universal, not Muslim-specific.** War for the poor, a plague for everyone, an emptiness for the rich. This is what confirms the model (the keeper left the post, so the *whole world* lost its peace) and what keeps the book off tribal-grievance ground.

Every figure below is attributed. Contested tolls are named to their source and flagged. Companion artifact: "The Last Five Years — A Factual Record."

## PART I — THE WORLD

### A plague for everyone
- **14.9–18.2 million** excess deaths worldwide from COVID-19, Jan 2020–Dec 2021 (~3× reported). [WHO](https://www.who.int/data/stories/the-true-death-toll-of-covid-19-estimating-global-excess-mortality) · [Lancet](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36517599/)
- Three highest national excess-death tolls: **India 4.07M, USA 1.13M, Russia 1.07M** — a Hindu-majority nation, a Christian-majority nation, and another. [CIDRAP/WHO](https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/global-covid-19-deaths-may-be-3-times-higher-recorded)
- *Point: the biggest killing event of the era was blind to religion, and worst in a Hindu-majority country.*

### War, at a peak not seen since 1945
- **61** active state-based conflicts in 2024 — most since 1946; 11 rose to full war. [UCDP/Uppsala](https://www.uu.se/en/press/press-releases/2025/2025-06-11-ucdp-sharp-increase-in-conflicts-and-wars)
- **~160,000** killed in organized violence in 2024, deadliest since WWII; Ukraine ~76,000. [UCDP](https://www.uu.se/en/press/press-releases/2025/2025-06-11-ucdp-sharp-increase-in-conflicts-and-wars)
- **122 million** forcibly displaced by mid-2025 — 1 in every 67 humans. [UNHCR/AJ](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/6/12/one-in-67-people-worldwide-remains-forcibly-displaced-unhcr-report)
- Sudan: **150,000+ dead, 14.6M displaced** (world's worst crisis). [IRC](https://www.rescue.org/article/top-10-crises-world-cant-ignore-2026)
- Gaza: **70,373 killed incl. 20,179 children** (Gaza MoH, Dec 2025); independent surveys **80,000–100,000+**. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-025-02009-8) · [Costs of War](https://costsofwar.watson.brown.edu/paper/HumanTollGaza)
- Myanmar: **~90,000** killed since the 2021 coup (Buddhist-majority state). [IRC](https://www.rescue.org/article/top-10-crises-world-cant-ignore-2026)
- **2025 = worst humanitarian year on record.** [Concern](https://www.concern.net/news/worlds-worst-humanitarian-crises)
- *Point: the wars span every people — Christian, Buddhist-ruled, Muslim alike.*

### The hungry
- **295 million** in acute hunger across 53 countries in 2024 — 6th straight yearly rise. [WFP/GRFC](https://www.wfp.org/news/acute-food-insecurity-and-malnutrition-rose-sixth-consecutive-year-worlds-most)
- **673 million** faced hunger (8.3% of humanity); **2.3 billion** food-insecure. [UN SOFI 2025](https://data.unicef.org/resources/sofi-2025/)

### The mind — even where the bombs never fell
- **1 billion+** living with a mental disorder; 91% of depression untreated. [WHO Sept 2025](https://www.who.int/news/item/02-09-2025-over-a-billion-people-living-with-mental-health-conditions-services-require-urgent-scale-up)
- **+25%** rise in anxiety, depression, loneliness since 2021. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news/item/02-09-2025-over-a-billion-people-living-with-mental-health-conditions-services-require-urgent-scale-up)
- **~871,000** loneliness-linked deaths/year (~100/hour); **720,000+** suicides/year. [WHO](https://www.who.int/news/item/02-09-2025-over-a-billion-people-living-with-mental-health-conditions-services-require-urgent-scale-up)
- **167 countries** where youth well-being is now falling. [NBER](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w33415/w33415.pdf)
- *Point: the wealthy "winning" societies are hit hardest here — material success bought nothing.*

### A burning planet
- **2024 hottest year on record**, first past 1.5°C (1.55°C). [Copernicus](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-2024-first-year-exceed-15degc-above-pre-industrial-level)
- Turkey–Syria earthquake (Feb 2023): **~60,000 killed**. [revised toll](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/nearly-60000-killed-in-2023-turkey-syria-quake-new-toll-1.1706864966200)

## PART II — INDIA (not outside the storm — at its center)

### The pandemic's heaviest toll on earth
- **4.7 million** estimated excess deaths 2020–21 — highest of any nation, ~10× the official 481,080. India is the **only** WHO member to reject the estimate. [WHO/IndiaSpend](https://www.indiaspend.com/covid-19/india-had-most-pandemic-related-deaths-of-any-country-at-47-million-says-who-816250)
- **2.3 million** of those in **April–May 2021 alone** (Delta second wave; oxygen shortage, mass cremations).

### An epidemic of despair
- **170,924** suicides in 2022 — highest number ever recorded; highest rate in 56 years. [NCRB/CMHLP](https://cmhlp.org/imho/blog/takeaways-from-the-ncrb-data-on-suicide-for-2022/)
- **13,000+** students (most in a decade); **10,000+** children under 18. [NCRB](https://cmhlp.org/imho/blog/takeaways-from-the-ncrb-data-on-suicide-for-2022/)
- **11,290** farmers/farm labourers — ~1 every hour. [NCRB/DtE](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/one-farmer-farm-labourer-dies-by-suicide-every-hour-in-india-ncrb-data-93184)
- **52,429** daily-wage workers (up from 42,004 in 2021). [NCRB/DtE](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/one-farmer-farm-labourer-dies-by-suicide-every-hour-in-india-ncrb-data-93184)

### The air itself
- **1.7 million** deaths attributable to PM2.5 air pollution in 2022 (up 38% since 2010); ~9.5% of GDP lost. [Lancet/DtE](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/health/pm-25-not-only-killed-17-million-people-in-india-during-2022-but-also-caused-financial-losses-to-the-tune-of-95-of-countrys-gdp-lancet)

### Work and wealth
- Youth unemployment **~16%** (2024); **80%+** of the jobless are under 30. [ILO 2024](https://www.ilo.org/publications/india-employment-report-2024-youth-employment-education-and-skills)
- Top 1% hold **40%** of wealth; bottom 50% hold 6.4% — among the most unequal on earth. [World Inequality Lab](https://sabrangindia.in/in-india-wealth-inequality-among-highest-in-the-world-top-1-holds-40-wealth-study/)

### The fracture (documented events, attributed — a record, not a verdict)
- **Manipur:** 258 killed since May 2023, ~60,000 displaced, 4,786 homes burnt, 386 temples/churches vandalised (govt figures). [reporting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Manipur_violence)
- **CAA (March 2024):** fast-tracks citizenship for non-Muslim refugees from 3 neighbours, excludes Muslims; HRW calls it discriminatory. [HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/03/15/india-activates-discriminatory-citizenship-law) · [AJ](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/3/12/why-is-indias-citizenship-amendment-act-so-controversial)
- **Waqf Amendment Act (2025):** rewrote Muslim endowment governance; nationwide protests + Supreme Court challenge. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwyn87ly1pqo)
- **Demolitions:** Amnesty documented 128 demolitions (Apr–Jun 2022), 617 made homeless, largely Muslim-owned. [Amnesty](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2024/02/india-authorities-must-immediately-stop-unjust-targeted-demolition-of-muslim-properties/)
- **"Electoral autocracy"** since 2019, further declines flagged 2024. [V-Dem](https://m.thewire.in/article/government/democracy-declines-globally-india-among-largest-electoral-autocracies-v-dem-report)
